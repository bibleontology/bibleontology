# Cross-referenced phrases, 101-200 -- the stretch list
#
# Continuation of `crossrefs_100_checklist.txt`, supplied as a second batch
# with the treatment left open on purpose. Two things about it are worth
# recording before any of it is worked, because both bear on what the right
# treatment is.
#
# **It repeats itself, and the repetition is the finding.** "The LORD is my
# helper" is 120, 135 and 185. My refuge is 119 and 188; my rock 115 and 192;
# my portion 129 and 186; my fortress 114 and 189; my deliverer 116 and 190;
# my shield 117 and 191; my light 133 and 194; my salvation 134 and 181; my
# keeper 136 and 199; my confidence 139 and 195; my hope 140 and 196; my
# strength 137, 187 and 197. Section 181-200 is very nearly a restatement of
# 101-140.
#
# That is not a defect in the list. It is what the Psalms do -- 2 Samuel 22:2-3
# piles up rock, fortress, deliverer, shield, horn of salvation, high tower and
# refuge inside two verses -- and a reader compiling from memory reproduces the
# pile. **The corpus prefers accumulation to precision here**, and any
# treatment has to decide whether that is one fact or twelve.
#
# **Most of these are epithets, not quotations.** "The LORD is my rock" is not
# a sentence one book borrows from another; it is a form several books reach
# for independently. The first hundred holds real quotation chains -- Isaiah
# 7:14 into Matthew 1:23, Habakkuk 2:4 into Romans and Hebrews. This hundred
# holds far fewer, and treating the two alike would manufacture a borrowing
# that nobody claims.
#
# Likely shape, undecided: the epithets gather on whatever record already holds
# the figure -- `image_rock`, `image_refuge`, `image_shield` -- with their
# verses, rather than each becoming a saying of its own. The handful that are
# genuine chains (171 Daniel 7:13 into Matthew 24:30; 172 Isaiah 45:23 into
# Romans 14:11 and Philippians 2:10; 180 Jeremiah 31 into Hebrews 8) are the
# same kind of thing as the first hundred and belong with those.
#
# Like the other checklists, this is an instrument for confirming the treatment
# is right. It is not a backlog of links to add.
#
# Format: n | phrase | references | status

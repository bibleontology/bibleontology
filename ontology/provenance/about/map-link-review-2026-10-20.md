# About: the link to the schema map, reviewed (2026-10-20)

The sentence About shows under its five tiles once /ns/map is published (plan 8.1e), read by the eleven-reader
panel (`ontology/provenance/README.md`) in one Claude Haiku pass.

**As drafted:** "The whole schema, as a map › Every published term in pictures, from four layers down to each kind,
and the open standards it is built on."

- **The seats:** all eleven accepted it. It names no doctrine, no people and no political frame.
- **Clarity:** the panel judged it a barrier for a reader who knows no RDF. "Schema" is database jargon, "term"
  reads as a glossary word, and "four layers" is unexplained.
- **Verdict:** ships with a plainer wording.
- **The completeness note:** the panel checked the word "every". The map draws the faded, not-yet-built kinds too,
  so the sentence says "defines" (true of all of them) rather than "records".

**As shipped:** "The whole design, as a map › Every kind of thing, part of the text and link the graph defines,
drawn as pictures from the four layers down, and the free, open standards it is built on."

# About's pictures and the welcome entry: the eleven-reader review (2026-10-20)

**What was reviewed:** the five-tile signpost, the John 3:16 picture, the four standings and the welcome pop-up's new
entry. **Reviewer:** Claude Sonnet, blind second reader, reading as the eleven seats in `ontology/provenance/README.md`
("The review panel"). Two passes.

## Pass 1: REVISE

Factual:
- Job 15:6 was quoted in the KJV's word order ("Your own mouth condemns you, not I"). The Berean reads "Your own
  mouth, not mine, condemns you". This was fixed in About and in the mock.
- "He gave His Son" was not the Berean's wording. It is now quoted in full: "He gave His one and only Son".
- "The Greek form says it happened" was too strong for an aorist. It now reads "reports it as a completed act" (in
  the picture: "reports it as done").
- "740 statements read from single clauses" was ambiguous. It now reads "740 statements, stated outright". The
  reviewer's suggestion of "815 shown" was wrong: described statements are not drawn on the cards
  (`app2/server.py`), as pass 2 confirmed.

Seats:
- **Lutheran and Reformed.** "only the narrator's are taken as what the text states" could read as ranking God's
  speech below the narrator's. It now reads: "A speaker's words are shown as that speaker's; what the narrator says
  in the text's own voice is shown as what the text states."
- **Reformed, Baptist and academic.** "John 3:16, the narrator" takes a side on whether 3:16 is Jesus' words or the
  evangelist's. It now reads "in the Gospel's own voice (no speaker is credited)".
- **Reformed, Pentecostal and rabbi.** "never stated as a fact", said of a divine promise, could read as doubting
  it. It now reads "a promise, not a past event". The legend reads "described: a promise, a command or a prediction,
  not a past event".
- **The literary reading and the rabbi.** "a promise, or a prediction?" on Gen 3:15 could nudge between readings. It
  now reads "the two readers differ on how to read it. Held until it can be read".

## Pass 2: PASS

One wording change: "Kept as God's promise" became "Recorded as God's promise", because the cards do not draw
described statements. Every seat accepted the changed lines and the welcome entry.

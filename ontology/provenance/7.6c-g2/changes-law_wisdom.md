# Changes to theme 6 (law_wisdom, now The Law)

Copilot's concepts:

- 1 "Law as Covenant Stipulation" → renamed "The covenant at Sinai" (the card's name; "stipulation" is a framing no verse uses). Exodus 20–23 and Deuteronomy 5 cut; the commandments moved to their own concept, "The Ten Commandments".
- 2 "Law as Revelation of God's Character" → renamed "Be holy, because I am holy" (the card; the texts' own link between the law and who God is). Exodus 34:6–7 dropped: it is not about the law. Psalm 19:7–9 and Romans 7:12 moved to "The Law of the LORD is perfect".
- 3 "Law as Path of Life" → renamed "Choose life" (Deuteronomy 30:19), with "the man who does these things will live by them" (Leviticus 18:5 and the places that quote it). Psalm 1 and 119 moved to "My delight is in Your law"; Proverbs 3:1–2 left to Wisdom Literature.
- 4 "Law as Instruction (Torah = Teaching)" → renamed "Teach them diligently" (Deuteronomy 6:7); the gloss on the Hebrew word dropped. Malachi 2:7 kept; whole-chapter Psalm 119 cut to verses in other concepts; Proverbs 6:23 left to Wisdom Literature.
- 5 "Wisdom as Skill in God-Ordered Living" → dropped: wisdom's sub-themes belong to Wisdom Literature (packet note).
- 6 "Fear of the LORD as Foundation of Wisdom" → renamed "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom" (the card), kept as the one bridge to wisdom, with only passages that join the fear of the LORD or wisdom to the law (Deuteronomy 4:6, 10:12–13, Psalm 19:7, 111:10, 119:98–100, Ecclesiastes 12:13).
- 7 "Law and Wisdom Overlapping" → dropped as a concept; its law passages (Deuteronomy 4:6, Psalm 19:7, Psalm 119:98–100) merged into concept 23, the bridge. Proverbs 6:20–23 left to Wisdom Literature.
- 8 "Law as Gift, Not Burden" → renamed and split into "The Law of the LORD is perfect" (Psalm 19:7) and "My delight is in Your law" (both table rows); "not a burden" now only where 1 John 5:3 says it.
- 9 "Law Exposes Sin" → renamed "The law brings awareness of sin" (Romans 3:20's words).
- 10 "Christ as Fulfillment of the Law" → renamed "Not to abolish, but to fulfill" (Matthew 5:17); the name no longer asserts in the site's voice. Romans 10:4 and Galatians 3:24 kept as quotations; Hebrews 10:1–10 dropped (a long range about sacrifice, not the law's commandments).
- 11 "Spirit as Empowerment to Obey" → renamed "Walk by the Spirit" (the card). Jeremiah 31:33 and Ezekiel 36:26–27 moved to the closing concept "I will give you a new heart".

Concepts added:

- "The law given through Moses" (table row; Exodus 24:12, Deuteronomy 4:44, John 1:17, Acts 7:53, Galatians 3:19).
- "The Ten Commandments" (Exodus 20:1–17, Deuteronomy 5:6–21 and where they are named).
- "You shall love the LORD your God" (the card; Deuteronomy 6:4–5 and the Gospels' greatest commandment).
- "Love your neighbor as yourself" (the card; Leviticus 19:18 and five New Testament quotations).
- "All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments" (the card; Matthew 22:40, 7:12, Romans 13:8–10).
- "Keep the Sabbath day holy" (table row; Exodus 20, Deuteronomy 5, Exodus 31, Isaiah 58, Mark 2).
- "Pursue justice, and justice alone" (Deuteronomy 16:20; the card "justice").
- "The foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow" (Deuteronomy 24:19; the card "strangers").
- "A blessing and a curse" (Deuteronomy 11:26; the table's "Cursed is he who does not do the words of the law" and "Hung on a tree"), kept as its own hard word.
- "Be doers of the word" (James 1:22; table row "Not hearers only, but doers").
- "You have heard that it was said" (the Sermon on the Mount's six sayings, Matthew 5:21–44).
- "Justified before God" (table row; Romans 3, Galatians 2–3 and James 2 side by side, each in its own words).
- "Not under law, but under grace" (Romans 6:14, 7:6, 1 Corinthians 9:20–21, Galatians 3:23–25, 4:4–5).
- "I will give you a new heart" (the card), the closing words of hope: Deuteronomy 30:6, 11–14, Psalm 37:31, Jeremiah 31:33–34, Ezekiel 11:19–20, 36:26–27, Hebrews 8:10, 2 Corinthians 3:3.

Secondary concepts: all eleven of Copilot's replaced (whole-chapter ranges; six were wisdom's). New ten are table rows or law topics cut to verses: offerings, clean and unclean, eye for eye, two or three witnesses, purge the evil, vows, certificate of divorce, each for his own sin, do not muzzle an ox, vengeance is Mine.

Linkages: Image of God, Kingdom, Priesthood dropped (no passage here truly overlaps); "Christology" → "Messiah/Christology"; "Sanctification" and "New Covenant" are not allowed labels (dropped; Jeremiah 31 is reached through Mercy & Restoration and Covenant). Added Sin/Exile/Death, Judgment, Freedom, Wisdom Literature, Mercy & Restoration.

## After the review

Concept numbers below are the pre-review ones. The new outline has 29 concepts.

Concept points:
- 2 (covenant at Sinai), drop Deuteronomy 29:9: applied. Deuteronomy 29:1 says that covenant was made "in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant He had made with them at Horeb".
- 5 (love your neighbor), drop Leviticus 19:34: applied. It says "love him as yourself" of the foreigner and stays in concept 10.
- 6, rename to "The essence of the Law and the Prophets": applied. Matthew 7:12 and Romans 13:8–10 do not speak of two commandments. The card name ("All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments") is still Matthew 22:40's note.
- 8, rename to "Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy" and add Romans 14:5, Colossians 2:16–17 and Hebrews 4:9: applied, with two quotation corrections from bsb.txt. Romans 14:5 reads "One person regards a certain day above the others, while someone else considers every day alike", not the reviewer's wording. Hebrews 4:9 reads "There remains, then, a Sabbath rest". Exodus 31:13 and 31:16–17 were joined as 31:13–17 to stay within 8 passages.
- 15, rename to "Your law is my delight" (Psalm 119:77), drop Joshua 1:8, add Psalm 37:31: applied. Joshua 1:8 moved to "Be doers of the word" with the note "so that you may be careful to do everything written in it".
- 16 (be doers), drop James 2:10: applied. Moved to "A blessing and a curse".
- 17/18 order: applied. "Christ and the law" now comes before "You have heard that it was said", in Matthew's order.
- 18, rename to "Christ and the law": applied. Every passage kept, Matthew 5:17 keeps its exact note, and Romans 10:4 keeps "Christ is the end of the law" with no gloss.
- 19, rename to "The law and sin": applied.
- 20 (justified), give James 2:21–24 whole and add Romans 4:2–3: applied. One passage line carries one note, so James is given as 2:21–23 and 2:24, which together cover 2:21–24 whole with both notes. 2:23 ("Abraham believed God") is now inside. Romans 4:2–3 was added with the same quotation.
- 21, rename to "Under the law": applied.
- 23, rename to "Wisdom and the commandments": applied. Psalm 111:10 keeps the card's words ("The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom") as its note. The concept was moved up after "Your law is my delight", next to the other Psalms.
- 24 (new heart), drop Psalm 37:31 and Deuteronomy 30:11–14: applied. They moved to "Your law is my delight" and "Choose life". Deuteronomy 30:11–14's note was changed to "this commandment I give you today is not too difficult for you or beyond your reach", so it does not repeat Romans 10:8. It stays the last concept, now six passages, all promises.
- 1, 3, 4, 7, 9–14, 17, 22 accepted: unchanged.

Secondary and link points:
- Romans 10:6–8 in "Choose life": applied, as Romans 10:5–8 with the note "The word is near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart". The Leviticus 18:5 wording is still shown by Leviticus 18:5 and Ezekiel 20:11. The optional Galatians 3:12 was not applied because the concept is at the 8-passage limit. The optional Nehemiah 9:29 was applied, in the new "All Israel has transgressed Your law".
- James 2:10 into "A blessing and a curse": applied.
- Optional secondary "Honor your father and mother": not applied. Section C is at its 10-item limit, and Exodus 20:12 and Deuteronomy 5:16 already stand under "The Ten Commandments".
- Optional secondary "Mercy, not sacrifice": applied as part of the new core concept "Justice, mercy, and faithfulness" (Hosea 6:6; Matthew 9:13; 12:7) rather than as a secondary.
- Restore the Priesthood link: applied, since Exodus 19:6, Malachi 2:7 and Hebrews 7:12 ("when the priesthood is changed") are in the outline. To stay at 10 links, Creation was dropped: its only overlap was Exodus 20:11 and 31:17.
- Justice, Faith or Israel links: not applied, because no such labels are allowed.

Missing sub-themes (all five added, each developed in more than one place):
- "All Israel has transgressed Your law" (Daniel 9:11's exact words): added as concept 28, just before the closing hope. Passages: 2 Kings 17:13–15, Nehemiah 9:26, 9:29, Jeremiah 6:19, Daniel 9:11–13.
- "The Gentiles and the law of Moses" (plain label; Acts 15:5 has "the law of Moses"): added as concept 26. Passages: Acts 15:1, 15:5, 15:10–11, 15:19–21, Romans 2:12–16, Galatians 5:2–3. The reviewer quoted Acts 15:10 as "neither our fathers nor we", but bsb.txt reads "a yoke that neither we nor our fathers have been able to bear", which is the wording used.
- "A change of law": added as concept 21, under the different name "Changed, set aside, abolished". The reviewer's name is not BSB wording: Hebrews 7:12 reads "the law must be changed as well". Hebrews 8:13 ("obsolete") is about the first covenant and Colossians 2:14 ("canceled") about "the decrees", so one verse's phrase would say more or less than the others. The label takes each passage's own verb. Passages: Hebrews 7:12, 7:18–19, 8:13, Ephesians 2:14–15, Colossians 2:14.
- "Justice, mercy, and faithfulness" (Matthew 23:23's exact words): added as concept 11, after the justice and foreigner concepts. Passages: Matthew 23:23, Luke 11:42, Hosea 6:6, Matthew 9:13, 12:7.
- "Is the law opposed to the promises of God?": added as concept 23 under the plain label "The law and the promise". The question itself is Galatians 3:21's note, with its "Certainly not!". Passages: Galatians 3:15–18, 3:21, Romans 4:13–16.

Every reference was re-checked against bsb.txt, and every note is an exact substring of its verses.

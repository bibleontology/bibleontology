- 4 "Messianic Kingship (Davidic Promise)" → renamed **The throne of David established forever** (the card "the throne of David" and the table's row; "Messianic" is a reading Jewish and Christian readers part on). Psalm 2:6 moved to the added "Reigning on Mount Zion"; Isaiah 11 to "Righteousness and justice ..."; Ezekiel 34:23–24 replaced by Ezekiel 37:24–25, which says "king"; Luke 1:32–33 added (the New Testament's own words, as its own passage).

Matthew 6:10 moved to the added "Your kingdom come"; Daniel 4:3, 4:34, Luke 1:33, Hebrews 12:28, 2 Peter 1:11 added.
- 6 "Kingdom as Present and Future" → dropped as a concept ("present and future" takes a position readers part on); its passages kept under names the verses support: Matthew 12:28, Luke 11:20, Luke 17:20–21 under the added "The kingdom of God has come upon you"; Matthew 25:34 under the added "Inherit the kingdom"; 2 Peter 1:11 under "An everlasting kingdom"; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28 cut to 15:25 ("Sit at My right hand") and 15:28 and Revelation 11:15 under the last concept.

Luke 4:43 moved to the added "The gospel of the kingdom"; Matthew 5–7 cut to Matthew 5:3, 5:10, 5:20, 6:10, 6:33, 7:21 across "Theirs is the kingdom of heaven", "Enter the kingdom", "Your kingdom come"; Matthew 13 cut to its "The kingdom of heaven is like" verses (added concept); John 18:36 to the added "The kingdom is not ...".

Romans 14:17 and 1 Corinthians 4:20 kept under the added "The kingdom is not ..."; Acts 1:3–8 cut to Acts 1:3 under "The gospel of the kingdom"; Matthew 12:28 ("by the Spirit of God") is under "The kingdom of God has come upon you".
- 9 "Kingdom Ethics (Righteousness, Justice, Peace)" → renamed **Righteousness and justice the foundation of the throne** (the table's row; Psalm 89:14). Psalm 72 cut to 72:1–2; Isaiah 32:1–8 cut to 32:1; Matthew 5–7 cut as above; Galatians 5:22–23 cut (the fruit of the Spirit is not said of the kingdom; Galatians 5:21 is kept under "Inherit the kingdom"); Romans 14:17 moved to "The kingdom is not ..."; Psalm 97:2, Proverbs 16:12, 29:14, Isaiah 9:7, 11:4–5 added.
- 10 "Kingdom Consummation in New Creation" → renamed **The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord** (Revelation 11:15, the front page's last quotation), placed last as the theme's words of hope. ## Concepts added

- **The great King over all the earth**: the table's rows "The great King over all the earth", "King of the nations", "King of kings and Lord of lords" (Psalm 47, Malachi, Jeremiah 10, 1 Timothy, Revelation 19:16).
- **His throne in heaven**: Psalm 103:19, 11:4, Isaiah 6:1, 66:1, Lamentations 5:19, Revelation 4:2; the table's "Enthroned between the cherubim".

- **A kingdom of priests**: Exodus 19:6, 1 Peter 2:9, Revelation 1:6, 5:10.
- **The kingdom torn away and given to another**: the table's row; the hard word kept as its own sub-theme (Saul, Solomon, Nebuchadnezzar, Belshazzar, Matthew 21:43).
- **Reigning on Mount Zion**: the table's row; Psalm 2:6, 99:2, 110:2, Isaiah 24:23, Micah 4:7, Obadiah 21, Zechariah 9:9, Matthew 21:5.

- **Theirs is the kingdom of heaven**: the Beatitudes' kingdom sayings (card: the Beatitudes), the little children, James 2:5.

- **A banquet, a feast, a supper**: the table's row; Isaiah 25:6 through Revelation 19:9.

## Sections C and D

- C: Copilot's nine were labels ("Kingdom → Ethics", "→ Mission", "→ Church") over whole chapters; replaced by ten in the texts' words, cut to verses. Matthew 8:12 ("the sons of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness") is kept there, not left out.
- D: Creation, Blessing & Commission and Church dropped (no core passage is about them); Christology given as the list's label Messiah/Christology; Temple/Presence (the throne in the temple, Isaiah 6:1, Psalm 11:4), Judgment (the kingdom torn away; Psalm 96:10) and Apocalyptic (Daniel 2, 7; Revelation 11) added.

## After the review

Concept numbers below are the first draft's; the revised outline renumbers them (four sub-themes added, 29 core concepts).

### Concepts
- 3 "His throne in heaven" → **The LORD on His throne**: applied. Psalm 93:2, 99:1, Isaiah 6:1 and Lamentations 5:19 do not say heaven. The four that do still say it in their notes.
- 5 "He rules over all the kingdoms and gives them to whom He wishes" → **The Most High rules over the kingdom of mankind**: applied. "Gives it to whom He wishes" is only Daniel 4:17 (and 4:25, 4:32); Daniel 5:21 says "setting over it whom He wishes". The Daniel wording covers the rest.
- 6 "These four great beasts are four kings" → **Kingdoms that will arise from the earth**: applied. Daniel 2 is a statue and Daniel 8:20–21 a ram and goat. Grouping them under "four" reads as an identification the site does not make. Every passage is kept.
- 7 "The LORD your King": applied; Deuteronomy 33:5 dropped. The Berean supplies "the LORD"; the reviewer says the Hebrew has no named subject, and bsb.txt cannot show that wrong. Under "the original arbitrates" it goes.
- 9 "He who rules in the fear of God": applied; Deuteronomy 17:15, 1 Samuel 16:1 and Psalm 72:1–2 dropped. The first two now stand in the added "The king whom the LORD your God shall choose"; Psalm 72:1–2 is in "Righteousness and justice ...". The Deuteronomy 17:18–20 note is now 17:19's “so that he may learn to fear the LORD his God”, which holds the name.
- 10 "The kingdom torn away and given to another" → **The kingdom taken away from you**: applied, as a plain label. The reviewer says every passage holds "taken away from you". That is true in sense but not in wording: only Matthew 21:43 says "taken away from you". The others say torn from, departed from, divided. The notes keep each verse's word and its "given" half. Hosea 13:11 (“in My wrath I took him away”) added. No word of replacement or fulfilment added.
- 13 "Sit at My right hand": applied; 1 Corinthians 15:25 moved to the last concept (no "sit", no "right hand").
- 16 "The gospel of the kingdom" → **Proclaiming the kingdom of God**: applied. Acts 1:3 and 28:31 do not say gospel.
- 17 "The kingdom of God has come upon you": applied; Colossians 1:13 moved to "Enter the kingdom" with its note “brought us into the kingdom of His beloved Son”.
- 19 "Your kingdom come" → **Your kingdom come; seek His kingdom**: applied. Matthew 6:33 and Luke 12:31 say seek.
- 1, 2, 4, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15, 18, 20–25: accepted by the review, no change.

### Section C, links, last concept
- "Authority → in heaven and on earth" → **Authority → given to Him**: applied. Daniel 7:14 says "He was given dominion"; "in heaven and on earth" is only Matthew 28:18.
- "Kingdom → divided against itself": applied. Daniel 5:28 dropped (it is divided and given over, not divided against itself; it stays in concept 13). Luke 11:17 added.
- Colossians 1:13 to "Enter the kingdom": applied.
- 1 Corinthians 15:25 to the last concept: applied, with its note “For He must reign until He has put all His enemies under His feet”.
- Links: no change, as the review says.
- Last concept: unchanged, still last. Matthew 25:41 is not added, as the review leaves it to the Judgment link.
- Section C also gains "No king in Israel → everyone did what was right in his own eyes (Judg 17:6; Judg 21:25)". It is the review's Judges 21:25, which does not hold the "manner of the king" name.

### Missing sub-themes (all four added: each in more than one place)
- **The King of the Jews, the King of Israel**: added. The reviewer's name "Are You the King of the Jews?" is not used, because Matthew 2:2, John 1:49, 12:13 and Luke 19:38 are not that question; the plain title covers all eight passages. Passages: Matthew 2:2, John 1:49, Luke 19:38, John 12:13, Matthew 27:11, John 18:37, 27:29, 27:37. The mockery and the charge stay in. The site's voice says nothing about who He is.
- **The king whom the LORD your God shall choose**: added. Passages: Deuteronomy 17:15, 1 Samuel 10:24, 16:1, Psalm 78:70, 1 Kings 11:34, and Hosea 8:4 (“They set up kings, but not by Me”).
- "This is what the king who will reign over you will do" → added as **The manner of the king who will reign over you**. The reviewer's wording is not the Berean's: 1 Samuel 8:11 reads “This will be the manner of the king who will reign over you”. Passages: 1 Samuel 8:9, 8:11, 8:14–17, 8:18, Deuteronomy 17:16–17, Judges 9:14–15 (Jotham's fable, cut to the thornbush). Judges 21:25 went to section C, as above.
- **The kings of the earth**: added. Passages: Psalm 2:2, 2:10, 72:11, 102:15, 138:4, Revelation 1:5, 19:19, 21:24. Both the kings who take their stand and the kings who bow and bring their glory are kept, with no order of events.

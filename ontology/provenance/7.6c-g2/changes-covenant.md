# Covenant (theme 4): changes from Copilot's two outlines

One outline replaces both Copilot's theme 4 and its theme 21 "Full Canonical Synthesis". All 220 passages and every quotation were checked by script against bsb.txt (0 missing, 0 mismatched; the check was proven by two planted faults).

## Copilot's theme 4

- **1. Divine Initiative in Covenant** — renamed → "I will establish My covenant" (the passages' own repeated phrase; "initiative" is a framing). Gen 6:18 and 9:9 kept; Gen 12:1–3 moved to "The covenants of the promise", Gen 15:18 to "The covenant with Abraham", Exod 19:4–6 to "The covenant at Sinai" and "My treasured possession", Jer 31:31–33 to "A new covenant".
- **2. Covenant as Binding Relationship** — renamed → "I will be your God, and you will be My people" (the formula the passages repeat, Jer 7:23; "binding relationship" says more than they do). Gen 17:7 and Exod 6:7 kept; Deut 29:12–13 cut to 29:13; Hos 2:19–20 moved to "I will betroth you to Me forever".
- **3. Covenant Promises** — renamed → "The covenants of the promise" (Eph 2:12's phrase). Gen 12:2–3 kept; Gen 15:5–7 and 17:4–8 moved to "The covenant with Abraham", Exod 19:5–6 to "My treasured possession", 2 Sam 7:12–16 to "The covenant with David", Jer 31:33 to "I will give you a new heart".
- **4. Covenant Obligations** — renamed → "Keep My covenant" (the texts' command, Gen 17:9). Gen 17:1, Exod 19:5 and Deut 10:12–13 kept; Exod 20–23 cut (a four-chapter range); Deut 30:15–20 moved to "Life and death, blessing and cursing".
- **5. Covenant Signs** — kept, worded as the texts word it: "A sign of the covenant". Rainbow, circumcision and Sabbath kept, cut to the verses that say "sign". Passover dropped from it: Exod 12:13 calls the blood a sign, but not of the covenant (the Passover stays, via 2 Kgs 23:21, under "The words of this covenant"). The Lord's Supper moved to "A new covenant": Luke 22:20 and 1 Cor 11:25 do not call the cup a sign.
- **6. Major Biblical Covenants** — renamed by splitting into the site's own covenant cards: "The covenant with Noah", "The covenant with Abraham", "The covenant at Sinai", "The covenant with David", and "A new covenant". Ranges cut (Gen 9:1–17, Gen 15, Exod 20–24, Deut 5 and 28–30 to the verses that carry each).
- **7. Covenant Faithfulness (God's Hesed)** — renamed → "His covenant of loving devotion" (the Berean's wording, Deut 7:9; the transliteration dropped). Exod 34:6–7 cut to 34:6; Deut 7:9 kept; Ps 89:1–4 moved to "The covenant with David"; Lam 3:22–23 dropped (no covenant in it).
- **8. Covenant Mediation** — renamed → "A mediator" (the texts' own word, Gal 3:19, 1 Tim 2:5, Heb 8:6; 9:15; 12:24). Moses kept (Exod 32:30; Deut 5:5); "Priests" (Mal 2:7–8) moved to "The covenant of peace" via Mal 2:8 "the covenant of Levi"; "David" (2 Sam 7) dropped from it (the texts do not call him a mediator).
- **9. Covenant Blessings & Curses** — renamed → "Life and death, blessing and cursing" (Deut 30:19). Lev 26 and Deut 28 cut to 26:3–4, 14–15, 25 and 28:1–2, 15; Josh 24:19–27 moved to "Made a covenant before the LORD" (24:25) and to section C (24:27, the witness stone).
- **10. Covenant Fulfillment in Christ** — dropped: the name says Old Testament covenants are fulfilled in Christ, where traditions (and Jewish readers) part. Its New Testament passages stay in their own words: Luke 22:20 under "A new covenant"; Rom 15:8 and 2 Cor 1:20 under "The covenants of the promise"; Hebrews 8–10 cut to 8:6, 8:8, 9:15, 9:18, 9:20, 10:16–17. Matt 5:17 dropped (it speaks of the Law and the Prophets, not a covenant).

## Copilot's theme 21 (the 'Full Canonical Synthesis')

- **1. Covenant as God's Chosen Mode of Relationship** — dropped: no passage says covenant is God's chosen mode of relationship. Its passages are kept elsewhere: Gen 6:18 ("I will establish My covenant"), Gen 15 and 17 ("The covenant with Abraham"), Exod 19:5–6 ("My treasured possession"), Deut 7:9 ("His covenant of loving devotion"), Ps 89:3–4 ("The covenant with David").
- **2. Covenant Structure (Parties, Terms, Blessings, Curses)** — dropped: a treaty-form analysis the texts do not name. Exod 19–24 cut and moved to "The covenant at Sinai" and "The words of this covenant"; Deut 28–30 to "Life and death, blessing and cursing"; Josh 24 and 2 Kgs 23 to "Made a covenant before the LORD".
- **3. Covenant Signs** — kept, merged with theme 4's into "A sign of the covenant"; the cup (Luke 22:20) moved to "A new covenant".
- **4. Covenant Mediators** — renamed → merged into "A mediator". Noah and Abraham dropped from it (the texts do not call them mediators); the chapter runs (Gen 6–9, Gen 12–22, Exod 19–24, 2 Sam 7, Heb 8–10) cut to the verses that say "mediator" or show Moses standing between.
- **5. Covenant Faithfulness (ḥesed)** — renamed → merged into "His covenant of loving devotion". Ps 136 dropped (no covenant in it); Hos 2:19–23 moved to "I will betroth you to Me forever".
- **6. Major Biblical Covenants** — renamed by splitting as in theme 4; its "Priestly Covenant" (Num 25:10–13) became "The covenant of peace", the site's card for Num 25:12–13, with the covenant of Levi (Mal 2:4–5, 8; Neh 13:29) and Ezekiel's covenant of peace (Ezek 34:25; 37:26).
- **7. Covenant Failure & Judgment** — renamed → "They broke My covenant" (the table's row; the texts' own verb). Exod 32–34 cut to 32:19; Judg 2:11–15 replaced by 2:20 (the verse that says "transgressed the covenant"); 2 Kgs 17:7–23 cut to 17:15; Jer 11:1–14 cut to 11:3, 11:6, 11:10 (spread across this concept and "The words of this covenant"); 2 Chr 36:15–21 dropped (no covenant in it).
- **8. Covenant Renewal** — renamed → "Made a covenant before the LORD" (the table's row; 2 Kgs 23:3). Josh 24 cut to 24:25; 2 Kgs 23:1–3 cut to 23:3; Neh 9–10 cut to 9:38 and 10:29; Deut 30:1–10 cut to 30:6, under "Circumcise your hearts".
- **9. New Covenant** — kept as "A new covenant" (Jer 31:31's words). Ezek 36:25–27 moved to "I will give you a new heart" (Ezekiel 36 does not say covenant); Ezek 37:26–28 moved to "The covenant of peace" and "I will be your God, and you will be My people".
- **10. Covenant Fulfillment in Christ** — dropped, as theme 4's. Luke 1:68–75 cut to 1:72 ("He remembers His covenant") and 1:73 ("By Myself I have sworn"); Acts 3:24–26 cut to 3:25 ("The covenants of the promise"); Rom 15:8–9 to 15:8; Matt 5:17 dropped.
- **11. Covenant Community (Church)** — dropped: Acts 2:42–47 and Eph 2:11–22 do not speak of a covenant there, and the name equates the church with a covenant people, where traditions part. 1 Cor 11:25 moved to "A new covenant", 1 Pet 2:9 to "My treasured possession", Eph 2:12 to "The covenants of the promise", Eph 2:19 to "Foreigners who hold fast to My covenant".
- **12. Covenant Consummation (New Creation)** — dropped: a claim about the end, and Isa 65:17–25 holds no covenant. Rev 21:3 moved to "I will be your God, and you will be My people"; Ezek 37:26–28 to "The covenant of peace".

## Concepts added

- **Passing between the pieces** — the table's row; Gen 15:9–10, 17 and Jer 34:18 tell of it in so many words.
- **Circumcise your hearts** — the table's row; Deut 10:16; 30:6; Jer 4:4; 9:25–26; Ezek 44:7; Rom 2:29; Col 2:11.
- **The words of this covenant** — the table's row (and the Book of the Covenant): Exod 24:7; Deut 29:9; 31:26; 2 Kgs 23:2, 21; Jer 11:3, 6.
- **The ark and the tablets of the covenant** — two table rows and the site's cards for the ark and the two stone tablets.
- **The blood of the covenant** — the front page's Exod 24:8 and the table's row, with Ps 50:5, Zech 9:11, the Last Supper in Matthew and Mark, and Heb 9:18–20.
- **My treasured possession** — the table's row (Copilot had it only as a note under Promises).
- **An everlasting covenant** — the front page's Gen 17:7 and 2 Sam 23:5 and the table's row; developed from Genesis to Hebrews.
- **By Myself I have sworn** — the site's card (Gen 22:16) and the table's "The covenant and the oath".
- **He remembers His covenant** — the table's row; Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Psalms, Jeremiah and Luke.
- **The two of them made a covenant** — covenants between people (Beersheba, Gilead, Jonathan and David, Hebron, marriage, brotherhood); the site has cards for the covenants at Beer-sheba and at Hebron.
- **You shall make no covenant with them** — the forbidden covenants, including Isaiah's "covenant with death" and Hosea's with Assyria.
- **I will betroth you to Me forever** — covenant in the words of marriage: Ezek 16:8; Hos 2:18–20; Isa 54:5; Jer 31:32.
- **Foreigners who hold fast to My covenant** — the nations and foreigners within the covenant: Gen 17:12; Deut 29:11–12; Isa 42:6; 49:8; 56:6–7; Eph 2:19.
- **I will give you a new heart** — the site's card; Jer 24:7; 31:33; 32:39; Ezek 11:19–20; 36:26–27; 2 Cor 3:3; Heb 10:16.
- **I will never break My covenant** — the closing concept of hope, from the table's row (Judg 2:1), with Lev 26:44, Deut 4:31, Ps 89:33–34, Isa 54:10, Isa 59:21, Jer 33:25–26 and Ezek 16:60. The hard words stay standing in their own concepts ("They broke My covenant", "Life and death, blessing and cursing", Ps 89:39 under David, Deut 7:2 under "You shall make no covenant with them").

## Sections C and D

- C rebuilt with references cut to verses: Land, Commandments, Sabbath, Salt (new: Lev 2:13; Num 18:19; 2 Chr 13:5), Throne, Priesthood, Dwelling, Witness (new), Forgiveness (new), Spirit. Dropped: Copilot's Relationship, Promise, Law, Kingship and Renewal (now core concepts), Mission and Church (no covenant in Isa 49:6, Acts 2 or 1 Pet 2), Sacrifice (now "The blood of the covenant"), New Creation (Jer 31 and Rev 21 hold no new creation of the covenant).
- D: kept Creation (the covenant with every living creature, Jer 33:25 "the fixed order of heaven and earth"), Blessing & Commission, Kingdom, Law/Wisdom, Sacrifice, Priesthood, Temple/Presence (added from theme 21), Sin/Exile/Death, Mercy & Restoration, Spirit. Dropped: Image of God (no overlap in these passages), Christology and Church (they framed fulfilment; and Christology is not a label on the list), "New Covenant" (not a theme), Judgment, Deliverance/Salvation and New Creation (to stay at ten links where the passages overlap most).

Counts: kept 3, renamed 13, dropped 6 (of Copilot's 22 across both outlines); added 15; 31 core concepts.

## After the review

The nine-reader review (verdict-covenant.json) asked for nine concept revisions, made five further notes, and named four missing sub-themes. Concept numbers below are the draft's; the revised outline has 34 core concepts (three added) and 225 passages. All were re-checked by script against bsb.txt: 0 missing, 0 mismatched, A and B names identical, every concept 3–8 passages.

### Concept points

- **6. Circumcise your hearts, drop Col 2:11** — applied. The verse does not say "heart", and its next verse is where traditions part on circumcision and baptism. Rom 2:29 stays.
- **8. The words of this covenant, drop Deut 31:26 (move to 9)** — applied. It is now under "The ark and the tablets of the covenant" as Deut 31:25–26 ("place it beside the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God"), in place of Deut 10:8 so the concept stays at 8. Both verses tell of the Levites carrying the ark, so nothing the concept says is lost.
- **12. The blood of the covenant, drop Ps 50:5** — applied. Section C is at its ceiling of ten, so the verse moved to "Made a covenant before the LORD", where "who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice" says what the name says.
- **21. The covenants of the promise, drop 2 Cor 1:20** — applied. It has no covenant and no patriarchs, and as the last item it read as the fulfilment claim the outline had removed.
- **22. The two of them made a covenant, trim Gen 21:27–32** — applied in part. It is now Gen 21:31–32 alone (21:31 the oath, 21:32 "After they had made the covenant at Beersheba"). The format takes one reference per line, and 21:27 tells of the same covenant, so a second line for it would repeat the event.
- **24. I will betroth you to Me forever, drop Hos 2:18** — applied. It moved to the new concept "The covenant between Me and the earth".
- **25. They broke My covenant, drop Exod 32:19** — applied. The suggested Deut 9:15–17 was not added. In the BSB, 9:15 says "the two tablets of the covenant" and 9:17 says "threw them out of my hands, shattering them", but neither verse says the covenant was broken. The reviewer's own objection to Exod 32:19 applies to them as well.
- **26. Made a covenant before the LORD, drop Neh 9:38 and 10:29** — applied. 2 Chr 34:31 was added ("made a covenant before the LORD to follow the LORD", checked in the BSB). 2 Chr 23:16 was not added: it does not say "before the LORD", and it tells the same event as 2 Kgs 11:17, which is already there.
- **28. Foreigners who hold fast to My covenant, drop Isa 49:8 and Eph 2:19** — applied. Gen 17:12, Deut 29:11–12, Isa 42:6 and Isa 56:6–7 stay.

### Other notes

- **Section C and the links** — accepted as they are; no change.
- **Concepts 13 and 14** (2 Cor 6:16, Rev 21:3, 1 Pet 2:9) — applied: kept with quotation-only notes and no gloss.
- **Concept 30** (now 33, "A new covenant") — applied. Heb 8:13 and 2 Cor 3:14 stay out, and no concept says the first covenant is replaced.
- **Josh 9:15 for concept 22** (now 24) — applied, with a correction. The BSB reads "Joshua made a treaty of peace with them", not "made a covenant", so the note quotes the BSB as written. It was added because the Hebrew word there is *berit*, the same word as at Deut 7:2, which the outline already quotes as "treaty" under "You shall make no covenant with them". This should be confirmed against MACULA. It replaces Prov 2:17 so the concept stays at 8. "Forgets the covenant of her God" does not plainly name a covenant between people, so Prov 2:17 was the weakest fit there.
- **Hos 6:7** — not added, as the reviewer advises.

### Missing sub-themes

- **The words of the covenant in the land of Moab** — not added as a core concept. The texts develop it in one place only: Deut 29, a single speech. No other passage names the Moab covenant. Deut 29:1 stays under "The covenant at Sinai" because the verse names Horeb itself. The rest of Deut 29 stays in the concepts whose words it carries.
- **My covenant with the day and the night** — added as concept 3, under a different name: "The covenant between Me and the earth" (Gen 9:13's phrase). The reviewer's name fits Jer 33:20 and 33:25 but says less than Hos 2:18 (beasts, birds, creeping things) and Job 5:23 (stones and wild animals); Gen 9:13's phrase covers all of them. Passages: Gen 9:13; Jer 33:20; Jer 33:25; Hos 2:18; Job 5:23, which is in the BSB as "a covenant with the stones of the field".
- **This will be My covenant with them** — added as concept 32, "This is My covenant with them" (the table's row): Isa 59:21; Jer 31:33; Rom 11:27; Heb 8:10; Heb 10:16. Each verse is quoted only for its "this is My covenant" sentence, with no gloss. Isa 27:9 is not listed because the BSB verse has no covenant in it.
- **The messenger of the covenant** — added as concept 18: Mal 2:7 ("he is the messenger of the LORD of Hosts"), Mal 2:8 ("the covenant of Levi") and Mal 3:1 ("the Messenger of the covenant, in whom you delight"). It is developed in two places, both in Malachi. Nothing identifies the messenger, and the concept is not under "A mediator" or "A new covenant". The name is lowercase, since the capital in Mal 3:1 is the translators' reading; the quotation keeps it as the BSB writes it.

The last core concept is still the theme's own words of hope: "I will never break My covenant" (now 34).

- Joshua 9:15 checked against MACULA (Claude): the second clause is karat berit, "he made to them a covenant to let them live"; the Berean renders it "a treaty of peace". The verse stands.

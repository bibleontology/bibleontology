# Changes: theme 19, wisdom_literature

## Copilot's concepts

1. Renamed: "Fear of the LORD as Foundation of Wisdom" → "The fear of the LORD". The passages say "beginning", not "foundation". The new name matches the site's card. Proverbs 15:33, Job 1:1 and Ecclesiastes 12:13 added.
2. Renamed: "Wisdom as Skill in God‑Ordered Living" → "Get wisdom, get understanding". "Skill in God-ordered living" is a gloss. The kept passages (Prov 4:5–7, Eccl 7:11–12) say "Get wisdom". Prov 1:1–6 cut. Prov 3:1–12 split into its own sub-themes (3:5–7 trust, 3:11–12 discipline, 3:13–15 here).
3. Renamed: "Wisdom Rooted in Creation Order" → "The LORD founded the earth by wisdom" (Prov 3:19 in its own words). Psalm 104 cut to 104:24. Job 38–41 moved to the whirlwind sub-theme and cut to verses. Proverbs 8:22–31 cut to 8:22–23 and 8:27–31. Jeremiah 10:12 and Job 28:25–27 added.
4. Renamed: "Wisdom as Divine Gift" → "The LORD gives wisdom" (Prov 2:6 in its own words). Prov 2:6, Eccl 2:26, Ps 51:6 and James 3:17 added.
5. Dropped: "Human Experience Interpreted Before God". This is a framing, and it cited whole books (Job 1–37, Ecclesiastes 1–12, "Psalms"). Its passages are now spread across specific sub-themes (The LORD gave…, Miserable comforters, Futility of futilities, Eat and drink, How long, O LORD?).
6. Dropped: "Poetic Theology (Parallelism, Imagery, Lament, Praise)". This names a literary genre, not something the passages say. Psalm 1 went to "The path of the righteous and the way of the wicked", Psalm 42–43 to the hope sub-theme (42:11), Psalm 103 to "All flesh is like grass" and "Man is like a breath" (103:14–16), and Lamentations 3 to the hope sub-theme (3:21–24). Psalm 23 was not kept.
7. Renamed: "Retribution Principle (Proverbs)" → "Sowing and reaping". The texts' own figure: Prov 11:18, Prov 22:8, Hos 8:7, Gal 6:7. Proverbs 10–15 cut to single verses. Prov 24:12 and Ps 62:12 added (the table row "He will repay each one according to his deeds").
8. Renamed: "Limits of Retribution (Job, Ecclesiastes)" → "Why do the wicked prosper?". The texts ask this in so many words (Jer 12:1, Job 21:7). Job 21 cut to 21:7–9 and 21:23–26. Ps 73 and Hab 1:13 added. Job 1:1 moved to "The fear of the LORD".
9. Renamed: "Wisdom as Instruction (Torah‑Wisdom Overlap)" → "Making wise the simple" (Ps 19:7). "Torah-Wisdom overlap" is a scholar's label. Ps 119:97–104 cut to 119:98–100 and 119:130 added. Ps 1:2 added.
10. Renamed: "Wisdom Personified" → "Wisdom calls out" (Prov 1:20; 8:1). Proverbs 8 and 9 cut to the verses where she calls (8:1–4, 8:35–36, 9:1–6), plus Folly's call (9:13–18).
11. Renamed: "Wisdom in Suffering & Lament" → "How long, O LORD?". No passage says wisdom comes "through suffering". The lament psalms say "How long" (Ps 13:1, 6:3, 94:3; Hab 1:2; Rev 6:10). Job 3–37 cut to Job 3:1–3. Lamentations 3 went to the hope sub-theme. Ps 42–43 is now 42:11 in the hope sub-theme.
12. Renamed: "Wisdom in Praise & Thanksgiving" → "Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good". This wording recurs (Ps 106:1, 107:1, 118:1, 136:1; 1 Chr 16:34; Jer 33:11). Whole psalms cut to verses. Ps 34:8 added (the table row "Taste and see").
13. Renamed: "Wisdom in Human Mortality & Meaning" → "Teach us to number our days" (Ps 90:12, quoted on the front page). Its Ecclesiastes passages were split out into "Futility of futilities" and "To everything there is a season".
14. Renamed: "Christ as Wisdom of God" → "Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God" (1 Cor 1:24, the New Testament's own words). John 1:1–14 cut: it speaks of the Word, not of wisdom. Col 2 cut to 2:2–3. 1 Cor 2:7 and Luke 2:40, 52 added (the table row "In favor with God and man"). No Old Testament passage is placed under it.

## Sections C and D

- C rewritten in the texts' words, with references cut to verses. Copilot's "Wisdom → X" lines were mostly repeats of the core concepts and are now core concepts themselves.
- D reduced to the links the passages support. Image of God comes from James 3:9, "made in God's likeness". Children of God comes from Heb 12:5, "addresses you as sons". Spirit comes from Exod 31:3. Covenant, Kingdom, Blessing & Commission, Sacrifice, Priesthood, Temple/Presence, Deliverance/Salvation, Church and New Creation were dropped, because no passage here is about them. Copilot's "Christology" became the allowed label "Messiah/Christology".

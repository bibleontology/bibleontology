# The four new themes' side-by-side tables: choosing and drafting the rows

Read `7.6b-G3-DRAFT.md` first: its rules hold here unchanged -- the
columns, shared or closely matching words, two columns a row, cells copied
exactly from `bsb.txt` and never quoted from memory, asides only for
another subject entirely, one home for each row (every table's rows, shown
or drafted, are listed at the end of your input), `overlaps`, the output
form, and no interpretation of any kind. And **a row can take a side by
its pairing alone, with every line in quotation** -- by setting two verses
so that one answers the other, or so that one names who the other is
about. Do not. You are drafting ONE table (your prompt says which). Your
input begins with the theme's own outline, every verse in full.

- **Father, Son and Holy Spirit (the Trinity) -- "Father, Son and Spirit
  side by side"**: the wordings the texts share where they speak of the
  Father, the Son and the Spirit together, of God as one, and of them
  distinctly: "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy
  Spirit", the Spirit descending at the baptism with "This is My beloved
  Son" (in each Gospel), "the LORD is One", "one God", "I and the Father
  are one" and "that they may be one as We are one", "the Father is greater
  than I", "the Spirit of truth", "the Spirit of God" and "the Spirit of
  Christ", "at the right hand of God". **It says neither that the three are
  one God nor that they are not**: both "I and the Father are one" and "the
  Father is greater than I" are the texts' words, and **no row, line or
  pairing may set one to answer the other.** The word "Trinity" appears in
  no title or line. Only verses that name at least two of the Father (or
  God), the Son (or Jesus, the Word) and the Spirit together, or that say
  God is one.
- **Spiritual Warfare -- "The armor and the struggle side by side"**: it
  **hinges on Ephesians 6:10-18**, and its first rows are the armor: the
  pieces' words are shared elsewhere ("He put on righteousness like a
  breastplate, and the helmet of salvation on His head", Isaiah 59:17;
  "the breastplate of faith and love, and the helmet of our hope of
  salvation", 1 Thessalonians 5:8; "the armor of light", Romans 13:12; "the
  word of God ... sharper than any double-edged sword", Hebrews 4:12). Then
  "resist the devil", "stand", the rulers and authorities, Satan before the
  LORD (Job 1-2, Zechariah 3), unclean spirits commanded, "the prince of
  this world", Michael. **Take no side** on whether "the satan" of Job and
  Zechariah is a title or a name, on demons today, territorial spirits, or
  what the armor is.
- **Freedom -- "Set free, side by side"**: "Do not be afraid" (who says it,
  to whom, the reason the text gives), "Be anxious for nothing" and "do not
  worry", "will not be put to shame", slaves to sin and "set free", "no
  longer a slave, but a son" (these live here), "the house of slavery", the
  yoke broken and "Take My yoke upon you", liberty proclaimed and prisoners
  set free, "mastered" (never "addiction"), the flesh and the Spirit,
  "It is for freedom that Christ has set us free". **Take no side** on
  freedom from the Law, free will, or freedom as political or inward.
- **Children of God -- "Children of God side by side"**: "children of
  God", "sons of the living God", "Israel is My firstborn son", "born
  again" and "born of God", adoption, "Abba, Father" (where the Spirit is
  not crying out as a slave set free -- those are Freedom's), heirs, "I will
  be a Father to you", "sons of the Most High", discipline as of a son,
  Jesus' brothers. **Take no side** on who God's children are (all people,
  Israel, believers), on Israel and the church, or on whether "born of
  water and the Spirit" (John 3:5) speaks of baptism.

Between 12 and 30 rows. Write the JSON array to the path you are given, and
reply with one line: how many rows.

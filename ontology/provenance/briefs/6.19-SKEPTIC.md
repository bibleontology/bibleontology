# A second, skeptical reading

Another reader has said that each poem passage in your packet refers to the
happening told in the account given beside it. Your job is to test that,
independently. Read **every word of both texts** for every item. A psalm's
title is printed at the start of its verse 1, as the Berean prints it.

For each item, try to break the claim:
- Could the poem's words be explained without this happening — praise of
  God's power in general, an image, a shared word or name only?
- Do the poem's details fit a different account better?

Then decide:
- `clear`: the poem (or its title) unmistakably points at this happening. You
  must quote, **copied exactly** from the poem, the detail that ties it to
  this account and that no one would write by chance otherwise
  (`distinctive`), and the account's words it matches (`account_words`, with
  its verse). A machine checks both.
- `thin`: possible, but the tie rests on a common word or a general idea.
- `no`: it does not refer to this happening.

## Output

Write a JSON array to the path you are given, one object per item, in order:

```json
[{"item": 12, "verdict": "clear",
  "distinctive": "when he fled from his son Absalom", "poem_verse": "Psalm 3:1",
  "account_words": "Arise and let us flee, or we will not escape from Absalom!", "account_verse": "2 Samuel 15:14",
  "reason": "one line"}]
```

Reply with one line when done: how many clear / thin / no.

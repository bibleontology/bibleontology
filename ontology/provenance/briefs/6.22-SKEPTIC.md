# A second, skeptical reading

Another reader has said that each passage of Revelation in your packet is
linked to the other passage beside it, in the way its **kind** says:
`words` (Revelation reproduces the other passage's distinctive wording),
`image` (both describe the same distinctive image in shared terms), `names`
(Revelation names a person, place, object or happening the other passage
tells), or `key` (Revelation says in its own words what one of its images
is, and the other passage is where that image appears). Test that,
independently. Read **every word of both passages** for every item.

Try to break the claim:
- Is the shared wording or image distinctive, or common to many passages
  (a throne, glory, thunder, "the Lord God Almighty")?
- Does a different passage fit better -- one Revelation's words match more
  closely?
- For `names`: is it the same person, place or thing, or only the same word?
- For `key`: does Revelation itself say what the image is, in so many words?

Decide:
- `clear`: unmistakable. Quote, **copied exactly**, Revelation's words that
  carry the link (`rev_words`, with `rev_verse`) and the other passage's
  words they match (`other_words`, with `other_verse`). A machine checks
  both.
- `thin`: possible, but the link rests on a common word, a general idea, or
  one word of an image.
- `no`: not linked as claimed.

Never say what an image stands for, whom it means, or when anything happens.

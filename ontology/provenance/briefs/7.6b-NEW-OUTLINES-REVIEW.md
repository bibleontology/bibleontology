# Reviewing a new theme's outline

A Bible website maps the Bible's major themes. Four are new, and their
outlines are the site's own: a list of core concepts, each with the passages
it cites, then secondary concepts and links to other themes. They speak in
the site's voice, so **every concept name and every note must be in the
texts' own words or a plain label -- never an interpretation**, and the site
takes no side where Christian traditions part. Your packet is one outline,
with every passage given in full.

For every concept, check:

1. **The name against its passages.** Does every passage cited under it say
   what the name says? A name that says more than its passages -- or that
   one passage says and the others do not -- is to be renamed (in the
   texts' words) or the passage moved or dropped.
2. **The notes.** A note is an exact quotation of its verse or nothing.
3. **As nine readers would take it**: a Lutheran, a Reformed Christian, a
   Wesleyan, a Baptist / free church Christian (often dispensational), a
   Pentecostal / Charismatic, a secular academic scholar of the Bible (does
   the text say this? -- with Jewish readers of the Hebrew Bible in mind),
   a Catholic, an Eastern Orthodox Christian, and a biblical-theological
   (literary) reader. Would any object that a name, or the choice and
   grouping of passages, takes a side?

What each outline must not do:
- **Father, Son and Holy Spirit:** say, or imply by its names or its
  grouping, that the three are one God or that they are not; use the word
  "Trinity"; include a passage that names none of the Father (or God), the
  Son (or Jesus, the Word) and the Spirit together and does not say God is
  one. Both "I and the Father are one" and "The Father is greater than I"
  are the texts' words; neither may be set to answer the other.
- **Spiritual Warfare:** take a side on whether "the satan" of Job and
  Zechariah is a title or a name, whether demons are cast out today and
  how, territorial spirits, or what the armor is.
- **Freedom:** take a side on freedom from the Law, free will, or freedom
  as political or inward; use "addiction" (the texts say "mastered").
- **Children of God:** take a side on who God's children are (all people,
  Israel, believers), on Israel and the church, or on whether John 3:5's
  "water and the Spirit" speaks of baptism.

Write a JSON object to the path you are given:

```json
{"verdict": "accept" or "revise",
 "concepts": [{"n": 3, "verdict": "accept"},
              {"n": 5, "verdict": "revise", "name": "<new name or null>",
               "drop": ["<reference to drop>", ...], "why": "<reader: reason>"}],
 "secondary_or_links": ["<any change, with why>"]}
```

Reply with one line: how many concepts accepted / revised.

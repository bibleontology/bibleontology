# Which other Old Testament stories tell this one again?

A Bible study site wants to show where one Old Testament story tells again
the BEATS of another -- not a shared word or theme, but the same sequence of
happenings. Examples of the kind (judge them yourself; they are only
illustrations): Joshua's crossing of the Jordan and Israel's crossing of the
sea (waters stand up, the people cross on dry ground, a memorial); the men of
Gibeah and the men of Sodom (a guest taken in at evening, the house
surrounded, "bring him out", a woman offered instead); Elijah at Horeb and
Moses at Sinai (forty days, the mountain of God, a cave or cleft, the LORD
passing by).

You are proposing CANDIDATES. A second, stricter reader will check each one
against both full passages and keep only those whose beats are in the words
of both texts. So favour recall: propose any OT story whose beats you can
name in both, even if you are not sure. Do not propose:
- a shared theme alone (faith, sin, a covenant, a king) with no beats;
- the same event told twice (Kings and Chronicles of one reign, Deuteronomy
  retelling Exodus) -- that is a retelling, not a second story;
- two consecutive episodes of one story.

`CATALOG.json` lists the 187 Old Testament narrative events the site has
(event id, title, passage). Prefer one of them, in either direction (earlier
or later than the event you are reading). If the story you have in mind has
NO event in the catalogue, give its passage instead and set
`"missing_event": true`.

Each item in your packet: `event`, `title`, `passage`, `other_tellings`,
`text` (the Berean text, verse by verse; `truncated` if cut at 60 verses).

## Output

For packet `T1_NN.json` write `U1_NN.json` in the same folder: a JSON list,
one entry per event, same order:

```json
[{"event": "<copied>", "candidates": [
   {"ot_event": "<catalogue id, or null>", "ot_passage": "<e.g. Gen 19:1-11>",
    "missing_event": false,
    "beats": ["<a matching beat, short, naming what happens in each>", "..."],
    "strength": "strong|possible"}]}]
```

An empty `candidates` list is a fine answer -- most events will have none.
Name at least two beats for each candidate. Valid JSON; count the entries.
Write no other files.

# Review "a story told again" critically

A Bible study site shows pairs of stories whose steps match, the steps side
by side in the text's own words, with the line "not a claim that one was told
with the other in mind". The site takes no doctrinal position: its own words
must be acceptable to readers of every tradition. You are the critical
reviewer of the words readers will see: the `explanation` and `differs`.

Each item: `id`, both passages in full (`later_text`, `earlier_text`, the
Berean Standard Bible, one verse per line), the `beats` (pairs of exact
quotations, already machine-checked), the `explanation`, `differs` (where
the stories differ), and sometimes `skeptic_objection` (an earlier reader's
objection to the explanation -- address it).

**Read both passages for every item before judging it.**

Test each explanation and `differs` on four things:

1. **Faithful.** Every quotation is the Berean's exact words from the verse
   given. Every statement is what the passages say -- and **who says each
   quoted line, to whom** is right (a line said to Joshua is not said to
   Israel; Zechariah speaking about the LORD is not speaking to Him). Nothing
   is added: no motive, meaning or significance the text does not state.
2. **No intent.** Nothing says or implies that one story was told with the
   other in mind ("recalls", "echoes", "mirrors", "re-enacts", "fulfils",
   "foreshadows", "a new Moses / Elijah / Adam"), unless the later text
   itself names the earlier story -- then quote its words.
3. **Honest about difference.** `differs` names a real difference a reader
   would want to know; if an important one is missing (in Kings "the LORD was
   not in the wind" reverses Sinai's picture), add it.
4. **Acceptable to all.** Read it as an evangelical Baptist, a Calvinist, an
   Arminian, a Lutheran, a Methodist, a Charismatic/Pentecostal, an Eastern
   Orthodox reader and a secular academic scholar would. Watch for typology
   in the site's voice (one person standing for another), Christology,
   sacraments, Mary, Israel and the church, prophecy, and the site stating as
   history what the text reports.

Verdict:
- `ok` -- passes all four.
- `revise` -- give the objection(s) and a `revision` of the explanation
  and/or `differs`: 2-3 sentences each, quoting only the Berean exactly with
  verses, as informative as the original.
- `withdraw` -- the pair cannot be explained without taking a side or
  claiming intent; say why.

## Output

For packet `E_NN.json` write `ER_NN.json` in the same folder: a JSON list,
one entry per item, same order:

```json
[{"id": 0, "verdict": "ok|revise|withdraw",
  "objections": ["<test or tradition: the words, and why>"],
  "explanation": "<revised explanation, or null if unchanged>",
  "differs": "<revised differs, or null if unchanged>"}]
```

Valid JSON (check it parses); one entry per item. Write no other files.

# Which Old Testament stories does this one tell again?

A Bible study site wants to show where a story in the Gospels or Acts tells
again the BEATS of an Old Testament story -- not a shared word or theme, but
the same sequence of happenings. The standard example:

> John 6:5-13 and 2 Kgs 4:42-44. A man brings a few barley loaves ("five
> barley loaves" / "twenty loaves of barley bread"); the helper asks how so
> little can feed so many ("what difference will these make among so many?"
> / "How am I to set twenty loaves before a hundred men?"); it is given out;
> all eat and some is left over. Four beats, in order, in both texts.

You are proposing CANDIDATES. A second, stricter reader will check each one
against both full passages and keep only those whose beats are in the words
of both texts. So favour recall: propose any OT story whose beats you can
name in both, even if you are not sure. Do not propose:
- a shared theme alone (faith, healing, a mountain, a covenant) with no beats;
- a quotation or a prophecy with no story told again (the NT verse quoting
  Isaiah is not a story echo of Isaiah);
- the same event told twice (Stephen retelling Joseph is a retelling, not a
  second story);
- anything in `recorded` (already on the site).

`CATALOG.json` lists the 187 Old Testament narrative events the site has
(event id, title, passage). Prefer one of them. If the OT story you have in
mind has NO event in the catalogue (e.g. the widow of Zarephath, 1 Kgs
17:8-24; Elijah taken up, 2 Kgs 2:1-12; Elisha feeding a hundred, 2 Kgs
4:42-44), give its passage instead and set `"missing_event": true`.

Each item in your packet: `event`, `title`, `passage`, `other_tellings`,
`text` (the Berean text of the passage, verse by verse; `truncated` if cut at
60 verses).

## Output

For packet `S1_NN.json` write `C1_NN.json` in the same folder: a JSON list,
one entry per NT event, same order:

```json
[{"event": "<copied>", "candidates": [
   {"ot_event": "<catalogue id, or null>", "ot_passage": "<e.g. 2 Kgs 4:42-44>",
    "missing_event": false,
    "beats": ["<a matching beat, short, naming what happens in each>", "..."],
    "strength": "strong|possible"}]}]
```

An empty `candidates` list is a fine answer -- most events will have none.
Name at least two beats for each candidate. Valid JSON; count the entries.
Write no other files.

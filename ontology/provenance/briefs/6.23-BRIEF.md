# Reading the history books for their links to each other

You are one of sixty-two readers. Your packet is part of Genesis–Esther in
the Berean Standard Bible, every verse numbered. Read **every verse** of the
packet, in order. Find every place where the text is tied to **an earlier
happening that an earlier passage of Genesis–Esther tells** -- or, the other
way about, says something happened as an earlier passage said it would.

## Three kinds, kept apart

Every link you list is exactly one of these. Never merge them.

**`back` -- names or retells an earlier happening**, with a detail from its
account:
- Joshua's farewell retelling the exodus and the conquest (Josh 24:2–13)
- Samuel recalling Jacob going down to Egypt and the judges (1 Sam 12:8–11)
- the Levites' prayer retelling creation to the return (Neh 9:6–31)
- Moses retelling the golden calf (Deut 9:7–21) or the spies (Deut 1:22–46)
- "Remember what the Amalekites did to you along your way from Egypt"
  (Deut 25:17; 1 Sam 15:2) -- Exod 17:8–16
- "another famine in the land, subsequent to the one that had occurred in
  Abraham's time" (Gen 26:1) -- Gen 12:10
- a genealogy that tells a happening ("Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in
  the sight of the LORD. So the LORD put him to death", 1 Chr 2:3) -- Gen 38:7

**`fulfilled` -- says it happened as an earlier passage said**, and that
saying is told in an earlier passage:
- Hiel rebuilding Jericho "according to the word that the LORD had spoken
  through Joshua son of Nun" (1 Kgs 16:34) -- Josh 6:26
- Solomon banishing Abiathar, "and thus fulfilled the word that the LORD had
  spoken at Shiloh against the house of Eli" (1 Kgs 2:27) -- 1 Sam 2:27–36
- Josiah burning bones on Bethel's altar "according to the word of the
  LORD proclaimed by the man of God" (2 Kgs 23:16) -- 1 Kgs 13:2
- "Not one of all the LORD's good promises to the house of Israel had
  failed" (Josh 21:45)

**`quotes` -- quotes an earlier passage's words**, often "as it is written
in the Law of Moses":
- "Fathers must not be put to death for their children" (2 Kgs 14:6) --
  Deut 24:16, "Fathers shall not be put to death for their children"
- the feast kept "in accordance with what is written" (Ezra 3:4; Neh
  8:14–15) -- Lev 23

## What does not count

- **A parallel telling** -- Chronicles telling what Samuel–Kings tells, or
  one book's account of an event another book also tells. These are already
  joined; do not list them.
- A passage pointing within its own account ("the Valley of Achor to this
  day", Josh 7:26, inside the Achan story).
- A name alone (a person or place mentioned, with no detail of a happening).
- Law or instruction that names no happening.
- Anything outside Genesis–Esther (the prophets, psalms, letters and
  Revelation have been done).

When unsure, include it and say so in `note`; the judges who follow decide.
Missing a link is worse than listing a doubtful one.

## Neutrality

Name what the text names. Do not say what a passage means or why it was
written. Where readers dispute **which** earlier happening is meant, list
it with `"disputed": true` and every candidate passage -- do not choose.

## Quoting exactly

Both quotations are checked by machine against the Berean. Your passage's
words come from the packet. For the earlier passage, **look it up**: the
whole Berean is in `bsb.txt` (path given with your packet), one verse per
line as "Book chapter:verse text". Search it (Grep) for a distinctive word
or for the reference ("^Joshua 6:26 ") and copy the words exactly. Do not
quote from memory.

## Output

Write one JSON file to the path you are given, exactly this shape:

```json
{
  "chapters_read": {"1 Kings 16": 1, "1 Kings 17": 0},
  "refs": [
    {
      "hist": "1 Kings 16:34",
      "hist_end": "1 Kings 16:34",
      "quote": "according to the word that the LORD had spoken through Joshua son of Nun",
      "kind": "fulfilled",
      "earlier": "Joshua 6:26",
      "earlier_end": "Joshua 6:26",
      "earlier_quote": "Cursed before the LORD is the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, Jericho",
      "event": "Joshua's curse on Jericho",
      "listed_event": null,
      "how": "fulfilled",
      "disputed": false,
      "note": ""
    }
  ]
}
```

- `chapters_read`: **every** chapter in your packet, with how many links you
  found in it (0 is a valid answer).
- `quote` / `earlier_quote`: copied exactly, the shortest span that shows
  the link; `...` may join two pieces of one verse.
- `kind`: `back`, `fulfilled` or `quotes`.
- `listed_event`: the matching title from `events.txt` if one fits the
  earlier happening, else null.
- `how`: `names`, `retells`, `fulfilled`, `quotes`, or `content` (only the
  content suggests the happening).
- One entry per link.

When done, reply with one line: how many links of each kind, and how many
chapters.

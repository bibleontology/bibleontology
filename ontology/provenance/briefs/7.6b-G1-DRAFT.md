# Three side-by-side tables for the Creation group: choosing and drafting the rows

A Bible website's theme pages each lead to one table that sets side by side
what different books say of one thing, in the texts' own words, with what
is alike and what differs. You are drafting ONE of these three (your prompt
says which):

- **Creation -- "Creation told again"**: the places that tell or retell the
  making of the world (Genesis 1 and 2, Psalm 104, Job 38, Proverbs 8,
  Isaiah 40 and 45, John 1, Colossians 1, Hebrews 11:3, Revelation 4:11).
  A row is one thing they tell in shared or closely matching words: "In
  the beginning"; the heavens stretched out; the foundations of the earth;
  by the word; the waters set their bounds; the light; the creatures; the
  man from the dust; the rest.
- **Image of God -- "The image side by side"**: every place that speaks of
  humankind made in God's image or likeness, or of an image or likeness
  restored or borne (Genesis 1:26-27, 5:1-3, 9:6; Psalm 8; 1 Corinthians
  11:7, 15:49; 2 Corinthians 3:18, 4:4; Colossians 1:15, 3:10; James 3:9).
  A row is one thing the texts say of it in shared words.
- **Blessing & Commission -- "Blessed and sent"**: the blessings spoken and
  the commissions given (Genesis 1:28, 9:1, 12:1-3, 22:18, 26:4, 28:14;
  Numbers 6:24-26; Isaiah 49:6; Matthew 28:19-20; Luke 24:47; Acts 1:8,
  3:25; Galatians 3:8). A row is one blessing or one charge, set beside the
  places that repeat or take up its words.

You are given the theme's input file: the passages the site's theme map
already cites for it, concept by concept, and every verse of the Berean
Standard Bible with the theme's key words. The whole Berean is in `bsb.txt`,
one verse per line. **Look every quotation up there and copy it exactly;
never quote from memory.**

The table has these columns:
**Genesis | The Prophets | Psalms & elsewhere | Gospels & Acts | The letters |
Revelation** ("The Prophets" is Isaiah to Malachi; "Psalms & elsewhere" is
any other Old Testament book.)

## Choose the rows

Between 12 and 25 rows. A row is one thing said in more than one source **in
shared or closely matching words**; a shared idea with no shared wording is
not a row. A row needs at least two columns. Leave out nothing that uses the
row's own words: a place that uses them of something else may be kept as an
aside (see below).

## For each row, write

- `title`: a few plain words naming what the row shows, in the texts' own
  terms.
- `cells`: for each column that has it, the verses and **the words that
  carry it**, copied exactly: `{"Genesis": [["Genesis 1:1", "In the
  beginning God created the heavens and the earth"]], ...}`. Short spans.
  A place that uses the row's words of something else is kept with a third
  value: `["Proverbs 3:18", "She is a tree of life", "aside"]`.
- `alike`: one or two sentences saying what the passages share, quoting them.
- `different`: one or two sentences saying where the wording differs -- who
  speaks, who acts, what is added or left out. Quote the words that differ.
  If nothing differs worth saying, `""`.

## The rule that governs every word

**No interpretation, of any kind.** Never say what anything means, whether
one passage looks forward to, fulfils or echoes another, or why it was
written. Each table has its own disputes the site takes no side on:
- **Creation:** whether the days are days, how old the earth is, how
  creation came about, how Genesis 1 and 2 relate. Report what each text
  says, in its own words; "Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 tell the making in a
  different order" is a judgement -- say instead what each says first.
- **Image of God:** what the image consists of (reason, relationship, rule,
  the soul), whether it was lost or marred, and who bears it now. Report only
  what each text says of it.
- **Blessing & Commission:** how the blessing to Abraham relates to the
  nations, the church or Israel, and whether the commission in Genesis 1 and
  the one in Matthew 28 are one charge or two. Report what each text says.

Every reader -- Lutheran, Reformed, Wesleyan, Baptist, Pentecostal,
Catholic, Orthodox, a secular scholar, a biblical-theological reader -- must
be able to accept every sentence, because every sentence only reports what
the texts say.

Every quotation is checked by machine against the verse you give.

## Output

Write a JSON array to the path you are given, one object per row:

```json
[{"title": "In the beginning",
  "cells": {"Genesis": [["Genesis 1:1", "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth"]],
            "Gospels & Acts": [["John 1:1", "In the beginning was the Word"]]},
  "alike": "...", "different": "..."}]
```

Reply with one line: how many rows.

# The Law table: rows on what the texts say about the Law itself

Read `7.6b-G2-DRAFT.md` first: its rules hold here unchanged (shared or
closely matching words, two columns, cells copied exactly from `bsb.txt`,
asides only for another subject entirely, one home for each row, no
interpretation). This brief adds rows to a table already drafted.

The theme is now **The Law** -- the Torah, the commandments, and what the
texts say of the Law -- and its table, "The commandments taken up", holds 32
rows: two bridge rows ("Fear the LORD and keep His commandments", "Making
wise the simple") and thirty commandments, each beside the passages that
quote it. The Sermon on the Mount's "You have heard that it was said" is
already in those rows. Its titles are in your input, with every other
table's.

**Add the rows on what the texts say about the Law itself**, wherever more
than one passage says it in shared or closely matching words. For example
(these are the Berean's words; search `bsb.txt` for every other place):

- "the law is holy" (Rom 7:12), "The Law of the LORD is perfect" (Ps 19:7),
  "the perfect law of freedom" (Jas 1:25)
- "the law merely brings awareness of sin" (Rom 3:20), "I would not have
  been mindful of sin if not for the law" (Rom 7:7)
- "not under law" (Rom 6:14, Gal 5:18)
- "the doers of the law" (Rom 2:13), "Be doers of the word, and not hearers
  only" (Jas 1:22)
- "to fulfill" (Matt 5:17), "has fulfilled the law" (Rom 13:8), "love is
  the fulfillment of the law" (Rom 13:10), "The entire law is fulfilled in
  a single decree" (Gal 5:14)
- "not justified by works of the law" (Rom 3:20, Gal 2:16), and Psalm
  143:2's "no one alive is righteous before You"
- "Do we, then, nullify the law by this faith? Certainly not!" (Rom 3:31),
  "not a single jot ... will disappear from the Law" (Matt 5:18)

Between 4 and 10 new rows. Number nothing; write only the new rows.

## The disputes, which here are at their sharpest

How a person is made right with God, and what the Law has to do with it,
is the question on which Lutheran, Reformed, Wesleyan, Catholic and
Orthodox readers most plainly part, and Jewish readers of the Hebrew Bible
read the Law as their own. So:

- **Report each verse's words; never sum up what Paul, James or Jesus
  "teaches about" the Law.** No line says the Law is or is not binding,
  ended or upheld, except in a verse's own words, in its cell.
- **Never set one verse against another as a contradiction, and never
  reconcile them.** Romans 3:28 and James 2:24 may both be quoted if their
  words fit a row; the line says what each says, and stops.
- "Different" lines say who speaks and to whom, and what each verse adds,
  in its words. Nothing like "Paul, by contrast", "James corrects", "Jesus
  intensifies", "the Law could not".

## Output

Write a JSON array of the new rows to the path you are given, in the same
form as the brief's (`title`, `cells`, `alike`, `different`, and
`overlaps` if any). Reply with one line: how many rows.

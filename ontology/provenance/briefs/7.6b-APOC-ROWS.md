# The apocalyptic imagery table: choosing its rows

A Bible website is building, on its "Apocalyptic" theme page, one table that
sets side by side how Daniel, Ezekiel, Isaiah, the other prophets, the
Gospels and letters, and Revelation describe the same images -- what is
alike, and what differs. It takes **no interpretive stance**: it never says
what an image stands for, whom it means, whether one passage looks forward
to another, or when anything happens.

You are given `links.txt`: every link already judged between a verse of
Revelation and another passage (`L…` lines: kind, certainty, both passages
and their matched words), and the links from the prophets to the Gospels
(`F…` lines). The whole Berean is in `bsb.txt`.

## Your task

Group the links into **rows**: one row per image or scene that two or more
books describe -- "the throne and the living creatures", "beasts from the
sea and ten horns", "the scroll that is eaten", "the measuring rod", "one
like the Son of Man", "the sun darkened, the moon to blood, the stars
falling", "the harvest and the winepress", "the fall of Babylon", "the
plagues of Egypt", "the river and the tree of life". Aim for every image
that recurs, not a sample: probably 30 to 50 rows.

For each row:
- `title`: a plain name for the image, in the texts' own terms. Never an
  interpretation ("the Antichrist", "the Church", "the Millennium").
- `links`: the ids (`L…`, `F…`) that belong to it.
- `also`: other verses that describe the **same** image in one of the
  columns but have no link yet -- for example a Daniel verse that a Gospel
  passage also takes up. Give each as `["Daniel 7:9", "exact words"]`,
  copied exactly from `bsb.txt`. Only where the image is unmistakably the
  same.

Leave out links that are only a stock phrase with no image ("the Lord God
Almighty", "forever and ever"); they belong to no row.

## Output

Write a JSON array to the path you are given:

```json
[{"title": "The scroll that is eaten", "links": ["L512", "L513"],
  "also": [["Ezekiel 3:3", "it was as sweet as honey in my mouth"]]}]
```

Reply with one line: how many rows, and how many links placed.

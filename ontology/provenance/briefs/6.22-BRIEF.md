# Reading Revelation for its links to the rest of Scripture

You are one of twenty-two readers. Your packet is two chapters of Revelation
in the Berean Standard Bible, every verse numbered. Read **every verse**, in
order, and list **every objective textual link** from it to the rest of
Scripture -- Old Testament, New Testament, and Revelation itself.

An objective link is one a reader can see by putting the two texts side by
side: the same distinctive words, the same named person or place, the same
distinctive image described in the same terms, or Revelation saying in its
own words what one of its images is. Nothing else.

## Four kinds, kept apart

**`words` -- words taken up.** Revelation's wording quotes or closely
reproduces an earlier passage's distinctive words.
- "Look, He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see Him, even
  those who pierced Him" (Rev 1:7) -- Dan 7:13, Zech 12:10
- "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty" (Rev 4:8) -- Isa 6:3
- "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great" (Rev 14:8; 18:2) -- Isa 21:9
- "God will wipe away every tear from their eyes" (Rev 7:17; 21:4) -- Isa 25:8

**`image` -- an image both use.** Revelation and an earlier passage describe
the same distinctive image or figure in shared terms, with no claim that one
points to the other.
- the four living creatures, full of eyes, with the faces of a lion, an ox,
  a man and an eagle (Rev 4:6-7) -- Ezek 1:10, 18; 10:12-14
- a beast with ten horns (Rev 13:1) -- Dan 7:7
- a scroll written on both sides, given to be eaten, sweet as honey (Rev
  5:1; 10:9-10) -- Ezek 2:9-10; 3:1-3
- water turned to blood, frogs, hail, darkness, sores (Rev 16) -- Exod 7-10

**`names` -- names it.** Revelation names a person, place, object or
happening that an earlier passage tells.
- "Balaam, who taught Balak to place a stumbling block before the
  Israelites" (Rev 2:14) -- Num 22-25; 31:16
- "that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess" (Rev 2:20) -- 1 Kgs
  16:31; 21
- "the song of Moses the servant of God" (Rev 15:3) -- Exod 15; Deut 32
- "the tree of life" (Rev 2:7; 22:2) -- Gen 2:9; 3:22-24
- "the hidden manna" (Rev 2:17) -- Exod 16
A name used figuratively ("figuratively called Sodom and Egypt", Rev 11:8) is
`names`: list the name and the passage that tells it; do not say what the
figure stands for.

**`key` -- Revelation's own key.** Revelation says, in its own words, what
one of its images is. The link is from the explaining words to the verse
where the image appears.
- "the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven
  lampstands are the seven churches" (Rev 1:20) -- Rev 1:12, 16
- "the bowls of incense, which are the prayers of the saints" (Rev 5:8)
- "the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan" (Rev 12:9)
- "the ten horns you saw are ten kings" (Rev 17:12) -- Rev 17:3, 7
Only where the text itself says "is", "are", "which are", "called",
"means" or the like. Your own explanation of an image is never a key.

## What does not count

- A shared common word or theme with no distinctive detail ("throne",
  "glory", "judgment", "the Lord God Almighty" alone).
- A likeness you have to argue for.
- What a vision stands for, refers to, or when it happens. **Never write
  this**, not even in `note`. Readers and traditions differ on all of it;
  this work maps only what the text itself puts side by side.

When unsure whether a link is objective, include it and say why in `note`;
the judges who follow decide. Missing a link is worse than listing a
doubtful one.

## Quoting exactly

Every quotation is checked by machine against the Berean. The Revelation
words come from your packet. For the other passage, **look it up**: the
whole Berean is in `bsb.txt` (path given with your packet), one verse per
line as "Book chapter:verse text". Search it (Grep) for a distinctive word
or for the reference ("^Ezekiel 1:10 ") and copy the words exactly. Do not
quote from memory.

## Output

Write one JSON file to the path you are given, exactly this shape:

```json
{
  "chapters_read": {"Revelation 1": 0, "Revelation 2": 0},
  "links": [
    {
      "rev": "Revelation 1:7",
      "rev_end": "Revelation 1:7",
      "rev_words": "every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him",
      "kind": "words",
      "other": "Zechariah 12:10",
      "other_end": "Zechariah 12:10",
      "other_words": "they will look on Me, the One they have pierced",
      "disputed": false,
      "note": ""
    }
  ]
}
```

- `chapters_read`: both chapters, with how many links you found in each.
- `rev_words` / `other_words`: copied exactly, the shortest span that shows
  the link; `...` may join two pieces of one verse.
- One entry per pair of passages. A Revelation verse taking up two passages
  (Rev 1:7: Daniel and Zechariah) is two entries.
- `disputed`: true where readers disagree which earlier passage is meant;
  list each candidate as its own entry, all marked.

When done, reply with one line: how many links of each kind.

# The prophets' words, taken up later: choosing and drafting the rows

A Bible website's "Prophets and Prophecy" theme page sets side by side a
prophet's words and the later passages that take them up -- that quote
them, or say something happened "as was spoken" by the prophet. One table,
in the texts' own words, with what is alike and what differs.

You are given `input.md`: (A) every place the Berean Standard Bible's own
editors mark a later passage as quoting a prophet (Isaiah to Malachi), with
both verses in full; (B) the later passages this site has already judged to
take up a prophet's words. The whole Berean is in `bsb.txt`, one verse per
line ("Isaiah 7:14 Therefore the Lord Himself..."). **Look every quotation
up there and copy it exactly; never quote from memory.**

The table has these columns:
**The Prophets | Elsewhere in the Old Testament | Gospels & Acts | The
letters | Revelation** ("The Prophets" is Isaiah to Malachi. A prophet taken
up by another prophet -- Jeremiah 26:18 quoting Micah 3:12 -- has both in
"The Prophets". "Elsewhere in the Old Testament" is any other OT book:
2 Chronicles 36:21-22 and Ezra 1:1 naming Jeremiah's word.)

## Choose the rows

Between 25 and 40 rows. A row is **one prophet's passage and the later
passages that take up its words**. Prefer, in this order: a passage taken
up in more than one later book; one the later text itself says was spoken
by the prophet ("as it is written in the prophet Isaiah", "to fulfill what
was spoken through the prophet"); one taken up within the Old Testament.
Spread the rows across the prophets, not only Isaiah. Leave out a later
passage whose words do not match the prophet's: a shared idea is not a row.

## For each row, write

- `title`: the prophet's own words, a few of them, as the row's name
  ("Behold, the virgin will be with child", "Out of Egypt I called My Son").
- `cells`: for each column that has it, the verses and **the words that
  carry the match**, copied exactly: `{"The Prophets": [["Hosea 11:1", "out
  of Egypt I called My son"]], "Gospels & Acts": [["Matthew 2:15", "Out of
  Egypt I called My Son"]]}`. Where the later passage has its own words
  about the prophet ("This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the
  prophet", Matthew 2:15), put those words in the same cell, as a second span: they
  are the text speaking, and the site never says them in its own voice.
- `alike`: one or two sentences saying what wording the passages share,
  quoting them.
- `different`: one or two sentences saying where the wording differs -- a
  word changed, added or left out, an order, who speaks, to whom; and what
  the later passage says about the prophet's words, quoted. Quote the words
  that differ. If nothing differs worth saying, `""`.

## The rule that governs every word

**No interpretation, of any kind, and never the site's own claim that
anything was fulfilled, predicted or foretold.** Where a later text says
"to fulfill what was spoken", quote it as that text's words; the site's own
sentences never say it. Never say whether a later use is right, whether the
prophet meant it, what a passage first referred to, or why a writer changed
a word. Readers and traditions read these pairs very differently (prediction
and fulfilment, pattern and typology, a later reading of an earlier text,
or a text with more than one sense); every reader must be able to accept
every sentence, because every sentence only reports what the texts say.
"Matthew 2:15 has 'Out of Egypt I called My Son' and says it was spoken
through the prophet; Hosea 11:1 has the same words, after 'When Israel was a
child, I loved him'" is right. "Matthew shows Hosea's prophecy fulfilled in
Jesus" is wrong. "Matthew applies Hosea typologically" is wrong.

Every quotation is checked by machine against the verse you give.

## Output

Write a JSON array to the path you are given, one object per row:

```json
[{"title": "Out of Egypt I called My Son",
  "cells": {"The Prophets": [["Hosea 11:1", "out of Egypt I called My son"]],
            "Gospels & Acts": [["Matthew 2:15", "Out of Egypt I called My Son"],
                               ["Matthew 2:15", "This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the prophet"]]},
  "alike": "...", "different": "..."}]
```

Reply with one line: how many rows.

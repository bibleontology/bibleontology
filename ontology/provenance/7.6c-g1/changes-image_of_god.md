# Theme 2, image_of_god: changes to Copilot's outline

- 1 "Imago Dei (Humanity Reflects God’s Nature)" — renamed → "In His own image": "reflects God's nature" says more than Genesis 1:26–27, 5:1, 9:6 say; the name is now Genesis 1:27 and 9:6's own phrase. Genesis 9:5–6 and James 3:9 joined it from concepts 2 and 7.
- 2 "Human Dignity Rooted in God’s Image" — renamed → "What is man that You are mindful of him?" (Psalm 8:4): "dignity" and "rooted" are no passage's words; the Psalm 8 passages carry the question the texts ask (Psalm 144:3, Job 7:17, Hebrews 2:6 repeat it), so it is the sub-theme. Genesis 9:6 and James 3:9 moved to concept 1; their three glosses removed.
- 3 "Human Vocation: Rule, Represent, Reflect" — renamed → "Dominion" (the site's card): "represent" and "reflect" frame the image as a vocation, which takes a side on what the image is; the passages say rule and subdue. Matthew 5:16 moved to section C; four glosses replaced by quotations.
- 4 "Humanity as God’s Royal Representatives" — dropped: "royal representatives" is a framing no passage states; Genesis 1:26–28 and Psalm 8 are in "Dominion", Revelation 1:6 and 5:10 in section C ("A kingdom of priests → they will reign").
- 5 "Humanity as God’s Priestly Representatives" — dropped: no passage joins the image to priesthood; the "priestly verbs" note on Genesis 2:15 and "humanity's vocation concentrated" on Exodus 19:6 are interpretations. Exodus 19:6 and 1 Peter 2:9 kept in section C beside the front page's "A kingdom of priests".
- 6 "Humanity as God’s Familial/Relational Image" — renamed → "Male and female He created them" (Genesis 1:27; 5:2): "relational image" takes a side on what the image is; the texts' own phrase is the sub-theme, with Matthew 19:4–5 and Mark 10:6–8 quoting it. Acts 17:28–29 moved to section C and concept 7.
- 7 "Image Retained After the Fall" — dropped: "the Fall" is no passage's word, and its two passages (Genesis 9:6, James 3:9) are in concept 1, where they say it themselves.
- 8 "Image Renewed in Christ" — renamed → "The old self and the new self" (the site's card): the passages' own pairing (Ephesians 4:22–24, Colossians 3:9–10); added Romans 6:6, Romans 12:2, 2 Corinthians 4:16. Romans 8:29 moved to concept 10.
- 9 "Christ as the Perfect Image" — renamed → "The image of the invisible God" (Colossians 1:15): "perfect" is no passage's word; added John 1:18 and 2 Corinthians 4:6. Hebrews 1:3's KJV gloss replaced by the Berean's words.
- 10 "Transformation into Christ’s Image" — renamed → "Transformed into His image" (2 Corinthians 3:18's own words); added Philippians 3:21.
- Added "You will be like God" (Genesis 3:5): the serpent's offer and Genesis 3:22, with Isaiah 14:14 and Ezekiel 28:2 using the same words; the front page's "Like God" row, given its passages.
- Added "The first man Adam, the last Adam" (the site's card): Genesis 2:7 and 5:3 ("in his own likeness, after his own image"), Luke 3:38, Romans 5:14, 1 Corinthians 15:45–49, each in its own words.
- Added "You saw no form of any kind" (Deuteronomy 4:15): the texts' refusal to make a likeness of God (Exodus 20:4, Isaiah 40:18, 46:5, Acts 17:29, Romans 1:23); agrees with the front page's "To whom will you liken God?" and "Exchanged their Glory for an image" rows.
- Added "Imitators of God" (Ephesians 5:1): Leviticus 19:2, 1 Peter 1:15–16, Matthew 5:44–48, Luke 6:36 say "be ... as I am / as your Father is" in so many words. Takes in Copilot's Matthew 5:16 thread through section C.
- Section C rebuilt: Copilot's "Rationality / Moral Agency", "Creativity", "Embodied Image", "Image & Mission" and "Image & Ethics" (Matthew 5–7, Ephesians 4–5) dropped as framings no passage states or as whole-chapter ranges; ten secondary concepts in the texts' words, references cut to verses.
- Section D: "Christology" → "Messiah/Christology"; "Sanctification" and "Union with Christ" dropped (not allowed labels); "Temple/Presence" dropped (no passage here overlaps it); added Law/Wisdom, Sin/Exile/Death, Children of God.

- Concept 2 (drop Genesis 2:18 and 2:22–24): applied. Neither says "male and female He created them"; Genesis 2:24 lives in section C ("Male and female → one flesh").
- Concept 2 (Mark 10:6–8 → Mark 10:6, note “God made them male and female”): applied; the note is exact in bsb.txt. For the same reason Matthew 19:4–5 was cut to Matthew 19:4 (19:5 is the one-flesh verse).

- Concept 4: accepted, no change asked. (Psalm 103:14 added from the "dust" sub-theme below.)
- Concept 5 (drop Genesis 2:17): applied. It forbids the tree and says nothing of being like God.
- Concept 6: accepted, no change.
- Concept 7 (rename to "To whom will you liken God?"): applied. bsb.txt bears the reviewer out: only Deuteronomy 4:12 and 4:15 speak of seeing no form; Isaiah 40:18's question covers all seven passages. The name is also one of the front page's table rows; it is kept as the concept's name because it is the one phrase all its passages answer, and the concept's passages go beyond the row.
- Concept 8 (now 9): accepted, no change.
- Concept 9 (now 10; drop Romans 12:2 and 2 Corinthians 4:16): applied. Romans 12:2 names no self; 2 Corinthians 4:16 pairs an outer and an inner self, not old and new. - Concepts 10 and 11 (now 11 and 12): accepted, no change.

The format needs an arrow, so the line is "Kingdom → priests (Exod 19:6; 1 Pet 2:9; Rev 1:6)", two words each verse holds, with no step from one verse to the next. - Secondary "Moses' face → the veil over his face" (cut Exodus 34:29–35): applied, now Exodus 34:29 and 34:33–35.
- Secondary "Male and female → one flesh": applied, kept as the home of Genesis 2:24 and Mark 10:8.
- Secondary "Offspring of God" (Acts 17:29 quoted twice): applied. Acts 17:29 is left to concept 7; Acts 17:28 merged with the former "From one man → every nation of men" (Acts 17:26) as "From one man → His offspring (Acts 17:26; Acts 17:28)" to make room in section C.
- Links: no change asked, none made.
- Also removed from section C: "Unveiled faces → reflect the glory of the Lord (2 Cor 3:18)", which repeated concept 11's first passage, to make room within the ten-line limit.
- Missing "Those who make them become like them": added as core concept 8. Psalm 115:8 and 135:18 say it word for word, and 2 Kings 17:15, Jeremiah 2:5 and Hosea 9:10 say "became worthless themselves" / "became as detestable as the thing they loved". - Missing "He remembers that we are dust": merged, not added as a concept. bsb.txt's Psalm 103:14 reads “He is mindful that we are dust” (not "remembers"), so it joins concept 4, "What is man that You are mindful of him?". Genesis 3:19, Ecclesiastes 3:20 and 12:7 went to section C as "Dust → to dust you shall return".

# Creation (theme 1): changes to Copilot's outline

- 1 "God as Creator": **dropped** as a core concept. It is the theme itself, and the front page and its table row "The Maker of heaven and earth, the sea and all that is in them" already carry it. It is kept as the secondary concept "God → the Creator", made up of the verses that use the word (Eccl 12:1; Isa 40:28; Matt 19:4; Rom 1:25; 1 Pet 4:19). Its other passages moved to concepts 1, 9 and 11, or are already on the front page.
- 2 "Creation by Divine Word": **renamed** to "By the word of the LORD" (Ps 33:6). Copilot's note "And God said…" over seven verses is cut to Gen 1:3, where the card "Let there be light" sits. John 1:1–3 moved to concept 11. Added Ps 148:5 and 2 Pet 3:5, which say it in so many words.
- 3 "Order, Boundaries, Function": **renamed** to "He set a boundary for the sea" (Prov 8:29), the bound given to the waters. No passage says "function". Job 38–41 is cut to Job 38:8–11. Light and darkness, and the lights for seasons, moved to the secondary concept "Day and night → the fixed order of heaven and earth". "According to their kinds" was dropped because it is not about bounds. The garden moved to concept 6. Added Gen 1:9 and Jer 5:22.
- 4 "Goodness of Creation": **renamed** to "It was good" so that it matches the card "it was good". Copilot's note on 1 Tim 4:4 ("every creature of God is good") is not what the Berean says; it is replaced with the Berean's "every creation of God is good".
- 5 "Humanity as Image-Bearers": **renamed** to "The image of God", the card's name. Ps 8:4–8 never says "image" and moved to concept 5. 2 Cor 3:18 speaks of being transformed into the Lord's image, not of creation, and was dropped. Added 1 Cor 11:7. To take no side on what the image is, this concept is kept apart from the concept of rule.
- 6 "Human Vocation (Rule, Subdue, Cultivate)": **renamed** to "Rule over the fish of the sea" (Gen 1:26, 28). "Vocation" is a gloss. The card "dominion" is not the Berean's word in these verses, so the concept uses the Berean's own words. The "cultivate" half became its own concept (concept 6, added below). Added Gen 9:2 and Jas 3:7.
- 7 "Creation as Temple‑Pattern": **dropped**. No passage says creation is a temple pattern. Exodus 25–40, 1 Kings 6–8, Ps 78:69 and John 1:14 were cited for a framing the verses do not state. Gen 2:9 and 3:24 moved to the secondary concept "The tree of life".
- 8 "Sabbath as Creation Climax": **renamed** to "The seventh day", because "climax" is a framing. Heb 4:3–10 is cut to 4:4 and 4:9–10. Exod 20:8–11 is cut to 20:11, and Exod 31:16–17 to 31:17. Added Mark 2:27.
- 9 "Creation Sustained by God": **renamed** to "All creatures look to You", which is said in both Ps 104:27 and Ps 145:15. The whole of Psalm 104 is cut to 104:27–30, and Job 38–41 is dropped. Col 1:17 and Heb 1:3 moved to concept 11. Added Neh 9:6, Job 12:10, Job 34:14–15 and Ps 147:8–9.
- 10 "Creation for Christ / Through Christ": **renamed** to "Through Him all things were made" (John 1:3), the card's name. It now cites only the New Testament verses that say it. Added John 1:10. Col 1:15–17 and Heb 1:2–3 now include the "holds together" and "upholding" verses.
- 11 "Creation Awaiting Renewal": **renamed** to "A new heaven and a new earth" (Rev 21:1; 2 Pet 3:13). Rev 21:1–5 is cut to 21:1 and 21:5. Rom 8:19–23 moved to the secondary concept "The whole creation has been groaning", the card's name. Rev 22:1–5 is cut to Rev 22:2 under "The tree of life".
- **Added** "To cultivate and keep it" (Gen 2:15), the work of the garden: Gen 2:5, 2:15, 3:23; Ps 104:14, 104:23.
- **Added** "The two will become one flesh": Gen 2:18, 2:21–24; Matt 19:4–6; Mark 10:6–9; 1 Cor 6:16; Eph 5:31.
- **Added** "The heavens declare the glory of God" (Ps 19:1), replacing Copilot's secondary concept "Creation as revelation": Ps 19:1–4, 50:6, 97:6; Job 12:7–9; Acts 14:17; Rom 1:20.

Secondary concepts: Copilot's eight were rewritten as ten in the texts' words.
- "Forming and Filling" was dropped because it is a framing of Genesis 1.
- "Blessing embedded in creation" became "Be fruitful and multiply".
- "Human relationality" became core concept 7.
- "Creation and wisdom" became "By wisdom → the earth founded".
- "Creation and covenant" became "Day and night" (Jer 33:25) and "The rainbow in the clouds". The card is named "the bow in the cloud", which is not the Berean's wording, so the Berean's wording is used.
- "Creation and judgment" (Gen 6–9) was cut to "The ground → cursed" (Gen 3:17–19; 5:29; 8:21).
- "Creation and redemption" became core concept 12.
- Added "The LORD's answer out of the whirlwind", "Formed from the dust → return to dust" and "The tree of life".

Links: Copilot's "Temple/Presence", "Kingdom" and "Eschatology" were dropped. Temple/Presence and Kingdom no longer have passages here, and Eschatology is not an allowed label. "Wisdom" became Wisdom Literature. Added Covenant (Gen 9:12–16), Law/Wisdom (Exod 20:11), Sin/Exile/Death (the ground cursed, return to dust) and Spirit (Ps 104:30; Job 34:14).

## After the review

- Concepts 1, 3, 4, 7, 10, 11, 12: accepted; no change.
- Concept 2, drop Genesis 1:9: **applied**. The verse gathers the waters "into one place" and names no boundary.
- Concept 5, drop Psalm 115:16: **applied**. It gives the earth to mankind and names no rule over creatures.
- Concept 6, drop Psalm 104:23: **applied**. It is about work in general, not the ground. Genesis 3:23 stays, with no framing.
- Concept 8, drop Mark 2:27 and Hebrews 4:9–10: **applied**. Neither says "the seventh day", and setting them beside Genesis 2 would make the grouping take a side on the Sabbath. Hebrews 4:4 stays because it quotes "on the seventh day".
- Concept 9, rename to "The life of every living thing is in His hand" (Job 12:10): **applied**. The Psalm 104:27–30 note is now Ps 104:28's "when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things", and Psalm 147:8–9 stays as an instance of the same provision.
- Links, "Law/Wisdom" and "Wisdom Literature" both used: **not applied**. Both are separate labels in the brief's allowed list. Law/Wisdom rests on the commandment (Exod 20:11), and Wisdom Literature on Proverbs 8, Job 38 and Psalm 104. The two links rest on different passages.
- Secondary "Be fruitful and multiply", drop Genesis 35:11: **applied**. Checking the others against bsb.txt found the arrow "fill the earth" was also wrong for Gen 1:22 ("fill the waters of the seas"), 8:17 and 9:7 ("spread out"). The arrow is now "God blessed them", which Gen 1:22, 1:28 and 9:1 each say, and 8:17, 9:7 and 35:11 are dropped.
- Secondary "The tree of life", cut Genesis 3:22–24 to 3:22 and 3:24: **applied**.
- Secondary "The whole creation has been groaning", keep as secondary: **applied**. It stays as it was and is not promoted.
- Missing "He stretched out the heavens": **not added**. The front page's table already has the row "He stretched out the heavens and spread out the earth", and also "The foundations of the earth".
- Missing "Let them praise the name of the LORD" (Ps 148:13): **added to section C** as "Let them praise the name of the LORD → heavens, sea, mountains and trees" (Ps 148:3–10; 96:11–12; 98:7–8; Isa 55:12). It is developed in four places and is not on the front page. Core is full at 12, so it went in C. To keep C at 10, "The LORD's answer out of the whirlwind → the foundations of the earth" (Job 38:1–7) was removed, because the front page already carries both that row and Job 38.
- Missing "In the beginning / before the mountains were born": **not added**. The front page's table has the row "In the beginning". Proverbs 8:22–26 and Psalm 90:2 ("Before the mountains were born") are left for that row, not added as a new sub-theme.

## Cap lifted

- "Let them praise the name of the LORD" was in section C only to keep the core at 12. It is now core concept 11, with its own passages, each quotation exact from bsb.txt: Ps 148:3–10; 148:13; 96:11–12; 98:7–8; Isa 55:12. "Through Him all things were made" and "A new heaven and a new earth" become 12 and 13. The C item "The LORD's answer out of the whirlwind → the foundations of the earth" (Job 38:1–7) had been removed only to make room, so it is restored. No other item sat in C because of the count.

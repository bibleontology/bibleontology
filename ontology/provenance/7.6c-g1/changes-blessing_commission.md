# Theme 3 (blessing_commission): changes to Copilot's outline

- **1. Divine Blessing as Empowerment**: renamed → "God blessed them". "As Empowerment" says more than the verses do, which say only that God blessed. Numbers 6 moved to "To pronounce blessings in His name". Psalm 67 dropped because the table already has its wording. Genesis 5:2, 24:1 and Job 42:12 added.
- **2. Fruitfulness & Multiplication**: renamed → "Fruitful and increased". The command (Gen 1:28; 9:1, 7) is already the table's row "Be fruitful and multiply", so the concept now holds the places that promise or record it (Gen 17:6; 26:22; 28:3; 47:27; Exod 1:7; Lev 26:9; Jer 23:3).
- **3. Filling the Earth**: dropped. Gen 1:28 and 9:1 are the table's row "Fill the earth". Isa 11:9 and Hab 2:14 say the knowledge of the LORD fills the earth, which is neither a blessing nor a charge. Copilot's "replenish" note is KJV wording, not the Berean's.
- **4. Subduing & Exercising Dominion**: renamed → "Ruler of the works of Your hands" (Ps 8:6). The Berean says "rule" and "ruler" in these verses, not "dominion". Genesis 9:2 added, and Psalm 8:6–8 kept with Psalm 115:16.
- **5. Humanity's Missional Purpose**: dropped. "Missional" frames the passages rather than naming what they say. Isa 43:7, Matt 5:16 and 1 Cor 10:31 speak of glory and say nothing of a blessing or a sending. Gen 2:15 moved to secondary ("to cultivate and keep it").
- **6. Blessing as Covenant Structure**: dropped as a name ("as ... Structure" frames). Its passages went to "All the families of the earth will be blessed through you" (Gen 12; 22; 26; 28) and to "A blessing and a curse" (Deut 28, cut to 28:2 and 28:15).
- **7. Blessing Mediated Through God's People**: dropped. It repeated concept 9's passages, which now stand under the card's name. The notes "channel of blessing" were glosses.
- **8. Blessing Linked to Obedience**: renamed → "A blessing and a curse" (Deut 11:26). Deut 28:1–14 cut to 28:2 and 28:15. Lev 26:3–4, Deut 11:26–28 and Josh 8:34 added. Psalm 1:1 moved to "Blessed is the man". Proverbs 3:1–10 dropped because it never says "bless".
- **9. Blessing Extending to All Nations**: renamed → "All the families of the earth will be blessed through you", the name of the site's card. Acts 3:25 kept with its own words, and the note "fulfilled in Christ" removed. Psalm 72:17 and Galatians 3:8 added in their own words. Isaiah 49:6 left to the table's row "A light for the nations".
- **10. Commission Renewed in Christ**: renamed → "Whom shall I send?" (Isa 6:8). "Renewed in Christ" frames one sending as the renewal of another. The concept now holds the sendings the texts record in the same words: Moses, Gideon, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, the disciples (John 20:21) and Paul (Acts 26:17). Matt 28:19, Luke 24:47 and Acts 1:8 were left to the table's rows. Mark 16:15 dropped because it sits in a passage whose place in the text is disputed. Matt 28:20 moved to "Do not be afraid, for I am with you".
- **Added: That I may bless you before I die** (Gen 27:4): the blessings fathers give before death, namely Isaac, Jacob and Moses, with Hebrews 11:20–21.
- **Added: To pronounce blessings in His name** (Deut 10:8): the priests' charge to bless (Num 6:23, 27; Lev 9:22–23; Deut 21:5; 1 Chr 23:13; 2 Chr 30:27). It adds to the table's "The LORD bless you" and does not repeat it.
- **Added: Blessed is the man**: the site's card. The form in Psalms, Proverbs, Jeremiah, Matthew 5 and James.
- **Added: The curse turned into a blessing** (Deut 23:5; Neh 13:2): Balaam (Num 22:12; 23:8, 20; 24:9; Josh 24:10).
- **Added: Bless those who curse you** (Luke 6:28): Rom 12:14; 1 Cor 4:12; 1 Pet 3:9.
- **Added: Do not be afraid, for I am with you**: the site's card. The promise given to those God sends or blesses (Gen 26:24; 28:15; Exod 3:12; Josh 1:9; Isa 41:10; Jer 1:8; Acts 18:9–10; Matt 28:20).

Secondary concepts were rebuilt from verses that say each thing in words (land, blessed on account of another, Jacob at the ford, Esau's lost blessing, blessing the LORD, the blessing over bread, the children, the garden, showers of blessing). Copilot's "Psalm 104" (a whole psalm) and "Ps 121" were not carried over. Linkages "Mission", "Nations", "Christology" and "Great Commission" are not on the allowed list. Kingdom, New Creation and Messiah/Christology were not linked because no passage here is about them. Priesthood, Law/Wisdom, Wisdom Literature, Prophetic Word & Fulfillment and Church were added where the passages overlap.

## After the review

- **Concept 1, name**: applied. Renamed "God blessed them" → "God blessed", because Gen 2:3, 24:1 and Job 42:12 do not say "them".
- **Concept 2, name**: applied. Renamed "Fruitful and increased" → "I will make you fruitful", because "increased" appears only in Gen 47:27 and Exod 1:7. Genesis 48:4 added, which has the exact words "I will make you fruitful and multiply you" (Gen 28:3 and Lev 26:9 have "make you fruitful").
- **Concept 3, drop Psalm 115:16**: applied. The verse says the earth is given to mankind, but nothing of ruling.
- **Concept 6, Leviticus 26:3–4**: applied by dropping it. Neither 26:3–4 nor 26:14–16 says "bless" or "curse", so extending the range would not make it hold the name. The Deuteronomy pair 28:2 and 28:15 already does.
- **Concept 11, name**: applied. Renamed "Do not be afraid, for I am with you" → "I am with you", because Gen 28:15, Exod 3:12 and Matt 28:20 have no word of fear. The name stays close to the site's card.
- **Concept 12, name**: applied. Renamed "Whom shall I send?" → "I am sending you", because the question is asked only in Isa 6:8. The name now matches the table's row, and the concept adds the sendings of Moses, Gideon, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel and Paul to it.
- **Secondary "the land", Gen 12:7**: applied. Replaced with Gen 12:1–2, where the land ("go to the land I will show you") and "I will bless you" stand together.
- **Secondary Gen 2:15**: applied. Dropped, because the verse says nothing of blessing or sending.
- **Concept 9, the Hebrew of Gen 22:18 and 26:4**: applied. Nothing changed: the name and notes are the Berean's own words, and no note settles the reading. No "also read" note was added, because a note here may only be an exact quotation from the Berean.
- **Link "Prophetic Word & Fulfillment"**: applied. It is kept only for the prophets' commissions (Isa 6:8; Jer 1:7; Ezek 2:3). No name or note says any passage is fulfilled.
- **Links "Law/Wisdom" and "Wisdom Literature"**: applied. Both are kept because they are separate labels on the allowed list. Law/Wisdom stands on Deut 11, 28 and 30, and Wisdom Literature on Ps 1, 32 and 112 and Prov 8:34.
- **Order, concept 9**: applied. "All the families of the earth will be blessed through you" moved to 4th place, after the rule of Ps 8 and before the fathers' blessings, so that Genesis 12 comes before Genesis 27 and Numbers 22.
- **Concept 7, Matthew 5:3**: not applied. The reviewer judged it acceptable, since "Blessed are the poor in spirit" has the same form as "Blessed is the man", which the texts repeat. The reviewer asked for no change.
- **Missing "I will bless you" (Gen 12:2; 22:17; 26:3)**: not added. It is already on the front page as the table's row "I will surely bless you and multiply you", and Gen 12:2 appears in section C under "the land".
- **Missing "Blessed be Abram by God Most High" (Gen 14:19–20; Heb 7:1, 6–7)**: placed in section C. It is developed in two places and is not on the front page, but a 13th core concept would exceed 12, and no current concept is the same thing, so none could be merged with it.
- **Missing "The cup of blessing / He lifted up his hands and blessed them"**: partly placed in section C. 1 Cor 10:16 joins the bread line as "the cup of blessing", and Ps 133:3 joins "showers of blessing" as "the blessing of life". Not added as a core concept because "He lifted up his hands and blessed them" (Luke 24:50) is already a table row, and the three passages do not form one sub-theme.

## Cap lifted

- **Promoted: "Blessed be Abram by God Most High"** (now concept 5, placed after the blessing of the families, so Genesis 14 comes before Genesis 27). Its passages are Gen 14:19, Gen 14:20, Heb 7:1 and Heb 7:6–7, each with an exact quotation. Its line was removed from section C.
- **Promoted: "Blessing over bread and cup"** (now concept 12). It holds the cup of blessing (1 Cor 10:16) together with the blessings spoken over bread (Matt 14:19; Luke 9:16; Matt 26:26; Mark 14:22; Luke 24:30). The plain label replaces "He spoke a blessing and broke it", which 1 Cor 10:16 does not say. Luke 9:16 and Mark 14:22 were added because they say it in so many words. 1 Sam 9:13 ("bless the sacrifice") and Mark 8:7 (fish) were left out because they bless neither bread nor cup. The notes quote only the words of blessing and nothing about what the bread or cup is. Its line was removed from section C.
- **Not promoted: Ps 133:3** stays in section C under "showers of blessing". It was placed there because it does not form a sub-theme with the cup or with Luke 24:50, not to keep the count down.
- **Not promoted: "I will bless you"**. It was kept out because the front page already has it (the table row "I will surely bless you and multiply you"), not because of the cap.

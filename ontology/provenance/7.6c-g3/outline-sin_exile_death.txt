B. Scriptural Support for Each Concept (Public‑Domain Translations)
1. Let no sin rule over me

* Psalm 19:13 (“Keep Your servant also from willful sins; may they not rule over me”)
* Psalm 119:133 (“let no sin rule over me”)
* John 8:34 (“everyone who sins is a slave to sin”)
* Romans 6:12 (“do not let sin reign in your mortal body”)
* Romans 6:16 (“slaves to sin leading to death”)

2. The serpent deceived me

* Genesis 3:1 (“Now the serpent was more crafty than any beast of the field”)
* Genesis 3:4–5 (“You will not surely die”)
* Genesis 3:6 (“she took the fruit and ate it. She also gave some to her husband who was with her, and he ate it”)
* Genesis 3:13 (“The serpent deceived me”)
* 2 Corinthians 11:3 (“Eve was deceived by the serpent's cunning”)
* Revelation 12:9 (“that ancient serpent called the devil and Satan, the deceiver of the whole world”)

3. Sin entered the world through one man

* Romans 5:12 (“just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin”)
* Romans 5:15 (“if the many died by the trespass of the one man”)
* Romans 5:19 (“through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners”)
* 1 Corinthians 15:21–22 (“For as in Adam all die, so in Christ all will be made alive”)

4. Iniquity, transgression, and sin

* Exodus 34:7 (“forgiving iniquity, transgression, and sin”)

* Isaiah 59:12 (“For our transgressions are multiplied before You, and our sins testify against us”)

* Romans 7:7 (“Is the law sin? Certainly not!”)
* Romans 7:12–13 (“so that through the commandment sin might become utterly sinful”)
* Galatians 3:19 (“It was added because of transgressions”)
* 1 John 3:4 (“Indeed, sin is lawlessness”)

6. They rebelled against Me

* Isaiah 1:2 (“I have raised children and brought them up, but they have rebelled against Me”)
* Isaiah 63:10 (“But they rebelled and grieved His Holy Spirit”)

* 1 Kings 11:6 (“So Solomon did evil in the sight of the LORD”)

* Sin → a sin offering (Lev 4:3; Lev 4:14; Lev 4:28)

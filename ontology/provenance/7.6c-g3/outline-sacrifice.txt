B. Scriptural Support for Each Concept (Public‑Domain Translations)
1. Abel's offering and Cain's

* Genesis 4:3–4 (“the LORD looked with favor on Abel and his offering”)
* Genesis 4:5 (“He had no regard for Cain and his offering”)
* Hebrews 11:4 (“By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain did”)

2. A pleasing aroma

* Genesis 8:20–21 (“When the LORD smelled the pleasing aroma”)
* Exodus 29:18 (“a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma”)
* Leviticus 1:9 (“a burnt offering, a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD”)
* Leviticus 2:2 (“a food offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD”)
* Numbers 28:2 (“as a pleasing aroma to Me”)
* Ephesians 5:2 (“a fragrant sacrificial offering to God”)
* Philippians 4:18 (“a fragrant offering, an acceptable sacrifice, well-pleasing to God”)

3. Abraham offered Isaac

* Hebrews 11:17–19 (“By faith Abraham, when he was tested, offered up Isaac”)
* James 2:21 (“when he offered his son Isaac on the altar”)

4. The Passover lamb

* Deuteronomy 16:2 (“the Passover sacrifice from the herd or flock”)

5. Without blemish

C. Secondary Ontological Concepts (Sub‑Nodes / Attributes)

* The regular burnt offering → in the morning and at twilight (Exod 29:38–42; Num 28:3–4)
* The altar → whatever touches it will be holy (Exod 29:37; Matt 23:19)
* The grain offering → the salt of the covenant (Lev 2:1–2; 2:13)
* Where to offer → in every place where I cause My name to be remembered; the place the LORD will choose (Exod 20:24; Deut 12:5–6; 12:13–14)
* David's burnt offerings → that cost me nothing (2 Sam 24:24–25; 1 Chr 21:24–26)
* Sacrifices of righteousness → offered (Deut 33:19; Ps 4:5; 51:19; Mal 3:3)
* Sacrificed to demons → not to God (Lev 17:7; Deut 32:17; Ps 106:37; 1 Cor 10:20)
* Food sacrificed to idols → eating it (Acts 15:29; 1 Cor 8:4–13; Rev 2:14)
* The ark → the mercy seat (Exod 25:22; Lev 16:2; Heb 9:5)
* With hyssop → the blood, the water (Exod 12:22; Num 19:18; Ps 51:7; Heb 9:19)

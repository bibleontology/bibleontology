# Spirit (theme 16): changes from Copilot's outline

Every passage and quotation was checked against the Berean by `tools/themes/outline_check.py`: 50 concepts, 10 secondary, 10 links, 0 failures. Every reference was found in bsb.txt.

Where traditions part on the Spirit, the outline sets the texts side by side and comments on none of them:
- **Baptism in or with the Spirit:** the Gospels and Acts 1:5 and 11:16 stand beside 1 Cor 12:13 ("in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body").
- **When the Spirit is received:** Acts 8:16 (after baptism, with hands laid on), Acts 10:44 and 10:47 (before baptism), Acts 2:38 (with repentance and baptism) and Acts 19:2, 6 sit together under "The Spirit fell upon them" and "The gift of the Holy Spirit".
- **Tongues:** 1 Cor 12:30 and 13:8 stand beside 14:5 and 14:39.
- **The procession of the Spirit:** both halves of John 15:26 are quoted.
- **Election and perseverance:** 2 Thess 2:13 and 1 Pet 1:2 are quoted only for the Spirit's words in them.

## Copilot's concepts

- **1. Spirit as Divine Presence & Power** — renamed and split. Presence became "Where can I go to escape Your Spirit?" (Ps 139:7, on the front page): Ps 139:7 kept, with Neh 9:20, Isa 63:11, 63:14 and Hag 2:5 added. Power became "By the power of the Spirit" (the table's row): Mic 3:8 and Luke 4:14 kept. Gen 1:2 moved to the creation concept. Copilot's notes were glosses ("omnipresence") and were replaced by quotations.
- **2. Spirit in Creation** — renamed → "When You send Your Spirit, they are created" (Ps 104:30's words). Gen 1:2, Job 33:4 and Ps 104:30 kept (Ps 104 cut to 104:29–30). Job 34:14–15 and Isa 40:13 added. Copilot's KJV-wording notes were replaced with the Berean.
- **3. Spirit in Revelation & Prophecy** — renamed by splitting into three concepts:
  - "The Spirit rested on them, and they prophesied" (Num 11:25's words): Num 11:25–29 cut to 11:25–26 and 11:29; 1 Sam 10, 19 and others added.
  - "The Spirit spoke through me" (2 Sam 23:2): 2 Sam 23:2 and 2 Pet 1:21 kept.
  - "The Spirit lifted me up" (the table's row). Ezek 2:2 was dropped; in it the Spirit "set me on my feet", which is Ezekiel's commissioning, not a carrying. Ezekiel's "lifted me up" verses were added in its place.
  - Joel 2:28–29 and Acts 2:16–18 moved to "I will pour out My Spirit", with Acts cut to 2:16–17. The note "Joel fulfilled" was dropped; Acts 2:16's own "this is what was spoken by the prophet Joel" is quoted instead.

  - Isa 32:15–18 cut to 32:15; Rom 8:18–23 cut to 8:23; Rev 22:17 kept.

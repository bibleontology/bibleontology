## Copilot's concepts

- **1. God's Mercy as Essential Divine Attribute**: renamed to "Compassionate and gracious, slow to anger". "Essential divine attribute" is a framing. The new name is the formula the texts repeat from Exodus to Jonah, and it is the front page's lead quotation and the site's card. Exod 34:6 is kept. Num 14:18, Neh 9:17, Ps 86:15, Ps 145:8, Isa 30:18 and Jonah 4:2 are added. Num 14:18 keeps "Yet He will by no means leave the guilty unpunished", so the formula's hard half is not cut. Ps 86:5 moved to "Rich in mercy". Ps 103:8–13 is cut to verses spread across concepts: 103:8 here, 103:9 under "Will He be angry forever?", 103:10 under "In wrath, remember mercy", 103:11 under "His loving devotion to those who fear Him", 103:12 under "As far as the east is from the west" and 103:13 under "As a father has compassion on his children". Lam 3:22–23 moved to "New every morning".
- **2. God's Compassion Toward the Repentant**: renamed to "Return to Me, and I will return to you" (the table's row; Zech 1:3, Mal 3:7). Ps 51:1–17 is cut to single verses: 51:1 under "Have mercy on me", 51:7 and 51:9 under "Blot out my transgressions", 51:10 under "I will give you a new heart", 51:12 under "Restore us, O God", and 51:17 under "A broken and contrite heart". Isa 55:6–7 is split: 55:7 here, 55:6 under "You will seek Me and find Me". Joel 2:12–14 is split: 2:12 here, 2:13 under "A broken and contrite heart", 2:14 under "He relents from sending disaster". Hos 14:1–4 is split: 14:1 here, 14:4 under "I will heal your faithlessness".
- **3. Forgiveness of Sin**: renamed to "With You there is forgiveness" (Ps 130:4). The site's "forgiveness" card applies. Exod 34:7 is cut to its forgiving clause in the note. Ps 32:1–5 is cut to 32:1, the "Blessed is the man" card (32:5 is under "If we confess our sins"). Ps 130:3–4 is kept. Mic 7:18–19 is split: 7:18 here, 7:19 under "As far as the east is from the west". Isa 1:18 moved to "Blot out my transgressions". Ps 85:2 and Jer 31:34 are added.
- **4. Restoration After Judgment**: dropped. No passage names that sequence. Its passages are kept under names they support: Isa 40:1–2 under "Comfort, comfort My people", Jer 30:17 under "He has wounded us, but He will bind up our wounds", and Ezek 36:33–36 cut to 36:35–36 under "Rebuild the ancient ruins". Hos 6:1–3 is cut to 6:1 (bind up our wounds) and 6:2 (under "Will You not revive us again?").
- **5. Return From Exile**: renamed and split into three of the table's rows and the texts' words:
  - "I will restore you from captivity" (Jer 29:14)
  - "I will gather you from the nations"
  - "They returned to Jerusalem and Judah" (Ezra 2:1). Ezra 1–2 is cut to Ezra 1:1, 1:5 and 2:1, with 2 Chr 36:23, Ezra 3:11, Neh 2:18 and Hag 1:14.

  Deut 30:1–6 is cut to 30:3, 30:4 and 30:6. Jer 29:10–14 is cut to 29:11, 29:13 and 29:14. Jer 31:8–10 is cut to 31:10. Ezek 34:11–16 is cut to 34:11 and 34:16, under "I will seek the lost".
- **6. Renewal of Covenant Relationship**: renamed to "I will remember My covenant" (Lev 26:42; Ezek 16:60; Ps 106:45 "He remembered His covenant with them, and relented"; Mic 7:20; Luke 1:54, 72). The passages tie mercy to remembering the covenant in so many words. Jer 31:31–34 is cut to 31:34, under "With You there is forgiveness" and "I will remember their sins no more". The new covenant itself stays with the Covenant outline. Ezek 37:26–28 is left to Covenant, since it has no word of mercy or restoring. Hos 2:19–23 is cut to 2:23, under "You are My people". Zech 8:7–8 moved to "I will gather you from the nations".
- **7. New Heart / New Spirit**: renamed to "I will give you a new heart", the site's card. Ezek 11:19–20 is cut to 11:19. Ezek 36:25–27 is cut to 36:26, and 36:27 is under "I will pour out My Spirit". Jer 24:7 and Ps 51:10 (the "create in me a clean heart" card) are kept. Ezek 18:31 and Deut 30:6 are added.
- **8. Healing and Rebuilding**: renamed and split into the table's rows:
  - "He has wounded us, but He will bind up our wounds"
  - "I will heal your faithlessness"
  - "Rebuild the ancient ruins"

  Isa 61:1–4 is split into 61:1 (liberty), 61:3 (mourning into joy) and 61:4 (rebuild). Jer 33:6–9 is cut to 33:6. Hos 14:4–7 is cut to 14:4. Amos 9:11–15 is split into 9:11 (the fallen tent of David), 9:14 (captivity) and 9:15 (plant).
- **9. Mercy Triumphing Over Judgment**: renamed to "In wrath, remember mercy" (Hab 3:2, the table's row). Jas 2:13 is kept: "Mercy triumphs over judgment" is the verse's own sentence, and so is "judgment without mercy". Ps 103:10 is kept. Exod 33:19 moved to its own concept, "I will have mercy on whom I have mercy" (the table's row). Mic 7:18 moved to "With You there is forgiveness".
- **10. Restoration Through the Messiah**: dropped. The name says the Servant passages are about the Messiah, and that is where Jewish and Christian readers part. Isa 42:1–7 and 49:5–13 are not used: they appoint a "Servant" whom traditions identify differently, and no line can quote them without seeming to. Isa 61:1 and Luke 4:18 are set side by side under "To proclaim liberty to the captives", with Lev 25:10, in their own words. Luke 4:21 ("fulfilled in your hearing") is left out. The concept does not need it, and the NT outlines carry it. Acts 3:19–21 is split into 3:19 (blot out) and 3:21 ("the restoration of all things", under "I make all things new").
- **11. Restoration Through the Spirit**: renamed to "I will pour out My Spirit", the texts' own phrase (Isa 44:3; Ezek 39:29; Joel 2:28; Acts 2:17). Ezek 36:25–27 is cut to 36:27. Ezek 37:1–14 is cut to 37:3, 37:11–12 and 37:14, under "Will You not revive us again?". Joel 2:28–32 is cut to 2:28. Acts 2:38–39 is dropped. It ties baptism to forgiveness and the gift of the Spirit, which is where traditions part. Acts 2:17 says the outpouring in Peter's words instead.
- **12. Restoration Anticipating New Creation**: renamed to "I make all things new" (Rev 21:5). "Anticipating" frames the Old Testament as pointing ahead. The cuts:
  - Isa 65:17–25 is cut to 65:17.
  - Isa 66:22 is dropped. It repeats 65:17, and the concept is at eight.
  - Rom 8:18–23 is cut to 8:21 (the "whole creation has been groaning" card).
  - Rev 21:1–5 is cut to 21:4–5.
  - Rev 22:1–5 is cut to 22:2 ("the healing of the nations", the "tree of life" card).
  - Isa 43:19, Matt 19:28 and Acts 3:21 are added.

# Temple / Presence (theme 9): changes from Copilot's outline

I looked up all 257 passages and every quotation in bsb.txt. outline_check.py reports 37 concepts, 10 secondary, 10 links and 0 failures, and none was missing from the Berean. The site's parser read all 257 passages, 10 secondary concepts and 10 links, so no line was dropped.

## Copilot's concepts

- **1. God's Presence as Central Biblical Reality**: renamed → "My Presence will go with you" (Exod 33:14). "Central Biblical Reality" is a claim about the whole Bible that no passage makes. Exod 33:14–16 is cut to 33:14 and 33:15. Lev 26:11–12 moved to "I will dwell among them" and "I will walk among you", Ps 16:11 to "Joy in Your presence", and Ps 27:4 to "To dwell in the house of the LORD". Added: Deut 4:37, Isa 63:9, and the "I am with you" passages Gen 28:15, Deut 31:8, Josh 1:9 and Isa 43:2.
- **2. Eden as Proto‑Temple**: renamed → "Eden, the garden of God" (Ezek 28:13). "Proto-temple" is an interpretation, and no passage calls Eden a temple. Gen 2:8–9 is kept as 2:8 and 2:9, Gen 3:24 is kept, and Ezek 28:13–14 is kept as two lines, quoted without the "sanctuary imagery" gloss. Gen 3:8 moved to "I will walk among you". Added: Ezek 31:9, Isa 51:3, and Rev 2:7 ("the tree of life in the Paradise of God").
- **3. Sacred Space (Holy / Clean / Common)**: renamed and split. It becomes "The place where you are standing is holy" (the site's card: Exod 3:5, Josh 5:15, Acts 7:33, plus Exod 19:23 and 2 Pet 1:18) and "Between the holy and the common" (the table's row: Lev 10:10, Ezek 22:26, 42:20 and 44:23). Leviticus 11–15 was a five-chapter range. It is cut to Lev 15:31 ("by defiling My tabernacle, which is among them"), with Num 5:3 added.
- **4. Tabernacle as Mobile Sanctuary**: renamed → "The pattern shown you on the mountain" (the table's row: Exod 25:9, 25:40, 26:30, Num 8:4, Acts 7:44, Heb 8:5). "Mobile sanctuary" is a gloss. Exod 25:8 and 29:45–46 moved to "I will dwell among them", and Exod 40:34–38 to "The glory of the LORD filled the house" and to the pillar concept. Num 1:50–53 is dropped: it is about who carries and guards the tabernacle, and it does not say "central to camp". Num 5:3 ("their camp, where I dwell among them") now carries the camp, under "Between the holy and the common".
- **5. Priestly Mediation of Presence**: renamed → "To stand before the LORD to serve Him" (Deut 10:8). "Mediation of presence" is a framing. Exodus 28–29 is cut to 28:12, 28:29 and 28:35 (the ephod and the breastpiece of judgment, both site cards). Leviticus 16 is cut to 16:2, under "The veil", with 16:34 in section C. Num 18:1–7 is cut to 18:7, also under "The veil". Hebrews 5:1 is dropped because its "on behalf of men" belongs to the Priesthood outline, and Heb 9:6 stands in its place. Added: 2 Chr 29:11, Ezek 44:16 and Luke 1:9.
- **6. Temple as Permanent Dwelling**: renamed → "A dwelling for His Name" (Deut 12:5; the table's two rows on the Name). "Permanent" overstates what these passages say: the same chapters tell of Shiloh and of the house that bears the Name becoming like Shiloh. The 1 Kings 6–8 range is cut. 1 Kgs 8:10–11 moved to "The glory of the LORD filled the house", 8:27 to "Will God indeed dwell upon the earth?", and 8:30 and 8:42–43 to "A house of prayer for all the nations". 2 Chr 7:1–3 is cut to 7:1, under the glory. Ps 132:13–14 moved to "The LORD who dwells in Zion" and "Arise, O LORD, to Your resting place". Kept here: 2 Sam 7:13, 1 Kgs 9:3, 2 Chr 7:16, Jer 7:11 and Ps 74:7, with Deut 12:5 and 12:11.
- **7. God's Glory Filling Sacred Space**: renamed → "The glory of the LORD filled the house" (1 Kgs 8:11, the front page's quotation). Exod 40:34–35, 1 Kgs 8:10–11 and Ezek 43:4–5 are kept, each as single verses. Ezek 43:1–3 is cut to 43:2, which moved to "The whole earth filled with His glory". Rev 21:23 moved to "I saw no temple in the city". Added: 2 Chr 7:1 and Ezek 44:4.
- **8. Presence Lost Through Sin**: renamed and split, so that the hard words stand as sub-themes of their own:
  - "Banished from His presence" takes the texts' own verb (2 Kgs 24:20). Gen 3:23 is kept here and Gen 3:24 moved to "Eden, the garden of God". Added: Gen 4:16, 2 Kgs 17:18, Jer 7:15, Ps 51:11 and Isa 59:2.
  - "The glory has departed" is the table's row, from 1 Sam 4:21. Ezek 10:18–19 and 11:22–23 are cut to 10:18 and 11:23. Added: Shiloh (Ps 78:60; Jer 7:12, 14), Ezek 8:6 and Lam 2:7.
  - Hos 5:15 moved to "Seek His face", because it ends "until they ... seek My face". Its "withdraws until repentance" was a gloss.
- **9. Presence Restored Through Covenant**: dropped as a name. "Restored through covenant" joins two themes in a way these passages do not state, and Copilot's note "presence tied to obedience" was a gloss. Exod 25:8 and Lev 26:11–12 are under "I will dwell among them" and "I will walk among you". Ezek 37:26–28 is cut to 37:26–27, and Hag 2:4–9 to 2:9. Both are under the closing concept, and Hag 2:5 is under "I will dwell among them".
- **10. Christ as True Temple**: renamed → "The temple of His body" (John 2:21; the table's row). "True temple" says the earlier temple was not the true one, and that is where traditions, and Jewish readers, part. John 2:19–21 is cut to 2:19 and 2:21, and Matt 12:6 is kept. John 1:14 and Col 2:9 moved to a new concept, "God with us". Added: Matt 26:61, Mark 14:58 and Mark 15:29, the other places where the saying about three days is reported.
- **11. Church as Temple of the Spirit**: renamed → "You are God's temple" (1 Cor 3:16, the front page's quotation). "Church" is not in these verses, and 1 Cor 6:19 speaks of one person's body. 1 Cor 3:16–17, 6:19, Eph 2:21–22 and 1 Pet 2:5 are kept as single verses. Added: 2 Cor 6:16 ("For we are the temple of the living God") and Rev 3:12.
- **12. New Creation as Ultimate Temple**: renamed and split.
  - "I saw no temple in the city" is the front page's Rev 21:22. "Ultimate temple" says more than the verse does: John says he saw *no* temple. It keeps Rev 21:22, Rev 21:23 (moved from Copilot's 7) and Isa 60:19, cut from 60:19–20. Rev 22:1–5 is cut to 22:1–2 and 22:5. Copilot's "Eden restored" gloss is gone. Rev 21:2 is added (the site's card "new Jerusalem, prepared as a bride").
  - "The dwelling place of God is with man" is from Rev 21:3, and it is the closing concept (see below).

## Sections C and D

- **C** was rebuilt with every reference cut to its verses. Presence → Blessing (Num 6:24–26) is kept, and Holiness is kept with Lev 19:2, adding 1 Pet 1:16. New: the ark and the mercy seat, the altar of incense, the basin, the anointing oil (all site cards), Atonement once a year (the day of atonement card), a voice from the cloud (a table row), zeal for Your house (a table row), and worship in spirit and in truth (John 4:21–23).
  - Copilot's Covenant, Worship (Ps 27:4), Glory, Priesthood, Judgment, Christology, Spirit and New Creation are dropped from C. Each is now a core concept or a link in D, and their references were chapter ranges.
  - Sacrifice (Lev 1–7) is dropped as well: it is a seven-chapter range, and it is the Sacrifice outline's subject.
- **D**: kept Creation (Eden), Covenant (Lev 26:11–12; Ezek 37:26–27), Sacrifice, Priesthood, Spirit, Church and New Creation. Christology became Messiah/Christology, the label on the list. Added Sin/Exile/Death ("Banished from His presence") and Judgment ("The glory has departed", Shiloh).
  - Dropped: Image of God, Blessing & Commission, Kingdom and Law/Wisdom. Their overlap with these passages is thin, and dropping them keeps the links at ten.

Counts (of Copilot's 12): kept 0, renamed 11 (three of them split into two concepts each), dropped 1 (9, its passages kept elsewhere); added 23; 37 core concepts.

## After the review

### Concept points

- **1. Drop Hag 2:5**: applied. It is not in concept 3, which is already at eight passages. It moved instead, as Haggai 2:4–5, to the new rebuilding concept (34), where it stands beside 2:4's "For I am with you", as the reviewer suggested.
- **6. Drop John 1:51**: applied. It moved to section C, as the reviewer suggested, but under a different label. In the Berean, Gen 28:12 says "going up and down" and John 1:51 says "ascending and descending", so the two verses do not share words. The line names both wordings: "The angels of God → going up and down; ascending and descending".
- **7. Drop 2 Pet 1:18**: applied. It stays in section C under the voice from the cloud.
- **9. Acts 7:44 note, and the optional 1 Chr 28:19**: both applied. The note is now "It was constructed exactly as God had directed Moses, according to the pattern he had seen". 1 Chr 28:19 is added, quoting "all the details of this plan, the LORD has made clear to me in writing by His hand upon me".
- **11. Drop Heb 9:5**: applied. Section C still holds Heb 9:4–5 under the ark and the mercy seat.
- **28. Rename to "My house will be called a house of prayer"**: applied. The name now holds every passage under it.
- **33. Rename to "The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us"; drop Matt 18:20, 28:20 and John 14:23; fix the Matt 1:23 note**: applied.
  - Matt 18:20 and 28:20 now form the new concept "I am with you always".
  - The Matt 1:23 note now quotes the verse exactly: “they will call Him Immanuel” (which means, “God with us”).
  - John 14:23 is in no concept now; the next point explains why.
- **35. Drop Rev 3:12; perhaps add John 14:23**: applied in part.
  - Rev 3:12 is dropped. It was not moved to concept 24: that concept is at eight passages, and the verse does not say the LORD is in His temple.
  - John 14:23 is not added. It says "make Our home with him" and not "temple", so it would carry the same fault the review finds in Rev 3:12, a name that says more than the passage.
- **34. Rename to "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up again"; drop Matt 12:6**: applied. John 2:21 stays as the Gospel's own comment. Matt 12:6 moved to section C (see the missing sub-themes below).
- **36. Drop Isa 60:19**: applied. The concept keeps Rev 21:2, 21:22, 21:23, 22:1–2 and 22:5.
- **37. Rename to "My dwelling place will be with them" (Ezek 37:27)**: applied. Hag 2:9 stays, quoted only for its own words, "The latter glory of this house will be greater than the former". It is still the closing concept, the theme's own words of hope, and the dark sub-themes still stand before it.

### Section C, links and order

- **Order**: applied. No connecting words were added. The new rebuilding concept (34) now stands between "The glory has departed" and the New Testament concepts, so the outline keeps the canon's order and no name answers another.
- **The ladder line**: applied, labelled as described under concept 6.
- **Worship in spirit and in truth**: kept as it was, citing John 4:21–23 in full (applied; nothing to change).
- **A voice from the cloud**: kept as it was (accepted).
- **Links to Israel, Land or Water**: not applied. None of these is a label on the list of themes the outline may link to.
- **Two C lines dropped**: section C is at its ceiling of ten, so two lines gave way for the ladder and Matt 12:6.
  - "Holiness → Be holy" went because Lev 19:2 and 1 Pet 1:16 say nothing of the sanctuary, and the holiness of the place is carried by core concepts 7, 8 and 16.
  - "The anointing oil → the tabernacle anointed" went because the consecration in Exod 30 and 40 is the Priesthood outline's subject.

### Missing sub-themes

- **Not one stone will be left on another**: added as concept 38, with Matt 24:2, Mark 13:2, Luke 21:6 and Luke 19:44, quoted without dating or interpretation.
  - 1 Kgs 9:7 ("I will banish from My presence this temple I have sanctified for My Name") was added to "Banished from His presence", where its own verb belongs.
  - 1 Kgs 9:8 and Jer 26:6 are not set under the Gospel's name. There they would read as the first house's end being the second's, or the reverse. Shiloh and Jer 7:14 already carry that warning under "The glory has departed".
- **Water flowing from beneath the threshold of the temple**: added as concept 31, "The water from the sanctuary" (Ezek 47:12's words). Ezek 47:1 says the water flowed "from under the threshold of the temple". The passages are Ezek 47:1, 47:9 and 47:12, Joel 3:18 ("a spring will flow from the house of the LORD") and Ps 46:4.
  - Zech 14:8 is left out: its water flows "from Jerusalem", not from the house.
  - Rev 22:1–2 is not set under it. Its river flows "from the throne of God and of the Lamb", and placing it there would read Revelation as Ezekiel's river, the Israel-and-the-church question the review raised at concept 37. It stays in "I saw no temple in the city".
- **Who is left among you who saw this house in its former glory?**: added as concept 34, with Ezra 3:11, Ezra 3:12 (the weeping and the shouting), Hag 1:8, Hag 2:3, Hag 2:4–5 and Ezra 6:16. Ezra 3:10–13 and 6:14–16 are cut to the verses that carry it.
- **I am with you always**: added as concept 36, with Matt 28:20, Matt 18:20 and Acts 18:10.
- **One greater than the temple is here**: not added as a core concept. Only Matt 12:6 says it. John 4:21–23 does not say "greater", and Acts 6:13–14 reports false witnesses. Matt 12:6 is now a section C line, "The temple → One greater than the temple", which the review allowed for. John 4:21–23 stays in C as before.

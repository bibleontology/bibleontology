# Judgment (theme 11): changes from Copilot's outline

57 core concepts and 385 passages. Every reference was looked up in bsb.txt and every note copied from it. `outline_check.py` reports 0 failures. Its one failure on the first run was a note that misquoted Mark 9:43 ("to go into hell" where the Berean has "and go into hell"), and that note is now fixed. Every concept has 3 to 8 passages.

Section 1 of the packet shows 11 Copilot concepts. The machine check in section 2 also names a 12th and 13th ("Judgment of Wicked Powers & Nations", "Resurrection & Final Judgment"), but their text is not in the packet. They are accounted for from their names and the references the check quotes.

## Copilot's concepts

- **1. God as Judge of All the Earth**: kept, in Gen 18:25's own words: "The Judge of all the earth". Gen 18:25, Ps 7:11, Isa 33:22 and Heb 12:23 are kept. Judg 11:27, Ps 50:6, 75:7 and 94:2 are added. Ps 9:7–8 moved to "He will judge the world in righteousness". Copilot's note "angry with the wicked" is not in the Berean's Ps 7:11.
- **2. Judgment as Expression of Divine Holiness**: renamed to "I will show My holiness" (Lev 10:3), because "expression of" frames the passages. Lev 10:1–3 is cut to 10:2–3. Isa 6:3–5 is cut to 6:3 and 6:5, and Hab 1:13 is kept. Isa 5:16, Ezek 28:22 and Ezek 38:23 are added because they say "holy" or "holiness" alongside judgment. Rom 1:18 moved to "The wrath of God".
- **3. Judgment as Covenant Enforcement**: renamed to "All these curses will come upon you" (Deut 28:15), because "enforcement" is a framing. Lev 26:14–39 is cut to 26:14–16 and 26:25. Deut 28:15–68 is cut to 28:15. Deut 29:27, 2 Chr 34:24, Jer 11:8 and Dan 9:11 are added. Deut 31:16–18 became its own concept, "I will hide My face". 2 Kgs 17:7–23 is cut to 17:23 (under "I will scatter you") and 17:7 (section C).
- **4. Judgment Against Idolatry & Injustice**: renamed by splitting it in two.
  - **"A jealous God"** takes the idolatry half: Exod 20:5 and 34:14, Deut 6:15, Josh 24:19, Nah 1:2 and Zeph 1:18. Each of these verses says "jealous".
  - **"Judgment on injustice"**, a plain label, takes the other half. Isa 1:21–31 is cut to 1:25 and 1:27, both under "Like a refiner's fire". Ezek 22:23–31 is cut to 22:29. Isa 10:1–2, Jer 22:13, Amos 2:6, Amos 5:11–12, Mic 3:9–12, Mal 3:5 and Jas 5:4 are added.
  - **Dropped from it:** Jer 2:11–13 (no judgment is pronounced in those verses; Jer 1:16 now carries "forsaken Me ... other gods" in section C) and Amos 5:21–24 (5:24 is a call to justice, not a judgment).
- **5. Judgment Through Historical Events**: renamed by splitting it into the events, each named as the texts or the site's cards name it.
  - **"The flood"**: Gen 6–9 is cut to 6:13, 6:17, 7:23 and 9:11.
  - **"The plagues of Egypt"**, the site's card: Exod 7–12 is cut to 6:6, 7:4, 9:14, 12:12, 12:13 and 12:29, with Num 33:4 and Ps 105:36. This brings in the cards for the death of the firstborn and the blood upon the lintel.
  - **"They shall never enter My rest"**: Num 14:22–23 is kept, and Ps 95:11 and Heb 3:17–18 are added.
  - **"I will scatter you among the nations"**: 2 Kgs 17 and 2 Kgs 25 are cut to 17:23 and 25:21.
  - **Babel** (Gen 11:1–9) is cut to 11:8–9 and moved to section C. No verse there says judgment, and the scattering is told once.
- **6. Judgment Through Exile**: renamed to "I will scatter you among the nations" (Lev 26:33; Deut 28:64), the texts' recurring words. Lev 26:33–39 is cut to 26:33, Deut 28:36, 64–68 to 28:64, and Ezek 12:15–20 to 12:15. Jer 25:11 moved to section C ("Seventy years").
- **7. Judgment Through Death**: renamed to "You will surely die". The phrase occurs in Gen 2:17 and again in Ezek 3:18 and 33:8. Gen 2:17, Gen 3:19, Rom 5:12 and Rom 6:23 are kept. Heb 9:27 moved to "The day of judgment", because its words are "after that to face judgment".
- **8. Judgment Through Cosmic Catastrophe**: renamed to "The sun will be darkened" (Isa 13:10; Matt 24:29), because "cosmic catastrophe" is a gloss. Isa 13:9–13 is cut to 13:10, Joel 2:1–11 to 2:10, and Rev 6:12–17 to 6:12 (6:16–17 went to "The day of wrath"). Amos 8:9 and Matt 24:29 are kept. Isa 34:4, Joel 2:31 and Acts 2:20 are added.
- **9. Day of the LORD (Comprehensive Judgment)**: kept as "The Day of the LORD", with the parenthesis dropped. The ranges are cut to their verses:
  - Isa 2:12–21 to 2:12. Isa 13:6–13 went to "The sun will be darkened" (13:10).
  - Joel 2:1–11 to 2:11. Joel 3:14 went to "All the nations gathered for judgment" (via 3:2 and 3:12).
  - Amos 5:18–20 to 5:18. Zeph 1:14–18 to 1:14 (1:15 went to "The day of wrath", 1:18 to "A jealous God").
  - Mal 4:1–5 to 4:5 (4:1 went to "Like chaff driven off by the wind").
  - Ezek 30:3 and Joel 1:15 are kept, and 1 Thess 5:2 is added.
- **10. Final Judgment**: renamed by splitting it into the texts' own phrases. "Final" is not the texts' word, and the order of the last things is where traditions part. Nothing in the outline sets the concepts in a sequence. The parts:
  - **"The day of judgment"**: Heb 9:27, Matt 11:22, Acts 17:31, 2 Pet 2:9 and 3:7, 1 John 4:17, Jude 6.
  - **"We will all stand before God's judgment seat"**: Rom 14:10, from the front page, with 2 Cor 5:10.
  - **"The sheep and the goats"**: the site's card. Matt 25:31–46 is cut to 25:32–34, 40, 41, 45 and 46, beside Ezek 34:17 and 34:20.
  - **"A resurrection of both the righteous and the wicked"**: Dan 12:2 and John 5:28–29.
  - **"And books were opened"**: Rev 20:11–15 is cut to 20:12 and 20:15, with 20:13 under the resurrection concept.
  - **Romans 2:5–11** is cut to 2:5 ("The day of wrath") and 2:6 ("Repay each one according to his deeds"). Heb 9:27 is not repeated.
- **11. Judgment as Pathway to Restoration**: renamed to "Like a refiner's fire" (Mal 3:2), because "pathway" frames the passages. Isa 4:2–4 is cut to 4:4, and Isa 1:25, 1:27, 48:10, Zech 13:9 and Mal 3:2–3 are added. Its other passages moved:
  - Hos 6:1–3, cut to 6:1, to "Return to Me".
  - Mic 7:8–9, cut to 7:9, to the closing concept.
  - Isa 40:1–2 and Ezek 36:24–28 (cut to 36:24 and 36:26) to section C.
- **12. Judgment of Wicked Powers & Nations** (named only in the machine check): renamed by splitting it three ways:
  - **"Against the nations"**, a plain label: Isa 13:1, 19:1, Jer 46:1, Ezek 25:7, Amos 1:3, 1:13 and Nah 1:1. These are the openings of the oracles, so the 20+ verse runs Isa 13–23 and Ezek 25–32 are cut to the verses that say "against" or "about the nations".
  - **"Fallen, fallen is Babylon"**: Isa 21:9, Jer 51:8 and 51:49, Rev 14:8, 18:2 and 18:10. Revelation 18 is cut to two verses, and the outline does not say whom Babylon names.
  - **"All the nations gathered for judgment"**: the table's row.
- **13. Resurrection & Final Judgment** (named only in the machine check): renamed to "A resurrection of both the righteous and the wicked" (Acts 24:15).

  - **Where the texts develop it:** in the Torah and again in Kings. The passages are Gen 15:16, Lev 18:24–25, Lev 18:28 ("it will vomit you out as it spewed out the nations before you"), Lev 20:23, Deut 9:4–5, Deut 18:12 and 2 Kgs 21:2.
  - **Reviewer's quotes corrected:** it quoted Deut 9:4–5 as "it is because of the wickedness of these nations that the LORD is driving them out". The Berean reads "it is because of their wickedness that the LORD your God is driving out these nations before you" (9:5), and the note quotes that.
  - **Not already in the outline:** it was not one of the concepts under any name. Deut 9:3 stands in "A consuming fire".
- **God gave them over** (concept 11), named from Rom 1:24. It is developed in the Psalms, Acts and Romans: Ps 81:11–12, Acts 7:42, Rom 1:24, Rom 1:26 and Rom 1:28.
  - **Reviewer's quotes corrected:**
    - Ps 81:12 reads "So I gave them up to their stubborn hearts", not "gave them over".
    - Rom 1:28 reads "He gave them up to a depraved mind".
    - Acts 7:42 reads "God turned away from them and gave them over"; the reviewer's quote dropped "from them".
  - **Not already in the outline:** "I will hide My face" is a different act, as the reviewer said.
  - **Left out:**
    - The hardening texts, as the reviewer advised.
    - Ezek 20:25 ("I also gave them over to statutes that were not good"), although it uses the words: what those statutes are is read very differently, and the review did not raise it.

C. Secondary Ontological Concepts (Sub‑Nodes / Attributes)

* Forsaken → My God, My God (Ps 22:1; Matt 27:46; Mark 15:34)
* Garments → divided, lots cast (Ps 22:18; John 19:24)
* Pierced → they will look on Him (Zech 12:10; John 19:37; Rev 1:7)
* Ransom → for many, for all (Mark 10:45; Matt 20:28; 1 Tim 2:6)
* Intercession → for the transgressors, for us (Isa 53:12; Rom 8:34; Heb 7:25)
* Lion → of Judah (Gen 49:9; Rev 5:5)
* Winepress → trodden (Isa 63:3; Rev 19:15)
* Melchizedek → king of Salem, priest of God Most High (Gen 14:18; Heb 7:1)
* Servant → His servant Jesus (Acts 3:13; Acts 3:26; Acts 4:27)
* Shepherd → the great Shepherd, the Chief Shepherd (Heb 13:20; 1 Pet 5:4)

# Theme 14, Prophetic Word & Fulfillment: changes to Copilot's outline

## Copilot's 13 concepts

1. God Speaks Through Prophets: **renamed** → "I have put My words in your mouth" (Jer 1:9, Isa 51:16). The texts' own phrase. Exod 4:15–16 became 4:12. Num 12:6–8 and Deut 18:18–19 moved to "Visions and dreams" and "A prophet like me from among your brothers", and 2 Kgs 17:13 to "My servants the prophets". Heb 1:1 kept. 2 Sam 23:2, Jer 20:9, Amos 3:8 and 2 Pet 1:21 added.
2. Prophetic Revelation as Covenant Enforcement: **renamed** → "My servants the prophets". "Covenant enforcement" is a framing. The texts say God sent "My servants the prophets" with "Turn from your wicked ways and keep My commandments" (2 Kgs 17:13; Jer 7:25; 35:15; Zech 1:6). Deut 28–30 dropped, because no prophet speaks there and the Covenant theme holds it. Jer 11:1–8 was left to Covenant. Hos 4:1–6 became Hos 4:1 under the added "The LORD has a case against His people".
3. Prophetic Call to Repentance: **renamed** → "Return to Me" (Zech 1:3; Mal 3:7). Isa 1:16–20 was cut to 1:16–17, with 1:18 moved to "a case". Jer 3:12–14 became 3:12, Ezek 18:30–32 became 18:32 and Joel 2:12–13 was kept. Isa 55:7 and Hos 14:1 added.
4. Prophetic Warning of Judgment: **renamed** → "I have made you a watchman" (Ezek 3:17; 33:6; Jer 6:17; Hos 9:8; Hab 2:1). These are the texts' own words for the prophet's duty to warn. Mic 3:8 kept, and Mic 3:12 appears through Jer 26:18 under "The former prophets". Isa 5, Jer 7:1–15 and Ezek 7 were whole chapters or runs: Jer 7 became 7:25, and Ezek 7's "the end has come" is in Amos 8:2 under "What do you see?". Amos 5:18–20 (the Day of the LORD) was left to Judgment and Apocalyptic.
5. Prophetic Promise of Restoration: **renamed** → "A future and a hope" (Jer 29:11), placed last as the theme's own words of hope. Isa 40:1–11 became 40:1 and Hos 14:1–7 became 14:4. Jer 30–33 was cut to 31:17, and its new covenant became a concept of its own. Ezek 36–37 became 37:14, with 36:26 under the new covenant. Amos 9:11–15 moved to "The throne of David" as 9:11. Joel 2:25, Zech 9:12 and Rev 21:5 were added.
6. Prophetic Prediction (Foretelling): **dropped**. "Predict" is a word the site does not say, and calling OT verses predictions takes a side. Isa 9:6–7 and Mic 5:2 went to "The throne of David". Isa 7:14 appears only where Matthew quotes it in his own words (Matt 1:22). Isa 53 appears as 53:1, "Who has believed our message?", under "Be ever hearing". Daniel 2 and 7 were cut to 2:19 and 7:1 under "Visions and dreams".
7. Prophetic Interpretation (Forthtelling): **renamed** → "You are that man!" (2 Sam 12:7; it is a card). Prophets confronting kings, in their own words: Samuel, Elijah, Micaiah, Hanani, and John to Herod. 2 Sam 12:1–14 was cut to 12:7. Isa 58 went to "I desire mercy, not sacrifice" as 58:6, and Jer 2–3 became 2:9 and 3:12 elsewhere. Ezek 16 and 23 were dropped: they are whole chapters, and "interpretive parables" is a gloss.
8. Prophetic Validation (True vs. False Prophets): **renamed** → "I did not send these prophets" (Jer 23:21). Deut 13:1–5 became 13:1–3, and Deut 18:20–22 became 18:22. Jer 23:16–32 became 23:21, and Ezek 13 became 13:6. Jer 28:9, Mic 3:5, Matt 7:15 and 1 John 4:1 were added.
9. Prophetic Word as Certain and Unbreakable: **renamed** → "The word of our God stands forever" (Isa 40:8, quoted in 1 Pet 1:25). Isa 55:10–11 became 55:11, and Jer 1:12 and Ezek 12:25–28 (as 12:28) were kept. Matt 5:18 moved, as 5:17, to "Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets". Num 23:19, Josh 21:45 and Hab 2:3 were added.
10. Messianic Prophecy: **dropped**. In the site's voice it names OT passages as about the Messiah. Gen 3:15, Gen 49:10, Ps 2, Ps 110 and Dan 7:13–14 were left to the Messiah theme. 2 Sam 7:12–16 (as 7:16) and Isa 11:1 were kept under "The throne of David", side by side with Luke 1:32 and Acts 2:30, which speak in their own words.
11. Fulfillment in Jesus Christ: **renamed** → "To fulfill what was spoken through the prophet" (Matt 4:14; 21:4). This is the Gospels' own formula, and only NT verses that say it are cited, each quoted. Matt 1:22–23, 2:5–6 and 4:14–16 were cut to 1:22, 2:15, 2:17, 4:14, 8:17 and 21:4. John 12:38–41 became 12:38, and John 19:24 was added. Luke 4:18–21 (as 4:21) and Acts 3:18–24 (as 3:24) moved to "Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets". Heb 10:5–10 was dropped, because Psalm 40 is not a prophet's word there and Sacrifice and Messiah hold it.
12. Prophetic Word Continued Through Apostles: **renamed** → "Apostles and prophets" (Eph 2:20; 1 Cor 12:28; 2 Pet 3:2). "Continued through" is a framing. John 14:26 and 16:13 were dropped, because they do not speak of prophets and the Spirit theme holds them. Acts 2:16–21 became its own concept, "I will pour out My Spirit on all people". Acts 11:27, Acts 13:1, 1 Cor 14:3, 14:29 and 1 Thess 5:20–21 were added, and each is the text's words only, taking no side on whether prophecy continues.
13. Prophetic Word Completed and Confirmed: **dropped**. "Completed" and Heb 1:2's "final word" framing claim more than the passages. Luke 24:25–27 (as 24:27) went to "Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets", and Heb 1:1 to concept 1. Acts 13:32–33 and 2 Cor 1:20 were dropped: they speak of promises, not prophets. Rev 22:6 was replaced by Rev 21:5, "these words are faithful and true", in the last concept.

## Concepts added (17)

- "The word of the LORD came": the opening formula of the prophetic books and narratives (1 Sam 15:10; Jer 1:4; Ezek 1:3; Hos 1:1; Joel 1:1; Jonah 1:1; Zech 1:1; Luke 3:2).
- "Here am I. Send me!": the call and sending (Isa 6:8; Jer 1:5, 7; Ezek 2:3; Amos 7:15; Exod 3:10; 1 Sam 3:10; Gal 1:15). It is a card and a front-page quotation.
- "A prophet like me from among your brothers": Deut 18:15, 18 and 34:10, beside John 1:21, John 6:14 and Acts 3:22 and 7:37, which ask or say it in their own words. It is a card.
- "Visions and dreams": Num 12:6, Isa 6:1, Ezek 1:1, Dan 2:19 and 7:1, Hab 2:2 and Jer 23:28.
- "What do you see?": the question put to Jeremiah, Amos and Zechariah (Jer 1:11, 13; 24:3; Amos 7:8; 8:2; Zech 4:2; 5:2). It holds the card "a plumb line among My people".
- "I have made you a sign to the house of Israel": sign-acts (Isa 8:18; 20:3; Jer 13:1; 19:10–11; Ezek 4:3; 12:6; 24:24; Acts 21:11, Agabus and the belt card).
- "The LORD has a case against His people": Isa 1:18; 3:13; Jer 2:9; Hos 4:1; 12:2; Mic 6:2.
- "I desire mercy, not sacrifice": a card and a table row (1 Sam 15:22; Isa 1:11; 29:13; 58:6; Hos 6:6; Amos 5:24; Mic 6:8; Matt 9:13).
- "Be ever hearing, but never understanding": a card and a table row (Isa 6:9; 53:1; Jer 5:21; Ezek 2:5; 12:2; 33:32; Matt 13:14; Acts 28:26).
- "Who kills the prophets": 1 Kgs 19:10; 2 Chr 36:16; Neh 9:26; Jer 26:23; Matt 5:12; 23:37; Acts 7:52.
- "The former prophets": prophets citing earlier prophets (Jer 26:18 quoting Micah; Jer 28:8; Dan 9:2 reading Jeremiah; Zech 1:4; 7:7, 12; Acts 15:15).
- "According to the word that the LORD had spoken": words the narratives record coming to pass, each pair in the text's own words (Josh 6:26 / 1 Kgs 16:34; 1 Kgs 13:2 / 2 Kgs 23:16; 1 Kgs 22:38; Jer 25:11 / 2 Chr 36:21 / Ezra 1:1, the seventy years).
- "The throne of David": a card (2 Sam 7:16; Isa 9:7; 11:1; Jer 23:5; Amos 9:11; Mic 5:2; Luke 1:32; Acts 2:30). The passages stand side by side, and nothing says one fulfils another.
- "A voice of one calling in the wilderness": a card (Isa 40:3; Mal 3:1; 4:5; Matt 3:3; Mark 1:2; Matt 11:14; Luke 1:17; John 1:23). The NT identifications are quoted as the NT's own words.
- "Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets": what the NT says of the prophets as a whole, quoted (Matt 5:17; Luke 4:21; 24:27, 44; Acts 3:24; 10:43; Rom 1:2; 1 Pet 1:10).
- "I will pour out My Spirit on all people": a table row (Num 11:25, 29; 1 Sam 10:10; Ezek 2:2; Joel 2:28; Acts 2:16, 18; Acts 21:9).
- "I will make a new covenant": a table row and chapter (Jer 31:31, 33; 32:40; Ezek 36:26; Luke 22:20; Heb 8:8). It overlaps Covenant, whose own concepts hold the same verses under its wording.

## Secondary and links

- Copilot's secondaries ("Prophecy → Fulfillment", "→ Messiah", and the others) were all replaced: their names framed the passages and all of them cited whole chapters. The new ones are the seer, the prophetess, the sons of the prophets, the burden, the nations, the Spirit of the LORD, no honor in his hometown, a fire and a hammer, and the Law and the Prophets. Each cites single verses.
- The links went from 14 to 10. Creation, Image of God and Blessing & Commission were dropped because no passage here overlaps them. Deliverance/Salvation and New Creation were dropped as thin: only Rev 21:5 touches New Creation. "Christology" became the allowed label Messiah/Christology. Law/Wisdom was kept for the Law and the Prophets, and Apocalyptic was added for Dan 2 and 7 and Rev 10:7.

## After the review

The outline went from 27 to 33 concepts. The numbers below are the review's, which refer to the first draft.

### Concepts

- 1, Amos 3:8 note: **applied**. It is now “The Lord GOD has spoken-who will not prophesy?”, with the hyphen exactly as the verse gives it, and no ellipsis.
- 3, "Here am I. Send me!": **applied**. Renamed "I am sending you" (Exod 3:10; Ezek 2:3; "I send you", Jer 1:7), and Galatians 1:15 dropped. The bsb.txt text bears the reviewer out: only Isa 6:8 says "Send me", and Gal 1:15 stops mid-clause. Isa 6:8 keeps the note “Here am I. Send me!”, so the card is still reached.
- 10, "Return to Me": **applied**. Isaiah 1:16–17 dropped: it says wash, stop doing evil and learn to do right, with no word of returning.
- 14, "Be ever hearing": **applied**. Isaiah 53:1 dropped: it says no one believed, not that people heard without understanding. John 12:38 still quotes it, in the Gospel's own words, under "To fulfill what was spoken through the prophet".
- 15, "Who kills the prophets": **applied**. Renamed "They persecuted the prophets" (Matt 5:12), because 2 Chr 36:16 says mocked and scoffed, not killed. The passages are unchanged.
- 17, "The former prophets": **applied**. Renamed "The prophets of old" (Jer 28:8) so the name does not suggest the canon division, and Acts 15:15 dropped. The verse does stop at "as it is written:" before any citation.
- 20, "The throne of David": **applied**. Micah 5:2 dropped: the verse names no throne and no David. Psalm 132:11 and Jeremiah 33:17, which name David's throne in so many words, were added as the reviewer suggested. Isaiah 11:1 was dropped to stay within 8 passages: it names Jesse, not David, and the reviewer called it borderline. Amos 9:11 stays because it names David.
- 21, "A voice of one calling in the wilderness": **applied**. Renamed "Prepare the way", with the passages and order unchanged. One correction to the reviewer's claim that every passage has the words: Mark 1:2 has "prepare Your way" and Luke 1:17 "a people prepared for the Lord", while Isa 40:3, Mal 3:1 and Matt 3:3 have "prepare the way". The name still says nothing its passages do not.
- 26, "I will make a new covenant": **applied**. Luke 22:20 dropped, and the Covenant theme holds it. Ezekiel 37:26 (“I will make a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant”) added, as the reviewer suggested.
- 27, Revelation 21:5 note: **applied**. It is now “Behold, I make all things new.” alone, with no ellipsis joining two sentences.
- Concepts 2, 4–9, 11–13, 16, 18, 19 and 22–25 were accepted unchanged.

### Secondary and links

- Sacrifice link: **applied**, as "Prophetic Word & Fulfillment → Sacrifice".
- "The Law → the Prophets": no change asked, and none made.
- "The prophet → the nations": **applied**. Jonah 1:2 was swapped for Jer 25:15–17 ("all the nations to whom I send you"). Jonah 1:2 does name a city, Nineveh, but neither a prophet nor "nations".
- Order of 22 and 23: **no change needed**. They stand together as 28 and 29, and both cite NT verses only.

### Missing sub-themes: all four added

The new concepts are not counted among the review points above.

- "Thus says the LORD": **added** as "This is what the LORD says", which is the Berean's wording of the formula (Exod 5:1; Isa 7:7; Jer 2:2, 5; Amos 1:3; Nah 1:12; Hag 1:5). It is concept 3.
- "Eat this scroll / I ate them": **added as two concepts**, because the texts develop eating and writing separately:
  - "Eat this scroll" (Ezek 3:1, 3; Jer 15:16; Rev 10:9, 10).
  - "Take a scroll and write on it" (Exod 17:14; Isa 30:8; Jer 30:2; 36:2; 36:28; Hab 2:2; Rev 1:19). Hab 2:2 moved here from "Visions and dreams".
- "Stand in the breach / Do not pray for this people": **added as two concepts**, the prophet who prays and the prayer forbidden. Both are developed in more than one place:
  - "Stand in the gap" (Gen 20:7; Exod 32:11; Ps 106:23; 1 Sam 12:23; Jer 42:4; Amos 7:2; Ezek 22:30).
  - "Do not pray for these people" (Jer 7:16; 11:14; 14:11; 15:1; Ezek 14:14).
- "A famine of hearing the words of the LORD": **added** (1 Sam 3:1; 28:6; Ps 74:9; Lam 2:9; Ezek 7:26; Amos 8:11, 12; Mic 3:7). It is placed among the dark concepts and is not softened.
- "A future and a hope" is still the last concept.

## After the second review

Every point below was checked against bsb.txt, and the text bears each one out.

- 4, "I am sending you": **applied**. Renamed "The call of the prophet". 1 Sam 3:10 is Samuel answering, Jer 1:5 is "set apart and appointed", and Amos 7:15 is "Go, prophesy": none of them says sending. Isa 6:8 keeps its note “Here am I. Send me!”.
- 26, "The throne of David": **applied**. Amos 9:11 ("the fallen tent of David") and Jer 23:5 ("a righteous Branch… He will reign wisely as King") were dropped, because neither names a throne. Six passages remain, and each one says throne.
- 28, "To fulfill what was spoken through the prophet": **applied**. John 19:24 dropped: it says "the Scripture" and quotes a psalm, not a prophet.
- 29, "Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets": **applied**. Luke 4:21 dropped: the verse itself names neither Moses nor the Prophets.
- 30, "I will pour out My Spirit on all people": **applied**. Renamed "The Spirit and prophesying". Num 11:25, 1 Sam 10:10, Ezek 2:2 and Acts 21:9 are the Spirit on particular people, not on all people. Joel 2:28 and Acts 2:16, 18 keep their own words in the notes.
- 32, "I will make a new covenant": **applied**. Ezek 36:26 dropped: it says a new heart and a new spirit, and the word covenant is not in the verse. It was not moved elsewhere, because "The Spirit and prophesying" and "A future and a hope" already hold 8 passages each.
- Kingdom link: **dropped**, as the coordinator directed and the review gave reason for. The outline now has 10 links.
- Secondaries: no change asked, and none made.
- `outline_check.py`: 33 concepts, 9 secondary, 10 links, 0 failures.
- Follow-up: the Kingdom link is **restored** now that the ten-link limit is lifted. The passages ground it: 2 Sam 7:16, “Your house and kingdom will endure forever”, and Isa 9:7, “He will reign on the throne of David and over his kingdom”, both in "The throne of David". The check now reports 11 links and 0 failures.

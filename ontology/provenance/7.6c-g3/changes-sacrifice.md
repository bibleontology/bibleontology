# Theme 7 — Sacrifice: changes to Copilot's outline

## Copilot's concepts

1. **Divine Provision of Substitution** — renamed → "God Himself will provide the lamb" (Gen 22:8). "Substitution" is a framing; the text's phrase is "in place of his son" (Gen 22:13), quoted as a note. Gen 3:21 cut (garments of skin, no offering in the verse). Exod 12 moved to "The Passover lamb", Lev 1:4 to "Lay his hand on the head", Isa 53 to "Like a lamb led to the slaughter".
2. **Atonement (Covering / Ransom / Reconciliation)** — renamed → "To make atonement" (table row; Lev 4:20, Heb 2:17). The three glosses are not the passages' words. Whole Lev 16 moved to "The Day of Atonement" and cut to verses; Num 35:33 cut (blood shed on the land, not an offering); Rom 3:25 moved to "By His blood".
3. **Blood as Life Given for Life** — renamed → "The blood is the life" (Deut 12:23; table row). "Life given for life" is not in the passages. Gen 9:5–6 cut (bloodshed between men, not sacrifice); Exod 12:13 moved to "The Passover lamb".
4. **Sacrifice as Worship** — renamed → "A pleasing aroma" (Gen 8:21; Lev 1:9; table row). "X as Y" framing. Gen 4:3–5 became its own concept (added below); Ps 50:14 and Heb 13:15 moved to "Thank offerings and the sacrifice of praise"; Exod 20:24 moved to section C.
5. **Sacrifice as Cleansing / Purification** — renamed → "A sin offering, a guilt offering" (table row), with Lev 4 and 5 cut to the verses that name the offerings. Lev 14, Num 19 and Heb 9:13–14 moved to the added "Sprinkled and made clean".
6. **Sacrifice as Covenant Maintenance** — renamed → "The blood of the covenant" (Exod 24:8; Matt 26:28; Heb 9:20). No passage speaks of "maintenance"; Lev 1–7 cut; Deut 12 moved to section C.
7. **Sacrifice as Fellowship (Peace Offerings)** — renamed → "The peace offering" (Lev 3:1; card "peace offering"). The Berean says "peace offering"; "fellowship" is the gloss. 1 Cor 10:18 kept for its own "fellow partakers in the altar".
8. **Priestly Mediation of Sacrifice** — dropped. Exod 28–29 (garments, consecration) and Lev 10:10–11 (teaching) are Priesthood's subject; the priest's part in the offerings stays in "To make atonement" (Lev 4:20; Lev 9:7; Heb 5:1) in the texts' words.
9. **Day of Atonement (Annual National Cleansing)** — kept as "The Day of Atonement" (Lev 23:27; card "the day of atonement"); the parenthetical gloss dropped, Lev 16 cut to verses (16:2, 8–10, 15–16, 21–22, 29–30).
10. **Christ as Final, Perfect Sacrifice** — renamed and split into three concepts in the New Testament's own words: "Look, the Lamb of God" (John 1:29, 36; card), "By His blood" (table row; Acts 20:28, Rom 3:25, Rom 5:9–11, Rev 5:9; card "reconciliation" in Rom 5:10) and "Once for all" (front page; Heb 7:27, 9:12, 10:10). "Final, perfect" is a framing. 1 Cor 5:7 moved to "The Passover lamb", Eph 5:2 to "A pleasing aroma"; 1 Pet 2:24 cut (no offering wording in the verse).

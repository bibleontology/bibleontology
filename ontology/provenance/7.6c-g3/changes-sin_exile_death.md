# Changes: theme 10, Sin / Exile / Death

Copilot's 11 concepts: 0 kept, 11 renamed, 0 dropped; 20 added. 31 core concepts in all.

## Copilot's concepts

1. **Sin as Rebellion Against God → "They rebelled against Me"** (renamed: Isa 1:2's phrase, the table's row). Gen 3:1–7 went to "The serpent deceived me", Gen 4:7 to "Sin is crouching at your door", Jer 2:13 and Rom 1:21–25 to the other-gods concept; added Num 14:9, Deut 9:7, Neh 9:26, Isa 63:10, Ezek 2:3, which say "rebel" in so many words.
2. **Sin as Lawlessness / Transgression → "Sin and the law"** (renamed: a plain label; only 1 John 3:4 says "lawlessness", while the rest say what the law does about sin). Rom 7:7–13 cut to 7:7 and 7:12–13; added Rom 3:20, Rom 5:13, Gal 3:19. Ps 51:1–4 moved to "Iniquity, transgression, and sin", "We have sinned" and "evil in the sight of the LORD".
3. **Sin as Idolatry / False Worship → "They have forsaken Me and served other gods"** (renamed: Judg 10:13's words, the table's row). 1 Kgs 12:28–33 cut to 12:28; Isa 44:9–20 cut to 44:17; Rom 1:23–25 cut to 1:25; added Judg 10:13, Jer 1:16, Jer 2:13.
4. **Sin as Corruption of Human Nature → "Evil in the heart"** (renamed: the table's row; "corruption of human nature" is a doctrinal framing where traditions part over original sin, and the passages say "heart"). Added Gen 8:21, Eccl 9:3, Mark 7:21–23, Rom 1:21. Psalm 14 went to "All have sinned", Rom 7:18–23 to "I do not do the good I want to do". Ps 51:5 is deliberately not used as evidence of a "nature".
5. **Sin Produces Death → "The wages of sin is death"** (renamed: Rom 6:23, the front page's quotation). Gen 3:19 moved to "To dust you shall return", Rom 5:12 to "Sin entered the world through one man"; added Ezek 3:18, Ezek 18:4, John 8:24 ("die in your sins" row).
6. **Sin Produces Exile (Removal from God's Presence) → "Cast out of His presence"** (renamed: the table's row; Jer 7:15). Gen 3:23–24 kept; Gen 4:12–16 cut to 4:14 and 4:16; Lev 18:24–28 moved to section C (the land vomits out); 2 Kgs 17:7–23 cut to 17:18; 2 Chr 36:15–21 cut to 36:20 and moved to "Taken into exile"; added 2 Kgs 13:23, 2 Kgs 24:20, Ps 51:11.
7. **Exile as Covenant Curse → "Scattered among the nations"** (renamed: "covenant curse" is a framing; the texts' word is "scatter", the table's row). Lev 26:33–39 cut to 26:33; Deut 28:36,64–68 cut to 28:64 (28:65 to "Wanderers among the nations"); Jer 25:11 moved to section C (seventy years); Ezek 12:15–20 cut to 12:15; added Deut 4:27, Neh 1:8, Jer 9:16, Ezek 22:15. Ps 44:11 says "scattered us among the nations" but is a lament of people who say they had not forgotten God, so it is left out of a sin theme.
8. **Death as Spiritual + Physical Reality → "Dead in your trespasses"** (renamed: Eph 2:1; "spiritual + physical" is a framing the texts do not use). Eph 2:1 and Col 2:13 kept; added Eph 2:5, Luke 15:24, 1 John 3:14, Rev 3:1. Gen 3:7–10 ("spiritual separation", a gloss) moved to section C as what it says (naked, afraid, hid); Heb 9:27 moved to "To dust you shall return".
9. **Human Inability to Self-Repair → "I do not do the good I want to do"** (renamed: the site's card on Rom 7:19; "inability" is where Reformed, Wesleyan, Catholic and Orthodox readers part, so the name stays with Paul's words). Jer 13:23 and Rom 8:7 kept; added Rom 7:18–19, Rom 7:23, Gal 5:17. John 6:44 dropped (it is about coming to Jesus, and contested over election); Ezek 36:26–27 moved to the last concept.
10. **Universal Sinfulness → "All have sinned"** (renamed: Rom 3:23, the card and the front page). Rom 3:9–23 cut to 3:10 and 3:23; Isa 53:6 moved to "Like sheep gone astray"; added Ps 143:2, Prov 20:9, Eccl 7:20, 1 John 1:8.
11. **Hope of Restoration → "Where sin increased, grace increased all the more"** (renamed: Rom 5:20, the brief's words of hope for this theme; placed last). Deut 30:1–6 cut to 30:3–4; Isa 40:1–2 kept; Ezek 36:24–28 cut to 36:24–26; added Isa 25:8, Ezek 37:12, Rom 5:20, Rom 6:23, 1 Cor 15:54–55 so that sin, exile and death each have the texts' own word of hope. Hosea 14:1–7 dropped (its call to return belongs to Mercy & Restoration). Hos 13:14 deliberately not used: the Berean reads it as a promise, other translations as a threat, and "Compassion is hidden from My eyes" closes the verse.

## After the review

The nine-reader review (verdict-sin_exile_death.json): 14 points, 11 applied and 3 not applied; 5 missing sub-themes, all added. There are now 36 core concepts, and the hope concept is still last (now no. 36).

### Concept points

- **1 "Sin is crouching at your door" → "Let no sin rule over me"**: applied. Only Gen 4:7 says "crouching", and Ps 119:133's words fit every passage. Gen 4:7 stays, with its own note.
- **6 drop Num 14:9**: applied. The verse is a warning not to rebel, not a report that anyone did.
- **11 (now 12) drop Jer 13:23 and Rom 8:7**: applied. Both move to the new "Neither are you able to do good". Rom 7:15 was added so that the concept keeps Paul's own words with 4 passages.
- **12 (now 14) Prov 20:9 note**: applied. The note now quotes the whole question, “Who can say, “I have kept my heart pure; I am cleansed from my sin”?”, inner quotation marks included as the Berean prints them.
- **15 (now 17) rename to "The fathers, the children, and each one's own iniquity"**: applied. The name now holds both voices, as the passages do.
- **17 (now 19) drop Heb 6:8**: applied. It is a figure inside the Hebrews 6 warning and does not say that the ground is cursed because of man.
- **19 (now 21) drop Heb 9:27**: applied. It says nothing of dust and moves to the new "What man can live and never see death?".
- **25 (now 29) drop 2 Kgs 13:23**: applied. It says the LORD was unwilling to cast Israel from His presence, the opposite of the name.
- **28 (now 32) drop Gen 4:12**: applied. Cain wanders "on the earth", not "among the nations", and the verse is already under "Cursed is the ground".

### Secondary and link points

- **Concept 31 (now 36): add 2 Kgs 13:23 to the hope concept**: not applied. The concept already holds 8 passages, the brief's limit, each giving sin, exile or death a word of hope in its own verse. The reviewer offered this as optional and found nothing dark softened.
- **Concept 2: Rev 12:9 may be dropped**: not applied. Its words are the text's own ("that ancient serpent called the devil and Satan"). Leaving out the one passage that names the serpent would hide an objective textual link, and no line in the site's voice draws from it.
- **Concept 8: rename to "Other gods and idols"**: applied. Exod 20:3–4, 1 Kgs 12:28 and Isa 44:17 do not say "forsaken Me", and the plain label holds all eight passages. It is also close to the site's "other gods" and "idolatry" cards.
- **Secondary "Sin → wash me clean"**: applied, renamed to "Sin → washing". Jer 2:22 says that washing does not remove the stain, so the line now holds both voices and Jer 2:22 is not presented as an instance of being cleansed.
- **Links: Law/Wisdom and Wisdom Literature**: not applied. They are two distinct themes on the brief's label list and in the site's parser (law_wisdom and wisdom_literature). Ecclesiastes and the Psalms on Sheol and dust overlap Wisdom Literature; Romans on sin and the law overlaps Law/Wisdom.

- **11. How can one born of woman be pure?**: added: Job 14:4, Job 15:14, Job 25:4, Ps 51:5, Ps 58:3. The name is Job 25:4's question rather than a doctrinal label or a phrase only one verse says. The verses are quoted plain, with no gloss on how traditions read them.

- **22. What man can live and never see death?**: added: 2 Sam 14:14, 1 Kgs 2:2, Ps 89:48, Eccl 9:3, Heb 9:27. 1 Kgs 2:2 ("the way of all the earth") was added from bsb.txt.
- **28. The second death**: added: Rev 2:11, Rev 20:6, Rev 20:14, Rev 21:8. The words only, with nothing on what it means or on the order of the last things.
- **35. All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten You**: added: Ps 44:11, Ps 44:17–18, Ps 44:22, Ps 44:24, Rom 8:36. The Berean reads "has … have", not the reviewer's "came … had". This is the other voice on scattering and death, set just before the hope concept. Ps 44:11 stays out of "Scattered among the nations", where it would read as punishment.

## After the second review

The second review (verdict2-sin_exile_death.json) raised 4 concept points, all applied, and 3 general remarks that ask for no change. The outline still has 36 core concepts, and the hope concept is still last.

- **8 "Other gods and idols", drop Jer 2:13**: applied. The verse alone names no god or idol; that is Jer 2:11, "exchanged their Glory for useless idols". Judg 10:13 and Jer 1:16 already carry "forsaken Me" with other gods. 7 passages remain.
- **11 "How can one born of woman be pure?", drop Ps 58:3**: applied. "The wicked are estranged from the womb" speaks of a class of people, not of everyone born of woman. 4 passages remain (Job 14:4, 15:14, 25:4, Ps 51:5). The reviewer's note that Job 15:14 and 25:4 are Eliphaz's and Bildad's speeches is recorded here for the owner; no gloss is added.
- **18 "He gave them over", drop Isa 64:7**: applied, after checking the original. In MACULA, Hebrew Isa 64:6 (English 64:7) has the lemma מוּג, glossed "have melted us". "Delivered us" is the Berean's rendering, not what the Hebrew word says, so the verse does not say "gave them over" in the original. It stays in "I will hide My face", where its "hidden Your face from us" is plain. 5 passages remain.
- **27 "Dead in your trespasses" → "You were dead"**: applied. Only Eph 2:1, 2:5 and Col 2:13 say "trespasses". Luke 15:24, 1 John 3:14 and Rev 3:1 say "dead" of living people, and "You were dead" (Eph 2:1) holds all six.
- **General remarks** (the closing hope accepted; Rev 12:9 kept on the drafter's reasoning; no change to the secondary lines or links): no change was asked for. No note in concept 2 says who the Genesis serpent is.

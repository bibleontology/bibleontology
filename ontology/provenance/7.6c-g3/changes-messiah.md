# Messiah / Christology (theme 15): changes to Copilot's outline

Every Copilot name was "Messiah as X" or "Messiah's X", a frame the passages do not state: the Old Testament passages do not say "Messiah", and the New Testament ones say who they mean in their own words. Every concept is now named as its passages name it, Old and New Testament passages stand side by side, and the site's voice never says an Old Testament passage is about Jesus. Where a New Testament text says so itself (Luke 4:21, Acts 2:31, Acts 8:35, Luke 24:27, 24:44), it is quoted as that text's own words.

## Copilot's concepts

Psalm 45:7 dropped for room (a king's wedding psalm; Hebrews 1:9 quotes it). Isaiah 61:1 and Luke 4:18–21 moved to the added "The Spirit on Him".

- 13 "Messiah as Fulfillment of All Scripture" → renamed **Written about Me** (Luke 24:44, John 5:46, Hebrews 10:7: the New Testament's own claim in its own words, not the site's). Luke 24:25–27 cut to 24:27 (24:26 under "The Christ had to suffer"); Luke 24:44–47 cut to 24:44 (24:46 under "The Christ had to suffer"); Acts 3:18–24 cut to 3:18 and 3:22; 2 Corinthians 1:20 dropped (it is the promises of God, not Scripture about Him; Prophetic Word link); John 5:39, 5:46 added.
- "Fulfillment in Jesus Christ" and "Messianic Victory" (listed by the machine check, not in the packet's text of the outline) → dropped: "fulfillment" is the site's voice on the Old Testament, and "victory" is a frame; the New Testament's own words on Scripture are "Written about Me", and the triumph is in "The Lamb" (Revelation 17:14).

## Concepts added

- **The Spirit on Him**: Isaiah 11:2, 42:1, 61:1; Luke 4:18–21, Matthew 3:16, John 1:32–33, Acts 10:38 ("God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit").
- **The Messiah, the Christ**: the front page's John 1:41 and the card "the Christ"; John 4:25–26 (card "I am he"), 4:29, 7:41, Acts 9:22, 18:28, John 20:31.
- **You are the Christ**: the front page's "Who do you say I am?" (Matthew 16, Mark 8, Luke 9), with Martha's answer and the high priest's question.
- **The Son of David**: the card and the table's row; the cries for mercy, the crowds, Romans 1:3, 2 Timothy 2:8.
- **But you, Bethlehem**: the card "you Bethlehem"; Micah 5:2 with Matthew 2:4–6, Luke 2:4, 2:11, and John 7:42, where the crowd argues it.
- **The King of Israel, the King of the Jews**: the table's row (Kingdom has a concept of the same name; these passages are the ones that say "Christ" and "King" together, the charge and the mockery kept).
- **Immanuel**: the card and the table's row; Isaiah 7:14, 8:8, 8:10 (“For God is with us”, the Hebrew "immanu El"), Matthew 1:22–23. The Berean's "virgin" is quoted as the Berean has it.
- **He will be called**: the names the texts give: Isaiah 9:6, Jeremiah 23:6, Matthew 1:21, Luke 1:31–32, Matthew 2:23, Philippians 2:9–11.
- **The Word**: the cards "In the beginning was the Word", "The Word became flesh", "The Word of God".
- **Through Him all things were made**: the card; John 1:3, 1:10, 1 Corinthians 8:6, Colossians 1:16, Hebrews 1:2.
- **The image of the invisible God**: Colossians 1:15, 2 Corinthians 4:4, Hebrews 1:3, John 14:9.
- **Light**: the cards "the people living in darkness have seen a great light", "the true Light"; Isaiah 9:2 beside Matthew 4:16, Isaiah 42:6, 49:6, Luke 2:30–32, John 1:9, 8:12.
- **The Branch, the Shoot, the Root**: the table's row; Isaiah 11:1, Jeremiah 23:5, 33:15, Zechariah 3:8, 6:12, Revelation 5:5, 22:16.
- **The Shepherd**: the table's row and the card "shepherd"; Ezekiel 34:23, Micah 5:4, Zechariah 13:7 with Mark 14:27, John 10:11, Hebrews 13:20, 1 Peter 5:4, Revelation 7:17.
- **Despised and rejected**: Isaiah 53:3, Psalm 22:6–7, Mark 8:31, Luke 17:25, John 1:11, Acts 3:14.

- **The Lamb**: the table's row and the cards "Look, the Lamb of God" and "behold the Lion ... a Lamb"; Isaiah 53:7 beside Acts 8:32–35, where Philip reads it, John 1:29, 1 Corinthians 5:7, 1 Peter 1:19, Revelation 5:6, 5:12, 17:14.

- **Your Holy One, the Holy One of God**: the table's row; Psalm 16:10 beside Acts 2:27, Mark 1:24, Luke 1:35, John 6:69, Revelation 3:7. Isaiah's "Holy One of Israel" is not used: it is said of the LORD.
- **The LORD said to my Lord**: Psalm 110:1 with Jesus' question (Matthew 22:42–45), Acts 2:34–36 ("both Lord and Christ"), Hebrews 1:13.
- **An iron scepter**: Psalm 2:9 with Revelation 2:26–27, 12:5, 19:15; the hard word kept as its own sub-theme.
- **In Him the nations will put their hope** (the last concept, the theme's words of hope): Isaiah 11:10 with Romans 15:12, Isaiah 42:4 with Matthew 12:21, Colossians 1:27 ("Christ in you, the hope of glory").

## Sections C and D

- C: Copilot's eleven were the core concepts again ("Messiah → King") over whole chapters; replaced by nine in the texts' words, cut to verses: the Psalm 22 cry and the garments (with the Gospels that quote them), the pierced one (Zechariah 12:10, John 19:37, Revelation 1:7), the ransom, the intercession, the lion of Judah, the winepress (Isaiah 63:3, Revelation 19:15, kept rather than left out), Melchizedek himself, and the name above all names.
- D: sixteen links cut to ten, each where the passages overlap: Kingdom, Covenant (2 Samuel 7, Psalm 89:3, Isaiah 42:6), Priesthood, Sacrifice (the Lamb, 1 Corinthians 5:7), Prophetic Word & Fulfillment (the prophet like Moses, "Written about Me"), Spirit, Judgment, Creation ("Through Him all things were made"), Deliverance/Salvation (Matthew 1:21, Luke 19:10), and Father, Son and Holy Spirit (added: the Son, the Spirit on Him, "The Word was with God"). Image of God, Blessing & Commission, Law/Wisdom, Sin/Exile/Death, Mercy & Restoration, Temple/Presence, Church and New Creation dropped as links: no core passage is about them (the image of the invisible God is said of the Son, and is left to that theme to link back).

## After the review

Concept numbers below are the first draft's (34 concepts); the revised outline has 40 and renumbers them. The first draft is kept as scratchpad/msh/outline-messiah.r1.txt.

### Concepts
- 1–6, 8, 11–13, 15, 19, 22–24, 26–34: accepted by the review, no change, except that 24 "He bore the sin of many" gains Isaiah 53:11 (“He will bear their iniquities”) from "My Servant", 11 "He will be called" cites Philippians 2:9 alone (2:10–11 went to the added "Jesus is Lord"), and 21 "The Son of Man" is split (below).
- 7 "But you, Bethlehem" → **Bethlehem**, Luke 2:11 dropped: applied. Luke 2:11 says "the city of David", not Bethlehem; it stands in the added "Jesus is Lord" (“He is Christ the Lord!”).
- 9 "My Son, the Son of God", drop Mark 1:1: applied. "Son of God" is absent from some early manuscripts there, the same kind of question for which John 1:18 was dropped. Mark 15:39 stays as the review allows; the concept rests on seven passages, not on it.
- 10 "Immanuel", Isaiah 7:14 note: applied. The note is now “the Lord Himself will give you a sign … and will call Him Immanuel”, which does not stand on the Berean's "the virgin" (Hebrew ha-almah); the verse itself is still shown whole.
- 14 "The image of the invisible God", drop John 14:9: applied. It does not say "image". Three passages remain (Colossians 1:15, 2 Corinthians 4:4, Hebrews 1:3).
- 16 "A prophet like me, the Prophet", add Deuteronomy 34:10: applied (“Since that time, no prophet has risen in Israel like Moses”). Deuteronomy 18:20–22 not added: it is the test of any prophet and does not hold the name "a prophet like me"; the concept is also at eight.
- 17 "A priest forever in the order of Melchizedek", drop Hebrews 7:24–25: applied; Hebrews 7:21 added in its place, as the review suggests (“Jesus became a priest with an oath … You are a priest forever”). 7:25 stays in section C's "Intercession".
- 18 "My Servant", add Isaiah 41:8–9, 44:1–2, 44:21: applied, all three. To stay at eight, Isaiah 53:11 moved to "He bore the sin of many" (its words “He will bear their iniquities” hold that name) and Acts 3:13 and 4:27 moved to a new section C entry "Servant → His servant Jesus" with Acts 3:26. Isaiah 49:3 and 49:5–6 are kept side by side, as the review asks, so the text's own tension stands unresolved.
- 20 "The Shepherd", add Ezekiel 34:15–16 and Psalm 23:1: applied, both. To stay at eight, Hebrews 13:20 and 1 Peter 5:4 moved to section C ("Shepherd → the great Shepherd, the Chief Shepherd").
- 21 "The Son of Man", add Daniel 7:18, 7:27, Psalm 8:4, Ezekiel 2:1: applied, by splitting it in two so neither goes over eight. **Son of man** (lower case, a plain label every passage holds) has Psalm 8:4 and Ezekiel 2:1, the ordinary Hebrew use, beside the Gospels' and Acts' sayings. **One like the Son of Man** has Daniel 7:13–14 with the chapter's own interpretation (7:18, 7:27: the kingdom given to "the saints of the Most High") and Revelation 1:13, 14:14, which use Daniel's words. Hebrews 2:6 not added (it quotes Psalm 8:4 and would be a ninth).
- 25 "The Lamb", add Exodus 12:3–7: applied (“each man must select a lamb for his family … Your lamb must be an unblemished year-old male”). Revelation 5:12 dropped to stay at eight; Revelation 5:6 keeps the slain Lamb. Genesis 22:8 not added, as the review says.

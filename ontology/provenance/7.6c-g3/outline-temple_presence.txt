* Presence → Blessing (Num 6:24–26)
* The angels of God → going up and down; ascending and descending (Gen 28:12; John 1:51)
* The ark → the mercy seat (Exod 25:21–22; Heb 9:4–5)
* The altar of incense → before the veil (Exod 30:6–8)
* The basin → washing before they enter (Exod 30:18–21)
* The temple → One greater than the temple (Matt 12:6)
* Atonement → once a year (Exod 30:10; Lev 16:34; Heb 9:7)
* The cloud → a voice from the cloud (Exod 24:16; Matt 17:5; 2 Pet 1:17–18)
* The house → zeal for Your house (Ps 69:9; John 2:17)
* Worship → in spirit and in truth (John 4:21–23)

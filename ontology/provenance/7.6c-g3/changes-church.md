# Church (theme 17): changes from Copilot's outline

Every passage was looked up in bsb.txt and every note copied from its verse. `tools/themes/outline_check.py` reports 47 concepts, 10 secondary, 9 links, 0 failures. There are 331 passages, every concept has 3 to 8, and the names in A and B are identical.

## Copilot's twelve concepts

Every one of Copilot's names began "Church as …", so none was kept as worded.

- **1. Church as God's Called‑Out People**: renamed → "The church of God" (the table's row; 1 Cor 1:2). "Called-out" glosses the word's etymology, and no passage says it. Matt 16:18 and 1 Cor 1:2 are kept. Acts 2:39 moved to "Those far off and those near". Rom 1:6–7 was cut to 1:7 and moved to "The saints". 1 Pet 2:9 moved to "A chosen people, a holy nation" and "A royal priesthood".
- **2. Church as New‑Covenant Community**: dropped. The name equates the church with the people of Jer 31's new covenant, and that is where traditions (and Jewish readers) part. Jer 31:31–34, Ezek 36:25–27 and Heb 8:6–13 do not speak of the church, so they stay with Covenant. Luke 22:20 moved to "The breaking of bread". Acts 2:42–47 was cut to 2:42 ("The fellowship", "The breaking of bread"), 2:44–45 ("Share with the saints who are in need"), 2:46 ("The church that meets at their house") and 2:47 ("The Lord added to their number daily").
- **3. Church as Body of Christ**: renamed → "You are the body of Christ" (1 Cor 12:27, on the front page). It was also split, because the texts develop the head separately: see "The head of the body". Rom 12:4–5 is kept. 1 Cor 12:12–27 was cut to 12:12, 12:13 and 12:27. Eph 1:22–23, Eph 4:15 and Col 1:18 moved to the head. Eph 4:16 stays here. Added: 1 Cor 10:17, Eph 3:6 and Col 3:15.
- **4. Church as Temple of the Spirit**: renamed → "A dwelling place for God" (Eph 2:22; Lev 26:11 "My dwelling place among you"). The texts call the people God's temple and His dwelling, and the name now covers the Torah's passages as well as Paul's. 1 Cor 3:16–17 and 6:19 are kept. Eph 2:19–22 was cut to 2:21–22; 2:19 moved to "God's house, God's household" and 2:20 to "The cornerstone". 1 Pet 2:5 moved to "A royal priesthood". Added: Exod 25:8, Exod 29:45, Lev 26:11 and 2 Cor 6:16.
- **5. Church as Royal Priesthood**: renamed → "A royal priesthood" (1 Pet 2:9's words, without the "Church as" frame). Exod 19:5–6 was cut to 19:6. 1 Pet 2:5, 1 Pet 2:9, Rev 1:6 and Rev 5:10 are kept. Isa 61:6 was added ("you will be called the priests of the LORD").
- **6. Church as Family of God**: renamed, split into two of the table's rows, "Here are My mother and My brothers" and "God's house, God's household". Matt 12:48–50 was cut to 12:49–50. Eph 2:19 went to the household. John 1:12–13, Rom 8:14–17 and 1 John 3:1–2 speak of becoming God's children, not of the people gathered, so they are left to the Children of God theme, which section D links. Added: Mal 2:10 ("Do we not all have one Father?"), Matt 23:8, Heb 2:11, 1 Tim 5:1–2 and 1 John 3:14, and for the household Num 12:7, Gal 6:10, 1 Tim 3:15, Heb 3:6 and 1 Pet 4:17.
- **7. Church as Bride of Christ**: renamed → "The bridegroom and his bride" (the table's row). "Of Christ" says more than Isaiah and Hosea do. Hos 2:19–20 was cut to 2:19. Eph 5:25–32 was cut to 5:25 and 5:32, with 5:27 moved to the closing concept. Rev 19:7–9 and 21:2, 9 also moved to the closing concept. Added: Isa 54:5, Isa 62:5, Matt 9:15, John 3:29 and 2 Cor 11:2.
- **8. Church as Kingdom Community**: dropped, because no passage calls the church a kingdom community. Its passages:
  - Matt 16:19 is kept, under the site's card "The keys of the kingdom of heaven", beside Matt 18:18 and John 20:23. Peter's keys and the disciples' binding are side by side, with no line on who holds them now.
  - Matt 28:18–20 was cut to 28:19–20 ("Go and make disciples of all nations") and to 28:19 (Baptism).
  - Col 1:13 is left to Kingdom.
  - Rev 1:6 moved to "A royal priesthood".
- **9. Church as Missionary People**: renamed, split into "You are My witnesses" (Isa 43:10; 44:8; Acts 1:8, said to Israel and to the disciples, side by side) and "Go and make disciples of all nations" (Matt 28:19). John 20:21 and Acts 1:8 are kept. Rom 10:14–17 was cut to 10:14–15, and 2 Cor 5:18–20 to 5:20. Added: Isa 43:10, Isa 44:8, Luke 24:48, John 15:27, Acts 2:32, Acts 5:32, Isa 52:7 (the verse Rom 10:15 quotes), Matt 24:14 and Acts 13:47.
- **10. Church as Holy & Set Apart**: renamed → "Be holy, because I am holy" (the site's card; 1 Pet 1:16, quoting Lev 11:44). Lev 20:26 and 1 Pet 1:15–16 are kept. 1 Cor 1:2 moved to "The church of God" and "Those who call on the name of the LORD". Eph 1:4 moved to "Called and chosen". Titus 2:14 moved to "Your congregation, which You purchased"; its note "zealous for good works" was not the Berean's wording ("good deeds"). Added: Lev 11:44, Lev 19:2, 2 Cor 7:1, 1 Thess 4:7 and Heb 12:14.
- **11. Church as Unified Yet Diverse**: renamed, split into "That they may be one" (John 17's words) and "Different gifts, the same Spirit" (the site's card). The changes:
  - John 17:20–23 was cut to 17:21 and 17:23, and 17:11 was added.

  - 1 Cor 12:4–27 ran 24 verses. It was cut to 12:4, 12:7, 12:11 and 12:28.

  - Added: Ps 133:1, Acts 4:32, Phil 2:2, 1 Cor 12:28, Eph 4:11–12 and 1 Pet 4:10.
- **12. Church as Eschatological Community**: dropped. "Eschatological" is a framing. Rom 8:18–23 speaks of the creation groaning, and Eph 1:13–14 of the Spirit's seal; neither speaks of the church. Matt 24:14 moved to "Go and make disciples of all nations". Heb 12:22–24 was cut to 12:22–23 and moved to "I will praise You in the assembly". Rev 21–22 (two chapters) was cut to 21:2, 21:9 and 22:17 (the closing concept) and 21:24 (the nations).

# Priesthood (theme 8): changes from Copilot's outline

Copilot's 11 concepts: 0 kept, 9 renamed, 2 dropped. 41 concepts added (50 in all). Every note is now a quotation copied from the Berean; every whole-chapter citation (Exodus 28, Exodus 29, Leviticus 6–7, 8–9, 16, 21–22, Hebrews 7) is cut to the verses that carry the point.

## Copilot's concepts

1. "Priests as Mediators Between God and People": renamed → "To serve Me as priests" (Exod 28:1, the front page's own quotation). "Mediators" is a framing none of its passages uses; the passages say "serve Me as priests" and "the work of the priesthood". Num 16:47–48 moved to "Intercession: between the living and the dead"; Heb 5:1 to "The priest will make atonement".
2. "Priests as Representatives of the People Before God": renamed → "A breastpiece of judgment" (the site's card). Its passages (Exod 28:12, 29) are about bearing the names of the sons of Israel on the shoulders and the breastpiece; Lev 16:15–17 and Heb 9:7 moved to "For himself and for the people" and "The Day of Atonement".
3. "Priests as Teachers of God's Law": renamed → "The lips of a priest should preserve knowledge" (Mal 2:7; the table's row). Lev 10:10 also became its own concept, "Distinguish between the holy and the common".
4. "Priests as Guardians of Holiness": renamed → "To those who come near Me I will show My holiness" (Lev 10:3, Nadab and Abihu). Leviticus 21 cut to verses and moved to "They must be holy to their God"; Num 18:1–7 to "Keep charge of the sanctuary".
5. "Priests as Sacrificial Ministers": renamed → "The priest will make atonement" (Lev 4:20 and the table's row). Leviticus 4:20–35 and 6–7 cut to single verses.
6. "Priestly Consecration & Holiness Requirements": renamed → "Ordain them and consecrate them" (Exod 28:41). Exodus 29 and Leviticus 8–9 cut to verses; the holiness laws (Lev 21–22) went to "They must be holy to their God"; Ps 132:9 to "May Your priests be clothed with righteousness".
7. "High Priest as Supreme Mediator": dropped. "Supreme Mediator" is a title no passage gives. Its passages went to the high priest's own sub-themes: garments (Exodus 28 → "Holy garments, for glory and splendor"), Leviticus 16 → "The Day of Atonement", Num 35:25 → "Until the death of the high priest".
8. "Melchizedekian Priesthood (Eternal Pattern)": renamed → "A priest forever in the order of Melchizedek" (Ps 110:4; Heb 5:6). "Eternal Pattern" is a framing; Hebrews 7 cut to 7:1–3 and 7:15–16.
9. "Israel as a Kingdom of Priests": renamed → "A kingdom of priests" (Exod 19:6). The texts' phrase without the gloss; Revelation's "a kingdom, priests" (1:6; 5:10) and 20:6 sit beside it in their own words.
10. "Christ as the Final High Priest": dropped as a name. "Final" says more than the passages. Its passages split into three sub-themes Hebrews names itself: "A great high priest" (Heb 4:14), "A permanent priesthood" (Heb 7:24), "Once for all" (Heb 7:27; 9:12; 10:10).
11. "Church as Royal Priesthood": renamed → "A royal priesthood" (1 Pet 2:9; front page quotation). "Church as" is a framing; Rev 1:6 and 5:10 moved to "A kingdom of priests".

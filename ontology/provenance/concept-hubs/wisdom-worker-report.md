## Contested points for the panel

1. **Row 1 and row 2, Wisdom personified (the one the brief named).** Row 1 holds the verses where wisdom calls, speaks, builds, is "my sister" (Prov 1:20, 7:4, 8:1, 8:11, 8:12, 9:1) and the Gospel sayings "wisdom is vindicated by her actions / all her children" (Matt 11:19; Luke 7:35) and "the wisdom of God said" (Luke 11:49). Row 2 holds the verses that name Jesus beside wisdom: Luke 2:40, 2:52; Matt 13:54, Mark 6:2; Matt 12:42, Luke 11:31; Luke 21:15; 1 Cor 1:24, 1:30; Col 2:3; Rev 5:12. Both notes say readers part (Christ, an attribute of God, a figure of speech) and the page does not choose. **Prov 8:22-31 is not listed**: it holds no word for wisdom in its own verses (Wisdom speaks in the first person). The row 1 note quotes 8:22 ("The LORD created me as His first course") and says the word-by-word Hebrew glosses the verb "possessed", and quotes 8:30 ("a skilled craftsman at His side"); it says readers part on both words. Please read that note closely: it is the heart of the point and it is the page saying something about the original, though only what the word-by-word text itself shows. Luke 11:49 is set beside Matt 23:34 ("I am sending you prophets and wise men and teachers") with "the page does not say how they relate". Luke 2:40, Matt 13:54, Mark 6:2 are in row 2 although the verse says "the Child", "this man", "Him" (the note says so; the passage around each is about Jesus). Strict reading of "the divine persons by hand" would drop them to a "not named in the verse" row; I kept them because no reader doubts whom they mean.
2. **Row 5, "a spirit of wisdom" / the Spirit.** Exod 31:3 and 35:31 ("the Spirit of God"), Isa 11:2 ("The Spirit of the LORD ... the Spirit of wisdom"), Acts 6:3, 6:10 ("the Spirit and wisdom") beside Deut 34:9, Exod 28:3, Eph 1:17 (lower-case "a spirit of wisdom"). The note says readers part on whether this names the Holy Spirit or a gift, and on whom Isa 11:2 speaks of. 1 Cor 12:8 ("given through the Spirit the message of wisdom") is in the row; the page says nothing of whether the gifts continue.
3. **Row 13, Ezek 28:4-7, 28:12, 28:17.** The lament "for the king of Tyre" ("full of wisdom and perfect in beauty"). The note says readers part on whether it speaks only of him. Visions rule: connected, not interpreted. Also 1 Cor 2:6 "the rulers of this age" (human or spiritual powers: note says the page does not choose).
4. **Row 17, Rev 13:18 and 17:9** ("Here is a call for wisdom", "This calls for a mind with wisdom"): placed with Daniel's wisdom to read dreams and writings; the note says what is to be understood the page does not say.
5. **Row 3 and row 13, "the wisdom from above" (Jas 3:17) and "earthly, unspiritual, demonic" wisdom (Jas 3:15)**: set in different rows. Row 3's note says the question of whether the wisdom by which the LORD made the world is an attribute, a figure or a person is row 1's, and the page does not choose.
6. **Row 10, Wisdom and the law** (the rabbi's seat should read this): Deut 4:6, Ps 111:10, Prov 31:26, Ezra 7:25. The note says תּוֹרָה is the law and also "instruction" and that the Berean prints "faithful instruction" for it at Prov 31:26; also cites Jer 8:8 (adjective) "We are wise, and the Law of the LORD is with us". Nothing about the law and the Christian is claimed.
7. **Row 14, Ecclesiastes**: both sides kept on purpose ("wisdom exceeds folly, as light exceeds darkness"; "with much wisdom comes much sorrow"; "no ... wisdom in Sheol"; "the wisdom of the poor man is despised"). Eccl 9:10 touches the death hub's intermediate state; the note only quotes it.
8. **Adjective, "God is called wise"**: three verses that name God (Job 9:4; Rom 16:27; 1 Cor 1:25). Isa 31:2 ("Yet He too is wise") says only "He" and is left in Every other use; the note says the LORD is named in 31:1 and the page does not fill the "He" in.
9. **Skill versus wisdom (row 6 and the adjective's Skilled row)**: one Hebrew word, kept as a sense of its own; the note says the page does not say whether craftsman's skill and Proverbs' wisdom are one meaning or two. Exod 31:3 and 35:31 print "skill" for the word beside "the Spirit of God".
10. **Adjective "wise in their own eyes"**: Matt 11:25 ("hidden these things from the wise and learned") is in this row with the note "the page does not say more". It could be read as touching election; it is placed only as the text.

## What the builder could not express

- **Aramaic and Hebrew nouns that share their letters.** MACULA has Aramaic חׇכְמָה (h2452) and Hebrew חָכְמָה (h2451) as two entries with the same lemma letters; the builder reads a clause's word by its letters, finds two candidates and none for either. With both in `words` the noun form lost every Hebrew verse (60 of 193 found). I kept only the Hebrew in `words`, left the Aramaic to `others` (with a note and its seven verses: Ezra 7:25; Dan 2:20, 2:21, 2:23, 2:30, 5:11, 5:14) and named those verses by hand in the rows where they fit (3, 4, 10, 17). They therefore carry no word tag in the rows. Option needed: key `which()` on the language as well as the letters, or let a spec list two words under one spelling and tell them apart by the book (Ezra and Daniel's Aramaic chapters).
- **A passage with no word of the concept in it.** Prov 8:22-31 is the verse range readers most want here, but no verse in it holds a wisdom word, so a row cannot list it. Option needed: a `span` condition (a verse range attached to a pick row, shown without a word tag, with a note that it is the speech of the word-bearing verse before).
- **A row conditioned on the gloss.** The Berean prints "skill" for the same word at Exod 31:3 and "wisdom" at Exod 28:3; I did it by hand (pick), as the faith hub did for "faithfulness".
- **The adjective in a verb form is a different record.** `חָכַם` the verb and `חָכָם` the adjective have the same letters; the builder keeps them apart by their pointing (both ran through), and it worked here, but a spelling in the data that differed by a point would have lost them silently.

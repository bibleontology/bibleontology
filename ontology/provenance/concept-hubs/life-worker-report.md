## What the builder could not express

- **A rule on a word's neighbour by phrase**: "the days of his life" and "as the LORD lives" are split across MACULA phrase parts, so `with` + `adjacent` found 0 and 51-of-77 (and took 10 "living creature" verses); both rows are by hand. A `with` that read the whole clause for the two words (or a regex on the Berean text of the verse) would have kept them rule-made.
- **A `sense` per word, not per hub**: the one regex covers all twelve words. For נֶפֶשׁ and ψυχή I needed "life only", for ζάω "live"; one regex did it, but a per-word `sense` would let a worker keep ψυχή "soul" out and ζάω "live" in without worry about stray glosses.
- **`object_of` is not sense-aware per verse**: Prov 29:24 and Ezek 14:14 entered "A life laid down..." because the original word is "life" while the Berean prints "soul" or "themselves"; a `sense_english` filter against the Berean text would catch these. They are out by `not_verses`.
- **Aramaic and Hebrew חַי share one spelling** (`pointed` is keyed by spelling): the Aramaic key had to be listed first so Hebrew would win; its five verses reach the rows only by hand.
- **Cross-form picks**: the participle ("the living God", "living water", "the Living One") is a verb in the lexicon and a noun/adjective in meaning; rows of the noun form hold picks of the verb ζάω, and the word tags do show the right word.

## What the builder could not express (not edited)

1. **A condition on the gloss** (e.g. `gloss: "faithfulness"`): Greek πίστις "faithfulness" vs "faith", Hebrew אָמַן "faithful" vs "believe", πιστός "believing" vs "faithful". All done with `pick`.
2. **Frames lack substantival participles** ("whoever believes in Him"; 39 verb verses including John 3:16, 3:36, 6:40, 1 John 5:1, Mark 16:16, Rom 10:10, Isa 28:16): in no rule row and not under More; hand-picked. Needed: build verb rows from the word-by-word text when the frames lack the verse.
3. **"This verse or the next"** (`in_verse` + `next: 1`): Gal 3:26-27, Acts 16:31-33.
4. **Window mix-ups**: `with`/`adjacent` counts lemmas only, not the phrase's attachment (Eph 3:17 "in your hearts", Rom 3:22 "of God"); the Jesus and God rows are picks for that reason.
5. **Clause filing**: Rom 3:26's "ἐκ πίστεως Ἰησοῦ" is filed under 3:25.
6. **Hebrew/Berean verse numbering**: Hos 12:1 needed `moves` (Berean 11:12).
7. **A row-count limit**: with `match: all` the builder happily builds 68 rows; a `merge`/`group` option (rows listed as sub-lines of one row, each with its own count) would let a hub keep the detail the consolidation had to drop.
8. **Titus 1:4 and Deut 32:20** (faith words) are in no row (clause filing; one verse each).

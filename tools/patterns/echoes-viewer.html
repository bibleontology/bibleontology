Echoes, NT → OT

Each New Testament verse with the Old Testament verses whose rare nouns and verbs it shares (through every Septuagint rendering of the Hebrew). The score is the rarity those words carry together, scaled by how much of both verses that is; it grades how much is shared, not whether it is a quotation, an allusion or an echo, and says nothing of the stance the later verse takes (affirms, extends, fulfils, contrasts). A rare PAIR of shared words (stone and stumbling) counts for more than the two words apart. Graded by hand, 8 pairs a band: score 14.5 and up about 5 of 8 real, 12.4–14.5 about 4, 10.7–12.4 about 2–3, below that about 1 — and some real quotations (Rev 5:10 → Exod 19:6) sit that low, among chance matches. Under each OT verse: where that verse is itself echoed in other OT books. Made by tools/patterns/echoes.py. Verse numbers are the BSB's.

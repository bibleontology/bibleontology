Psalm 23 · The LORD Is My Shepherd

Six verses, one voice. Spoken about the LORD, then to him, then about him again; the shepherd becomes the host; and the name that opens it closes it. Each part opens its card.

Swipe sideways inside the drawing to see all of it.

Drawn by hand from the psalm's grammar, a reader's drawing. The turns are the text's own: who is spoken to changes at v. 4 (“You”, אַתָּה), and the image at v. 5 (a table, oil, a cup). What the valley, the table and the enemies stand for, the psalm does not say, and the drawing adds nothing.

Romans 8

From “no condemnation” to “nothing able to separate us”, in the five sections the Berean editors mark. The Spirit is named 21 times, and not once after 8:27; love is named only at the end. Each box opens its card.

8:12–17 · Heirs with Christ

The scale of 8:18, the creation waiting, three groanings of one root, and hope.

The chain of 8:29–30 as the grammar makes it, and four questions.

8:35–39 · More than Conquerors

One question, two lists, and the answer.

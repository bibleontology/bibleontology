Bible Ontology — relations

Namespace https://bibleontology.org/ns/relation# · by Doug Egge · CC BY 4.0 · version 1.2.1 (this release, kept) · view: formatted · Turtle text · shapes

The controlled vocabulary of relations the Bible Ontology graph may assert: each reads '<source> <label> <target>', and every relation in the graph uses one of these types. Companion to https://bibleontology.org/ns/meaning.

Reasoning policy

These terms describe the graph; they are not built for automated inference over it. Every relation in the graph is a claim that carries its verse, its certainty and, where someone in the text says it, its speaker, and a reasoner that ignores those turns a character's claim into the narrator's or a reading into a fact. So: no owl:sameAs between records (possibly_same_as and spoken_of_as are readings and names, never identities); no functional or inverse-functional kinship (the accounts name more than one father or mother for some people, and the graph keeps both); no transitive chaining of images, figures, symbols or places; and nothing about the records of God, the Father, Jesus or the Holy Spirit is derived by inference: they change only by a recorded editorial decision, as the accounts give them. The policy forbids derivations, not descriptions. Constraints are checked, closed-world, by the SHACL shapes at https://bibleontology.org/ns/shapes, not inferred.

Versioning

A term's address never changes and is never reused. A term whose meaning changes gets a new address, and the old one is marked owl:deprecated with dcterms:isReplacedBy. Additions raise the minor version; a change of meaning raises the major. Each release is kept forever at its dated version IRI; the undated address serves the latest.

Only a statement made outright, by a verb whose form says it happened, is asserted; a command, promise, prediction or purpose is described and never stated.

How a text is published. LRMoo 1.1.1 (http://iflastandards.info/ns/lrm/lrmoo/) supplies the Work (F1_Work, a psalm), its Expressions (F2_Expression, the Hebrew text and each translation; R3_is_realised_in), the Manifestations that embody them (F3_Manifestation, an edition or a dataset; R4_embodies), and each Work's creation (F27_Work_Creation; R16_created). A collection is a Work of its own whose Expression incorporates its members' Expressions (R75_incorporates): each psalm stays its own Work. This vocabulary adds the order a tradition gives a collection (Arrangement), the numbered verses of one Expression (Segment), and the two relations a heading's reading uses inside a claim.

Reasoning policy

These terms describe the graph; they are not built for automated inference over it. Constraints are checked, closed-world, by the SHACL shapes at https://bibleontology.org/ns/shapes, not inferred. In particular nothing here states who wrote a text: authorship is a reading, published as a claim (https://bibleontology.org/ns/claim) with its verse, its certainty and whose reading it is, and the Work's creation event carries no agent, date or place the text does not give.

Versioning

A term's address never changes and is never reused. A term whose meaning changes gets a new address, and the old one is marked owl:deprecated with dcterms:isReplacedBy. Additions raise the minor version; a change of meaning raises the major. Each release is kept forever at its dated version IRI; the undated address serves the latest.

Classes

arrangement

Arrangement

The order a tradition or an edition gives the members of a collection: the Psalms in the order of the Masoretic Text, as the Berean prints them. An arrangement is attributed to whoever gives it and is not part of any member Work; another tradition's order is another arrangement of the same collection.

arrangement entry

ArrangementEntry

One Work's place in one arrangement: Psalm 73 at position 73 of the Masoretic order, the first of Book III. The place belongs to the arrangement, so the same psalm can hold a different place in another.

section

Section

A named division of an arrangement, as a run of its positions: Book III of the Psalter, Psalms 73 to 89. A division of the order, never a Work.

segment

Segment

One numbered verse of one Expression's text: Psalm 73:1 in the Hebrew, or in an English translation, each its own segment. Verse numbers are a reference system added to the text later, not a part the author made, so a segment is how the text is cited, not a component of the Work. The verse in the site's reader is the English segment's address.

aligned with

alignedWith

The two segments, in two Expressions of the same Work, render the same stretch of it: an English verse and the Hebrew verse it translates. A correspondence, not an identity; where the versification of two texts differs, the alignment says so verse by verse.

translation of

translationOf

This Expression is a translation made from that one: the English of a psalm from its Hebrew. Both are Expressions of the same Work; a translation is never a new Work. It is LRMoo's R76 (is derivative of) with the derivation's kind fixed as translation, so an LRMoo tool reads it without the property-of-a-property R76.1 needs. Where a translation departs from its source at a verse (following another witness), that is a claim about the verse, not a change to this relation.
